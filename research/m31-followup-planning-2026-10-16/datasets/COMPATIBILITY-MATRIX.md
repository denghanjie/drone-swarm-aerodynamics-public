# M31 public-data compatibility desk review

Checked 2026-10-15 UTC. **Three complementary public candidates were found; none is established as satisfying the joint source-wake evidence contract.** Two are measurement-archive candidates whose internal rawness and schema remain unverified; NanoBench supplies processed actuator telemetry. This is a bounded provider-metadata review, not model validation or a finding that suitable data do not exist.

The reference model assumes **27 g mass, 23 mm rotor radius and arm coordinates ±28 mm**. These are not verified specifications of tested hardware. Required evidence combines independently measured total thrust and uncertainty, spatial mean air velocity and sampling locations, per-motor commands/RPM/supply, settled all-off background, symmetric and lower-symmetric settings, and an unequal setting matched in independently measured total thrust. Registration, supports, repeats, timing and missingness must also be traceable.

## Manifest verification and caveats

The official Zenodo API manifests list:

- MODERA: `experimental_data.zip` **2,512,858,162 bytes**; `digital_model.zip` **21,886,719 bytes**; `digital_model_sample_hover.sim` **7,163,689,466 bytes**.
- Air ducts: `aero_forces_in_air_ducts.tgz` **5,051,965,098 bytes**.

Exact download URLs and provider MD5 values are in `SOURCES-AND-MANIFESTS.json`. These are advertised public downloads, not payload-integrity checks. Archives were not downloaded or expanded; internal file manifests, raw versus processed records and CSV schemas have not been independently inspected.

At the NanoBench pin, `datasets/dataset/` contains **107 CSVs and 107 paired YAMLs**, with **87 distinct CSV Git blob IDs**. The README and current v2 paper advertise 172 recordings. The complete recursive tree is untruncated and includes additional benchmark data and result tables elsewhere; the 107 count is strictly directory-scoped. Eighteen groups share blob IDs, accounting for 20 CSV entries beyond distinct content. This establishes repeated file content at this pin; it does **not** establish trial independence, dependence, cause, or misconduct. Reconcile the advertised dataset accounting before selecting repetitions or splits. No CSV payloads were fetched. See `SOURCE-SECTIONS.md` for exact evidence locations and `PUBLICATION-NOTE.md` for projection details.

Paper and data permissions were checked separately: NanoBench's preprint is **CC BY 4.0**, while its dataset declaration is **BSD 3-Clause**; the air-duct article and dataset each declare **CC BY 4.0**. No MODERA paper license was established. MODERA also publishes a research/education use notice that should accompany any later acquisition review.

## Recommendation and search scope

Prioritize a later, separately scoped MODERA force-file/schema inspection, then NanoBench accounting and instrumentation reconciliation. Keep air-duct data as a boundary-effect reference. Do not substitute any of them for paired thrust-plus-flow validation of the assumed nanoquad.

Search covered Zenodo, GitHub, arXiv, publisher/provider pages, and discovery queries for Crazyflie thrust/PWM, multirotor wake/PIV and explicitly licensed raw measurements. Simulation-only records were excluded; Bitcraze's thrust page and ISAE's rotor-data lead were not promoted without verified downloadable-file/license evidence. Previously discussed Wolf, DLR and vortex-merging leads were not repeated. No fitting, source execution, author contact, experiments or large downloads were performed.
