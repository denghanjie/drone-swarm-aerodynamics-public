# Three.js milestone display verification

Installed Playwright/Chrome tested the local page after all scientific allocations and the terminal qualification were closed. The browser plugin was unavailable; the installed browser was the documented fallback. No scientific module, generator, solver or timing experiment ran in this QA.

The single saved attempt passed all 50 check groups with no console errors, warnings or failed requests. It selected all three new cases, scrubbed/played/paused recorded frames, checked final-only load labeling, scaled drone glyphs, orbited/reset the camera and followed every evidence link. At all 21 frames for each case, every rendered body translation matched the Float32 representation of the saved Float64 pose coordinate: 693,000 coordinate comparisons. This establishes display translation fidelity, not an independent flight/physics result. Source quaternion rotations retain the existing renderer and were source-inspected rather than independently numerically audited here.

Separate display fault injections confirmed failure with hidden claims for a mismatched evidence hash, an inconsistent terminal closure and a timing-table mismatch even when the altered display record was rehashed. These injections altered routed browser responses only; saved scientific artifacts were unchanged.

Desktop 1440×1000 and mobile 390×844 views had no horizontal overflow. The parent visually inspected the full desktop page, selected 5,000-drone scene and mobile scene: legible controls/limitations, visible three-dimensional volume, no clipped swarm or overlapping controls. The two-second starting lattice remains apparent and is explicitly not presented as mature emergent flocking. Screenshots, exact QA source and report are preserved in attempt-01/.

Browser checks and screenshots do not validate physical laws or qualify the timing result. The page requires both actual supervisor closures, independent audit and the terminal seal, and binds selected pose/load bytes by SHA256.
