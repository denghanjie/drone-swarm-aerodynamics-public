What this tells us

A single center-speed check could miss differences in how far the airflow spreads. A future approximation needs to preserve the relevant profile, not just its peak.

The curves come from the same paper that informed our existing wake model. This is a reproducible check of exposed published evidence, with no new fit.

What remains open

These are plotted medians from hover experiments, not the original measurement samples. Their statistical uncertainty is unavailable here. We cannot declare the difference physically significant or the model sufficiently accurate.

This does not validate changing motor commands, shutdown, multiple interacting wakes, tiny drones, or 5,000-drone feasibility.
