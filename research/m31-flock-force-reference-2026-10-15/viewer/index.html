WHAT IS BEING CHECKED

Arithmetic across the entire flock.

The original implementation uses a spatial grid and Newton's method. The independent version enumerates sources directly and uses bisection for rotor response. Every rotor and vehicle must satisfy the precision screens fixed before the run.

Recorded force differences are numerical consistency checks. An accepted comparison would support using these equations as a conditional reference for later approximation tests.

WHAT REMAINS UNPROVEN

The model still needs measurements.

Source strength is fixed at isolated-hover level, overlapping wakes add under a model assumption, and the receiver law holds ideal induced power constant. Missing motor states are explicitly prescribed. Walls, ground effects and unsteady airflow are absent.

Numerical agreement does not repair the earlier trajectory failures or prove correct physics. The skipped geometry remains visible in the preceding domain viewer.
