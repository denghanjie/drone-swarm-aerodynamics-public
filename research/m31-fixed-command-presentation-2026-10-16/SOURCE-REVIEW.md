## Why this uncertainty now

The prior PHYSICAL-DATA-QUESTION already selected a single CW propulsion unit,
source-defined axial incidence `ip=0`, PWM 1475, one matched static baseline and
the two settings 0.25/0.5 psf. Selection predates new target acquisition. The
methods admission establishes that this kind of directly measured fixed-command
experiment exists, while the actual matched observation presentation is still
unadmitted. Resolving that yes/no access question is the immediate dependency
for a physical receiver-transfer comparison.

The source is Simmons and Hatke, *Investigation of High Incidence Angle Propeller
Aerodynamics for Subscale eVTOL Aircraft*, NASA/TM-20210014010 (May 2021).
Previously verified author-uploaded methods §§3.1–3.5 and Tables 1–3 establish a
16-inch three-bladed propulsion unit, distinct CW/CCW variants, direct balance
loads, recorded PWM/RPM, fixed PWM/pressure settings within incidence sweeps, and
static cases at corresponding commands. Warmup/drift and model-informed
retesting/replacement are part of the provenance. Table 2's balance accuracy is
not a complete uncertainty budget.

The related *System Identification for Propellers at High Incidence Angles*
article, DOI 10.2514/1.C036329, is the same experimental campaign. Its fitted
predictions and validation summaries are neither replacement direct observations
nor independent replication. The earlier GWS transfer is prescribed-RPM and
already exposed; it does not answer this new command-bridge question. The saved
source-flow alternatives still lack a comparably small admitted load/flow/control
triplet. This recommendation does not abandon the separate source-field problem.

## Admission criteria: all essential identity fields must pass

This gate accepts a *presentation for a later extraction protocol*, not the
receiver model. It must resolve all of the following from explicit source
evidence rather than from the visual shape or numerical plausibility of a curve.

1. **Version and location.** Record title/authors/report identity, displayed
   version/upload date, exact page or section, table/figure/panel identifiers,
   caption and legend. Save the accessible representation or a precise tool-
   output locator privately; hash saved bytes. Retain any mismatch with the
   previously verified NASA identity instead of silently assuming equivalence.
2. **Actual observations.** The presentation explicitly contains measured or
   source-processed direct balance loads, distinguished from fitted/model curves.
   Published means/medians are admissible summaries if their semantics are
   stated; they are not raw repeats. Authors' interpolation or fitted curves do
   not supply missing observations. If observation/fit identity is ambiguous,
   admission fails.
3. **Same physical/control condition.** Associate the static and both nonzero
   settings with the same CW propeller/motor/drive, PWM 1475, axial orientation,
   source-defined force sign and comparable supply/thermal/zero procedures.
   A shared command number alone is insufficient. A publication-defined common
   baseline or matching rule is required; unexplained competing baselines mean
   failure. Individual run IDs may be absent if a clearly identified published
   matched summary is supplied, but that absence must remain explicit.
4. **Observable and units.** Identify dimensional axial load and the stated
   tare/background treatment. If only installed-system balance force is
   available, define that as the target and declare the extra force-to-thrust
   transfer assumption; do not rename it pure propeller thrust. If force meaning
   or coordinate sign cannot be identified, fail. If only coefficients appear,
   independent dimensionalization inputs must be available within this view;
   otherwise fail admission for the proposed newton-valued comparison.
5. **Exactly supported conditions.** Static and both selected axial samples are
   explicitly present. For a graphic, identify the measured markers/curve and
   the supported `ip=0` locations, axes, scales, clipping and readable separation.
   Do not interpolate a missing axial sample, bridge overlap, extrapolate or use
   a fitted line. An ambiguous or off-scale selected point fails the triplet.
6. **Prospective extraction can be written.** From the presentation, specify exact
   three observation/summary identities, axis registration, marker/stroke bounds,
   clipping and a finite transcription procedure. Fix graphical ambiguity rules
   before extracting targets. Source coordinate intervals remain intervals when
   a stroke is thick. If the representation cannot support a reproducible
   extraction specification, fail now.

Nominal pressure and nominal diameter are not automatic failures for this
restricted descriptive transfer: preserve their status as nominal. Do not call
settings per-point measurements, infer density or pressure from thrust, or fill
missing metadata with standard-atmosphere assumptions. Remaining installation
loads, drift, actual pressure, geometry precision and total uncertainty must be
declared as conditional inputs/limitations rather than silently repaired.

Record reuse terms separately, including an explicit `unresolved` state where
needed. Public readability does not grant raw-data or full-figure redistribution
rights. Private presentation admission can remain distinct from publication;
unknown reuse terms do not authorize copying the source into the public repo.
If the planned subsequent artifact requires redistribution that the available
terms do not support, its publication gate remains unresolved.

## Failure and completion rules

Possible outcomes are `ADMITTED_PRESENTATION` or `NOT_ADMITTED_WITHIN_SCOPE`,
with field-level reasons and separate uncertainty/reuse flags. Partial success
does not silently become a two-point or one-pressure experiment. Fail on blocked
access, exhausted calls/time/panels, missing static association, wrong command or
variant, unresolvable force convention, fit-only values, missing selected support,
or inseparable/clipped targets. Keep all evidence of the failed attempt.

The failure statement is “not recoverable within this access scope,” not “no
dataset exists” or “the receiver law is false.” End this attempt without alternate
sources, command changes, new pressures, baseline shopping or further access.
Any future direction would need an independent decision informed by the exact
gap, rather than a rolling retry.

Successful admission stops after saving the exact extraction specification. It
does not continue directly into transcription or scoring. The current records
do not identify eligible page/panel IDs, so inventing them now would be a false
preregistration. This two-stage boundary is necessary: first identify recoverable
observations, then freeze extraction and analysis before calculating outcomes.
Visual inspection may incidentally expose numbers; log those exposures candidly
and do not later call the source blind or wholly unseen.

## What a later comparison would test

Keep the existing explicit bridge `f0 = T0(PWM)` at the selected command, one
baseline shared by both targets, and the frozen receiver law
`x^3 + alpha*x - 1 = 0`, `T = T0*x`. Under its stated nonnegative axial-flow
definitions, `alpha = sqrt(4*A*q/T0)` avoids an invented density. It does not
eliminate pressure/area/baseline uncertainty or prove that a real PWM command
holds model-implied ideal disk power fixed. Recorded RPM, torque or electrical
variables could be diagnostics only if separately admitted; no such acquisition
is added to this minimal checkpoint.

A later frozen analysis should retain the unchanged-static-thrust comparator,
both nonzero conditions, dimensional and baseline-normalized discrepancies,
shared-baseline dependence, all domain/sign failures and a finite calculation
budget. It must not infer a fresh f0 from each target, fit a gain, adjust pressure,
discard a negative observation or select a baseline to improve agreement. A
nonpositive static value or inadmissible target sign discovered at transcription
would be retained as a domain failure, not trigger substitution.

Without total experimental uncertainty and an application-based accuracy budget,
the outcome is descriptive consistency or discrepancy conditional on the
published inputs. Table 2 calibration accuracy and two independent digitizations
cannot create physical confidence intervals or independent experimental repeats.
A discrepancy challenges the receiver equation **plus the command-to-static-
thrust bridge and declared hardware/load transfer assumptions together**. It
cannot uniquely blame the cubic, motor response, installation force or baseline.
Agreement supports only the admitted finite comparison.

This is 16-inch isolated-rotor steady uniform axial inflow, not qualification of
the 46 mm modeled rotor, nonuniform neighboring wakes, moments, source-amplitude
scaling, unequal commands, aggregation, transients, coupled flight or 5000-drone
browser feasibility. Prior source and computation failures remain intact.

Novelty remains open. Recovering an accessible observation triplet and applying a
known ideal-disk closure is useful evidence work, not by itself a novel law or an
accuracy–cost result. A future contribution would need its own verified prior-art
comparison and a supported new claim; no new literature search is allocated here.
