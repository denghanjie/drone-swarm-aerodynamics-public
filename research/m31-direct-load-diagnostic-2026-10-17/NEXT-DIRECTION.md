# Parent decision: qualify an ordinary native baseline next

The fixed weakest-tenth omission route is closed. Adopt the prerequisite recommended in review/NEXT-DECISION-NOT-ALLOCATED.md: establish a complete ordinary binary64 full-contribution calculation, then measure where its time goes. Do not add grouped approximation before a fair native baseline exists.

This is an explicit change in experimental interface, not a relaxation of the accuracy threshold. The next proposed interface produces current rotor, collective, world-force and moment values and declared domain/status information. Independent exact-model checking is performed offline on registered qualification cases. It does not return or claim Q-current's per-query certified load enclosures/admission proof. The earlier Q-current results and failures remain valid under their original contract. Strict1e-8 remains the proposed primary numerical accuracy criterion;1e-4/1e-2 remain labelled diagnostics. No physical tolerance is adopted.

Why this comes first: the current full control used precomputed oracle coefficients, so it did not establish ordinary native coefficient construction cost or accuracy. The preceding seconds-scale exact pipeline includes arithmetic/proof obligations that differ from this proposed interface. Those numbers cannot be used as a matched speedup denominator. Replacing exact arithmetic with ordinary floating point is not algorithmic novelty; it is necessary baseline qualification before making a contribution claim.

The next source-only preparation will identify existing reusable native code, audit semantic differences, define status and guard behavior, declare current-input-to-consumed-output cost, fix browser/runtime identity and practical cadence/budget assumptions, and freeze fresh small-state tests plus retained boundary/failure controls. No source implementation, new seed, numerical case, benchmark, or execution allocation is approved by this direction note. The full protocol must separately justify its limits and evidence gates before launch.

Only after accuracy and cost are measured should the dominant cost justify contribution-preserving grouping or temporal reuse. Both remain hypotheses. No free precomputed coefficients, positive membership, reference roots or stale state may enter a timed native query. The original target remains efficient local3D Boids with meaningful flight dynamics and mutual aerodynamics at5000drones in a browser; native-baseline qualification alone will not establish that outcome or physical validity.
