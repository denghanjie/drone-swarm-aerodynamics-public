# Parent decision: admit the reading, reserve a small falsification test

The two original papers were read within the recorded access scope. Their method ideas are relevant antecedents. Neither supplies a certified cheap error indicator or an applicable complete integration scheme for the current drone implementation. Publisher access and correction/version clearance remain limited. We therefore admit scoped method evidence, not physical validation, a new algorithm or an operational refresh rule.

The history inventory materially limits the next claim: prediction and adaptive refresh already exist in this research. Main M28 also tested feedback airflow cadence. The later coupled-root variable, evolving nominal motors and full current wrench are a distinct diagnostic setting, not a new extrapolation algorithm. Preserve the earlier cold-start/source-pitch failure and all closed allocations.

Adopt the proposed causal secant-root diagnostic as the next implementation question, subject to a separate prospective protocol/source freeze. It asks a narrow prerequisite: is a straight-line estimate of the newly coupled root vector accurate at the already-saved refresh endpoints? It may fail. This inexpensive saved-data check is useful only as a premise screen before committing to a more complex monitor or new feedback paths. There is no prediction score, source freeze or executable allocation yet.

Required refinements before that future freeze:

1. The prediction function receives only the previous one/two fresh root vectors and their times plus the current target time. It cannot see the target fresh root, target error or future events. Current nominal motor state/orientation are permitted only in the stated same-state load assembly. Original input bytes are already exposed and may be parsed in memory; this is causal computation on known data, not a blind or held-out forecast. Audit the function interface and reconstruction, not a claim of hidden future data.
2. All200positive events must be represented, including the first cold-start hold. Invalid finite roots fail the candidate; retain their extrapolated load arithmetic as explicitly invalid-domain diagnostics if finite. Nonfinite or uncomputable errors use null plus a reason and coverage counts. Do not turn a valid-only maximum into the primary result. Any invalid/unscored event fails qualification, even if other maxima are small. No clipping, fallback to the oracle, fitted slope, selective reset or post-result tolerance change.
3. These states come from the rejected held-ratio path. They are a fixed exposed diagnostic sequence, not states the predictor would produce in flight. An endpoint pass would not establish intermediate accuracy, coupled-model domain validity between events, trajectory improvement, a useful error trigger, speedup or fresh generalization.
4. The suggested16MiB cap means aggregate NEW artifacts, including logs/review/supervisor records. The immutable published input remains a referenced, hash-pinned dependency and is not copied into the new output allocation. Memory/time accounting must include loading it. The future supervisor/retention contract must specify reserves, hard-kill limits and consumed invocations before execution.
5. Preserve the three same-state thresholds and held comparator, root-domain predicate, exact event sides and zero-based argmax IDs; independently verify all new algebra and gate logic. Any claim of improvement must show the complete paired error table, not just a pooled average. Failure closes this particular predictor; it does not authorize tuning another one under its budget.
6. A new substantial numerical result needs a new Three.js view tied to its saved data. If it is a same-state diagnostic, use the exact original poses and label them as such; do not imply that forecasted loads generated a new flight trajectory.

This decision does not solve the more important open requirements: physically meaningful model/observable validation, justified application tolerances, a cheap current-domain/error monitor, fresh conditions and total5000-drone browser cost. Those remain independent research gates. No claim is made that another extrapolator will satisfy them. Separate physical-data admission remains an alternative priority; no extra physical source is acquired or admitted in this two-paper scope.
