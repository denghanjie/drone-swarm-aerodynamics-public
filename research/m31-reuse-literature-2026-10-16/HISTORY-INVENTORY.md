## Derivative correction and event triggering: exact limits of the inventory

The inspected protocols establish a causal two-point slope predictor and a prospective analytical error-budget scheduler. The latter selects the next horizon from an enclosure; it is not evidence of an online trigger based on measured unknown current error. The held-value/fresh-value errors saved in the current ratio-reuse study are diagnostic observations, not an implemented adaptive trigger.

Within these ten files, no analytic time derivative/Jacobian correction of the current joint-source solution, implicit derivative propagation through source amplitudes, or residual-triggered joint-root predictor in integrated feedback flight is documented as implemented and tested. This is a bounded finding, not proof of repository-wide absence. Keyword/filename discovery found no local `campaign-temporal-sampling` or `campaign-joint-stage-reference` directory in this checkout. The cadence protocol links those newer-main packages and reports both temporal-reference screens failed and the joint-stage reference remained incomplete; their linked files were not reopened or independently re-read here.

The predictor-stress package already attributes its broad two-sample idea to Grubmüller–Tavan DC-1b and lists Tuckerman et al. (1992) among prior multirate sources. Those are historical attribution records, not newly verified original-source claims from this task. This inventory performs no additional paper admission and consumes no external retrieval budget.

## Finite planning recommendation

Keep this desk checkpoint source-only. A possible separately frozen question is whether a specified causal predictor of joint solved rotor ratios or a specified correction of the full wrench can reduce pre-refresh error while motor states, attitude and controller feedback evolve. Freeze the predicted variable, source/motor dependence, startup and reset behavior, support/domain handling, refresh ownership and exact event side before evaluating anything. Compare against the already rejected hold10 ms and the accepted every-stage reference; retain paired full-wrench trajectory metrics and same-state pre-refresh load metrics, along with the extra predictor/indicator cost. An error indicator would need a demonstrated relation to these errors; neither a smaller residual nor fewer joint calls alone establishes accuracy or speed.

The prior moment normalizations differ: the prescribed-input studies use mg times opposite-motor distance D, while current ratio reuse uses mg times motor-arm radius R. Their percentages and acceptance targets must not be copied across without conversion and explicit justification. No new method, fresh seed, predictor score, cadence choice or expanded execution is authorized by this inventory.
