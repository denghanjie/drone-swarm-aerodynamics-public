# Independent saved-root secant reviewer

This source is for the separately frozen `m31-root-secant-2026-10-16` endpoint diagnostic. Its CLI is `python source/review.py PACKAGE`; it exclusively creates `results/independent-review.json`. Preparation was static source/schema inspection and AST parsing only. No candidate, historical scientific module, predictor score or synthetic control was executed before publication/release. The reviewer imports NumPy and standard-library modules only; it never imports a candidate, geometry builder, root solver, controller or integrator.

## Inherited evidence and admission

The exact 15,814,425-byte input and four provenance files are embedded as immutable pins. The reviewer checks the prior independent audit's success, completed numerical allocation, successful external review closure, source-commit/manifest agreement, raw/audit/closure membership in the result manifest, and publication receipt identity/counts. Prior approximation failure is retained; only geometry/root implementation correctness is inherited. The new candidate's successful external closure, protected inputs, source-manifest pins and candidate artifacts are checked independently before reading its new conclusions.

Input must be the completed finest 16-drone reuse record with exactly 201 events from 0 to 2 s, including 200 propagation refreshes and one terminal audit. All event IDs, times, roles and predecessor indices are checked. States, commands and roots must have finite 16×17, 16×4 and 16×4 shapes, positive nominal rotor loads, unit quaternions and admitted fresh metadata within the inherited residual limits. Cache roots must equal witness roots directly. Fresh/RIGHT and old-root LEFT forces and both correction fields are reconstructed at the current state. Cached metadata cannot claim current geometry or closure. No geometry, coefficient or root equation is reevaluated.

## Independent algebra and causality

The independent predictor accepts only prior times/roots and a target time. Target 1 uses the cold-start hold. Targets 2–200 use the prescribed time-scaled secant of the two preceding fresh roots. Each prediction and predecessor trace is checked before the independent loop accesses the current oracle. Fresh saved roots enter history after every fixed target, including an invalid prediction. There is no clipping, smoothing, warm history, fallback, reset, fitted parameter or selective target omission.

The flushed candidate trace and frozen source ordering document prediction before scoring. Loading the complete immutable JSON is allowed; a trace does not independently prove inaccessible memory. The decisive check is that all saved predictions equal the prior-only formula at the declared numerical tolerance.

NumPy vector algebra independently computes current nominal thrust times the selected roots. Quaternion cross products rotate the collective correction into world coordinates; a corner-coordinate matrix produces roll/pitch moments. This differs from the candidate's scalar axial-rotation expression and per-rotor summation. Errors are maximum per-rotor absolute difference divided by mg/4, force-vector norm divided by mg, and body-moment norm divided by mgR, where R = sqrt(2)×0.028 m. All per-drone values are checked with absolute/relative tolerances 1e-11/1e-10. Nominal yaw is identical between methods and the added aerodynamic yaw moment is zero.

After independently validating every retained scalar error array, candidate extrema and first-tie locations are reconstructed from those arrays. Candidate primary/secondary tables and exact better/equal/worse counts are reduced from the validated retained values, preserving their binary64 identity when independent arithmetic changes tiny roundoff amounts. Separately, the reviewer aggregates its own reconstructed numeric error arrays and applies the strict 0.001 and no-worse-than-hold gates without any comparison tolerance. Every primary candidate gate and scientific pass flag must agree with this independent qualification. The output includes independent event maxima and `independentPrimaryStrict`; independently tied argmax locations are not required to match. Thus agreement tolerance cannot conceal a threshold or comparator flip.

## Invalidity, coverage and qualification

Every invalid root retains its target/drone/rotor identity, finite value or null, and named nonfinite/domain reason. Finite out-of-domain roots retain their computed load/error diagnostics. Nonfinite or uncomputable predictions are unscored; no zero/error fallback is inserted. Complete-set maxima become null if any target is unscored, while finite diagnostic maxima remain separate.

All 200 positive targets, including cold start and terminal audit, are primary. The 199 secant-only targets are explicitly secondary and have no qualification flag. Primary scientific pass requires admitted input, complete scoring, no invalid roots, all three maxima at most 0.001, and each no worse than the held-root comparator maximum. Exact tie outcomes are retained. Audit integrity and scientific qualification are separate: an intact rejected predictor can receive a passing independent audit. No parameter or threshold is changed after a miss.

The sole audit also checks the candidate's five frozen synthetic fixtures against literal expectations: startup, constant history, nonuniform-time linear history, overshoot and JSON-safe invalid serialization. Independent controls cover causal formulas, root-domain boundaries, named NaN/infinity serialization, identity-root zero correction and all four rotor force/moment signs. These controls run only inside the single authorized audit; no separate control trial is performed.

## Bounds and remaining limits

Each external invocation has a 60 s wall and 512 MiB sampled RSS cap; the checker cooperates at 55 s. Candidate results including closure are capped at 6 MiB, leaving capacity for review within the overall 10 MiB results cap. New package files share 16 MiB; logs share 1 MiB. The checker reserves 128 KiB for its external closure when sizing output. It creates output exclusively, retains existing artifacts and does not truncate them to turn a cap miss into a pass. A failed/externally terminated audit can leave partial or empty output; its sole allocation stays consumed. Error messages reveal classes/labels without private paths or tracebacks.

The 201-event input population is exposed development evidence, not an independent validation set. A pass would concern only those endpoints on the old reuse trajectory. It would not establish intermediate-time validity, a predictor-driven feedback trajectory, an adaptive trigger, saved joint solves, measured speed, physical accuracy, stability or novelty. Missing/failed checks remain incomplete and require a separately frozen repair allocation rather than a rerun.
