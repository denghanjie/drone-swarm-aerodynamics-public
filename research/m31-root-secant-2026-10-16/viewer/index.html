What this comparison measures

The predicted quantity is the jointly solved rotor-load ratio. Both estimates use the same current nominal motor states, orientation and body lever arms. Their errors are measured against the saved fresh solution on that state.

This is development on exposed data. Agreement at refresh endpoints does not establish validity between them, or show what a predictor-driven trajectory would do.

What remains open

No new flight was integrated and no query was avoided by this diagnostic. Runtime records describe the execution allowance, not a speed benchmark. Physical validity, operational accuracy, generalization and 5,000-drone performance remain unresolved.

The 0.001 thresholds are provisional numerical screens. Moment uses mgR with R = √2 × 0.028 m; it is separate from collective force and per-rotor load.
