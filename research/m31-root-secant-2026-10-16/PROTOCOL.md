## Question and exact population

Can the fixed causal secant predictor meet the three previously declared same-state load-error screens on the exposed finest 10 ms reuse path? Input is the published, hash-pinned 15,814,425-byte `boids16-reuse10ms-0.00125.json`: 16 drones, seed260201, 2 s, integration dt0.00125 s. This is development on exposed data, not held-out evidence or a new flight. There are 201 ordered refresh events t=0,...,2 s and exactly 200 positive targets. Include the terminal audit and mandatory cold start. No new geometry, wake, root solve, trajectory integration, fit, cadence or external source acquisition is allocated.

For k=1, predicted x is the saved fresh x[0]. For k>=2, predicted x = x[k-1] + ((t[k]-t[k-1])/(t[k-1]-t[k-2]))*(x[k-1]-x[k-2]). The predictor receives only preceding fresh roots/times and target time; no state, commands, current/future root or errors. Compute and append the prediction and predecessor IDs to a flushed trace before reading the current oracle for scoring. Loading the immutable JSON in memory is allowed; this audit enforces causal computation rather than hidden data. After scoring, saved fresh x[k] enters history even when prediction is invalid, according to the unchanged reference refresh schedule. No clipping, smoothing, fitted slope, selective restart, warm history or oracle fallback.

Comparator: previous fresh root x[k-1] (hold10ms). Oracle: current saved fresh root x[k]. For each, actual rotor thrust is the same current nominal motor thrust times the selected root. Compute aerodynamic corrections with current quaternion: world force R(q)[0,0,sum(T-f0)] and body torque [sum(y_r*(T_r-f0_r)), -sum(x_r*(T_r-f0_r)), 0], with motor corners (+a,+a),(-a,+a),(-a,-a),(+a,-a), a=.028 m. Nominal yaw is identical, so its difference is zero; this closure adds no aerodynamic yaw torque. Do not omit the roll/pitch term or use old mgD normalization.

For candidate and hold, every event retains per-drone rotor absolute errors divided by mg/4, force-vector norm errors divided by mg and body-moment norm errors divided by mgR, R=sqrt(2)*a. Preserve all predicted/held/oracle roots, rotor loads, force/torque corrections, per-event/per-drone errors and zero-based extrema locations. Primary maxima cover all 200 positive targets; also report all 200 paired event maxima and the 199 secant-only targets as explicitly secondary. Cold start cannot be excluded to rescue a pass.
