# Planning after the rejected fixed secant

No new execution allocation is created here. Do not reopen this experiment, tune its cadence/slope, clip its failures, introduce warm history or reclassify the199 later targets as a passing result. Preserve all source, raw data, audit and failure records.

The current evidence makes a startup-only explanation incomplete: later force/moment and rotor errors still exceed the screen. A useful next question must therefore address the evolving coupled load relation, current geometry/support changes or the accuracy–cost tradeoff beyond this particular two-point forecast. A different predictor, residual/error indicator or startup policy is a new method choice requiring its own causal definition, source review, frozen target/limits and bounded test; it cannot inherit a success claim from the smaller maxima here.

Before allocating another flight experiment, select a distinct question using the retained history and verified literature. Possible priorities are a mathematically justified monitor for the actual body-load error, a different explicitly derived treatment of the coupled solution, or physical-data compatibility/validation. These are options rather than adopted implementations. A cheap monitor must be related to load error and include its own cost; a small residual, fewer queries or a visually plausible flock is insufficient.

The broader goal stays active and incomplete. This checkpoint is a useful falsification of a simple premise, not a publishable breakthrough, proof that forecasting is impossible, or permission to scale an unqualified approximation.
