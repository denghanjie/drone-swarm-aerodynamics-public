## Arithmetic and partition

Interpret each logged decimal exactly; these strings record rounded binary64 timings, not exact underlying clock differences. Producer uses Decimal precision80 with the Inexact trap enabled for sums/differences; an independent auditor uses rational arithmetic. Canonical times are exact finite decimal strings (no redundant trailing fractional zeroes; zero is "0"). No extra measurement precision is claimed. Ratios are represented by numeratorMs and denominatorMs; display rounding cannot affect decisions.

For success, wrapper phases input/buildAndAdmission/schedule/solve/residual/loads/consumption/finalization partition inner total. Kernel input/index/geometry/admission are nested inside buildAndAdmission. Replace that broad build bucket with kernelInput, kernelIndex, geometry, kernelAdmission and buildUnassigned (broad build minus kernel sum). Preserve other wrapper phases. Add outerBoundary = outerMs − inner total, and timerResidual = inner total − sum(wrapper phases). These disjoint signed leaves sum exactly to outerMs. Require absolute timerResidual ≤0.000001 ms and outerBoundary/buildUnassigned ≥−0.000001 ms; retain signed values, never clamp raw evidence. Non-residual recorded phase times must be finite/nonnegative.

For the inherited refused case, retain input and rename finalization to unfinishedPhaseAndFinalization. It includes the unclosed wrapper build interval and catch/output assembly; the kernel may already have returned. Kernel timing is unavailable, never zero-filled or attributed to ordinary finalization. Keep outerBoundary and timerResidual. Reject any unexpected phase-key/status pattern.

KernelIndex includes screening-predicate and certificate work, not spatial-index construction. KernelAdmission includes graph/reason checks and coefficient scaling/ordinaryLmax work. Geometry means interaction construction, including cache checks/builds, center tests, rotor terms and row assembly. It cannot isolate center-norm savings or cache overhead. BuildUnassigned includes wrapper evidence/validation and kernel post-t4 return/assembly work. OuterBoundary includes external copy, call/return and timing-object work; it is not pure copying.

## Fixed outputs and descriptive decision

Save every transformed row. Aggregate separately by warmup/measured × method × status, by repetition × method × status, and by repetition × window × method × status. Retain counts, inner/outer totals, disjoint leaves and the recorded evidence intervals. The primary population is measured F native-success outerMs only; warmup, refusals and resets are excluded from this denominator and reported separately.

Require strictly positive primary and per-repetition measured successful F outer totals. Rank primary leaves by decreasing exact sum with alphabetical ties, excluding only timerResidual. A stable-majority label requires the leading leaf to account for at least one half of pooled primary outerMs AND to be largest in at least six of eight measured F repetitions using the same ranking rule. Otherwise label distributed-or-unstable. This selects a descriptive inspection priority, not an acceleration claim or a hypothesis test. It does not alter the earlier 10% qualification gate. Retain all per-repetition winners, exact numerator/denominator and both branch conditions.

Reconcile each saved group by method: transformed successful/refused outer sums, their counts, and total including separate logged resetMs, against saved admitted/refused/sumQuery/total fields. Save signed deltas; absolute tolerance0.000001 ms. F reset is the matched branch/timer interval; C includes actual reset. Never allocate reset to a success/refusal category.

encodePersistMs is a partial evidence interval outside outerMs; comparisonMs is nested inside it and covers the common comparison subset. It excludes query-log append, later assertions and other producer work. Neither interval is added to the partition or interpreted as complete process wall time. Asymmetric evidence work can influence later caches/GC. No causal attribution, uninstrumented throughput, timing population inference, physical accuracy, safety, novelty or scaling result follows.
