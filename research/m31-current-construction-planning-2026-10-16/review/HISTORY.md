## Decision

**Conditionally retain one implementation-specific question:** can a conservatively certified componentwise far screen, inserted before the current lean all-pairs center `Math.hypot`, reduce complete-query cost against the unchanged current lean comparator while preserving its numerical/output contract?

The visible intervention is distinct from the closed E/L source-axis radial-screen experiment and the storage intervention. It is ordinary broad-phase screening and lazy evaluation, not a new algorithm. This review finds a separable source mechanism, not evidence of a speedup or a complete arithmetic justification. A separate arithmetic challenge must establish the omission and fallback conditions before implementation/test allocation. If that proof or a matched comparator cannot be fixed, reject the experiment; do not replace it with a tuned collection of screens.

The decisive source fact is that the current lean loop computes a center norm for every visited nonself pair before the neighborhood exclusion. The proposed test can target that calculation without a grid, positive omission, altered coefficient assembly or stale geometry. This makes a narrow experiment defensible even though prior screening results are adverse. It does not establish that the modification will resolve the larger performance goal.

## Actual duplication and source-order evidence

**Current lean all-pairs path.** In the typed-assembly kernel, `mode==='lean'` selects ordinary rows/sets and scalar geometry within `buildAssembly`. The source visits receivers, then ascending source IDs, then rotors. Lines 101–107 compute source-minus-receiver center components and immediately call `Math.hypot(dx,dy,dz)`, apply invalid-center guards, then skip at `center>=c.neighbor`. There is no componentwise neighborhood rejection ahead of this norm in the inspected current lean source. The componentwise comparison that does exist, at line 106, is an invalid-geometry guard after the norm; it is not the proposed far-support screen.

**Closed E/L source.** `control.cjs` first obtains `ids=grid.neighbors(...)` at line 60. It then calls its source-axis envelope screen for those IDs. E computes a radial norm of the center displacement after subtracting its source-axis projection; L delays that same radial norm until after downstream interval exits. Both retain explicit near-domain fallback before radial/projection exclusion. This is a different norm and a later stage than the proposed raw center-distance screen in all-pairs lean. The new proposal is not a rerun of E/Z package removal or L/E downstream-before-radial scheduling.

**DOT exact support/lean/reuse.** The directly inspected historical `exact-aero.cjs` constructs a grid and obtains neighbor IDs before support work. `zeroSupport` forms the projected center radial norm eagerly. Support/reuse flag exact-zero pairs separately depending on whether a near-domain guard is still required; rotor-major evaluation preserves first-error/source-sum behavior. Lean omits this support stage; reuse refreshes its scratch for current geometry. The historical implementation has coordinate/quaternion admission and arithmetic/traversal rules that differ from current coupled construction. The broad ideas of zero screening, lean scalar arithmetic, delayed evaluation and refreshed scratch are therefore already present; none can be claimed as new.

The imported historical `Grid` implementation is outside the eight-file inspection. This review cannot assert that no componentwise center screen appears inside any historical neighbor search. The supported distinction is the explicit missing pre-center screen in the current ordered all-pairs lean loop and the different intervention measured by E/L. Even an analogous historical grid shortcut would remain established technique; the potential new evidence would be its effect in this particular current complete-query path.

**Closed outcomes and other directions.** The inspected post-cost design records that E/Z and L/E missed their joint usefulness gates and that fewer center radial norms did not establish a corresponding elapsed saving. The audit/comparison records mixed incremental DOT gains and support/reuse not consistently beating lean. These are adverse published narrative findings, not reprocessed measurements. The post-cost decision rejects resumed grid tuning, positive-omission budgets, solver microtuning and unsupported stale-matrix reuse. The current proposal must stay separate from those closed lines. It still reconstructs current geometry and coefficients and introduces no temporal reuse or positive omission.

## What work could actually be removed

For an early-rejected pair, the unchanged lean path would already reject that pair at its center-neighborhood test and perform no rotor profile, coefficient insertion or dependency construction. The marginal opportunity is thus its **center `Math.hypot` and subsequent original center-check work**, subject to whatever checks the safe screen must retain. It is not newly avoided rotor work or a reduction of the all-pairs enumeration itself.

The candidate still visits ordered pairs, obtains center components, preserves required counters/order, and pays its extra comparisons and any whole-scene certificate. Retained pairs continue into the same current geometry, scaling, solve and residual path. Certificate construction, fallback and branch overhead belong in complete-query cost. No inspected evidence isolates center norm time or predicts the balance of those costs. This note supplies no operation-count-to-runtime conversion, saved-time estimate, phase-share transfer or speedup bound.

The current query wrapper at line 207 includes preparation/build, schedule, solve, residual and consumption. A prospective comparison must preserve that complete boundary and ordinary lean assembly. A comparison only against the older anchor, or a candidate additionally changing storage, arithmetic, grid enumeration or solver behavior, would fail to isolate the proposed screen.

## Domain, zero and order conditions

The current source assigns invalid symbol `11` to all four rotor slots when the center is nonfinite, exceeds its `512` guard, or a displacement component exceeds its `257` guard. Only afterward does the ordinary center-neighborhood exclusion leave default symbol `1`. A naive far-component `continue` before those checks could turn invalid geometry into admitted outside-neighborhood zeros. That is a correctness failure even if its numerical coefficient list happens to be empty.

A prospective whole-scene certificate must justify that every screen-skipped pair would pass the original center guards and take the original neighborhood exclusion under the declared floating arithmetic. It must account for subtraction, finite/span evaluation and the runtime `Math.hypot` comparison; the real-number inequality alone does not finish that argument. This review chooses no threshold, span limit or safety margin. Failed, nonfinite or ambiguous certification must use the unchanged lean behavior, not silently zero the uncertain pairs or reject cases the old path admitted.

Preparation and its input/admission records must still occur. Retained pairs must preserve component arithmetic, rotor points, rotor branch order, source-ID order, current nominal scaling, source sums, coefficient ownership, cap/failure behavior and final load computation. The new screen may only prove the existing center-neighborhood exclusion. It cannot replace unsupported near-wake or invalid classifications with zero support. The current near-field symbol and invalid-term symbol feed admission at lines 170–171, so final force agreement alone is insufficient evidence.

The output symbol tensor/census, canonical CSR source order/support/values, graph/admission and solutions need comparison. Work counters specifically measuring changed algorithm work may differ, but their new semantics must be frozen and separated from scientific output equality. Do not relabel skipped norm calls as skipped logical pairs or silently change existing diagnostic meanings.

## Conditions for one separately preregistered test

Proceed only after the independent arithmetic review establishes a fixed conservative screen and whole-path fallback, with no target-guided threshold selection. Freeze an unchanged current lean comparator and a candidate differing only in the certificate/screen and necessary explicit work diagnostics. Keep the closed E/L, grid, storage and reuse outcomes visible as prior evidence.

Correctness controls must challenge neighborhood/guard boundaries, invalid/nonfinite inputs, translations and certificate failure, source ordering, near-domain retention, ownership and full model outputs. The protocol must distinguish exact-output qualification from the intended change in work counts. Independent checking, resource limits, complete-query cost and a practical stopping rule must be fixed before fresh target exposure. A failure closes this single question; it does not authorize threshold/cadence/grid tuning.

This recommendation is planning only. It creates no execution allocation, endorses no floating-point omission proof on its own, and makes no performance, physical-validity or novelty claim.
