When would we have an answer?

Before a real test, define how much disagreement matters and how uncertain each measurement is. A result clearly within that limit supports agreement at that precision; clearly outside shows mismatch; overlapping both remains inconclusive.

A missing calibration or required location makes the test incomplete—it does not count as success. We also need an independently justified conversion of the model to physical units.
