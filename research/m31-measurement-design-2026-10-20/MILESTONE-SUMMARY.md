# A useful test design — and a computational next route

20 October 2026, Shanghai time.

We completed an independently reviewed design explaining what would distinguish outward flow, swirl and a misleading calibration. Its key requirement is three signed velocity components at the same location, plus comparisons around the wake. The candidate layout has 48 planned spatial sites, not 48 independent experiments. Finite samples cannot establish perfect symmetry everywhere.

No new experiment, physical measurement, model evaluation or accuracy score was produced. Instrument precision, physical scaling and experimental acquisition remain unresolved. The plan does not certify physical feasibility or novelty.

**The active route remains computational.** The design is saved for claim boundaries; we will not pursue a laboratory acquisition. The next numerical question must be genuinely distinct from the already closed tree-speed tests and must retain a numerical reference and prospective decision rules.
