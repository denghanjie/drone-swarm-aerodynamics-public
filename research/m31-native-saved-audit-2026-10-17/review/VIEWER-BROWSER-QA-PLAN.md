The prior direct-load viewer's three attempts are exhausted and must not be reused. Keep the existing server at port 8876 unchanged.

Before launch, record immutable original native manifest, checker, browser, failed attempt and journal hashes; the independent audit source manifest, publication, attempt and report hashes; and the final viewer source/display/binding hashes. Confirm that the parent produced the linked independent audit summary. Preserve all QA attempts and failures; do not silently retry or overwrite artifacts. Use the parent's already-reviewed launch/containment mechanism and fixed resource envelope. A failed launch or cleanup remains failed.

1. Independently compare every projected pose word and every projected load word against the original saved browser record, including stable IDs, all six cases, both methods, all 64 drones, exact field addresses and the canonical per-call load hash. This comparison is JSON/word equality only and imports no model. Compare source input pose words as well. The compact display must not omit a scheduled state or method.
2. Open the native viewer once on desktop. Check three separate cards, a visible original EPERM failure banner, 12 returned calls and 12 independent qualifications. Internal browser `complete: true` must never become an overall run-success label. Confirm six table rows, six parity matches, four normalized categories and links to original and separate audit records.
3. Exercise exactly the 12 state/method combinations. For each, confirm selected IDs, 64 rendered body centers, current saved record address and exact displayed load words. Decode all 64 actual returned force vectors independently from saved hex words; compare every arrow's double-precision endpoint to center plus force times scale and its uploaded positions to the corresponding Float32 values. Confirm finite positions and endpoints, zero-force omission if applicable, correct selected-ID highlighting and no stale previous-state entries. Do not use screenshot appearance as numerical evidence.
4. On one representative selection, change arrow scale through 1, 4 and 8, hide/show arrows, choose a different stable ID, orbit and reset. Check buffer counts, updated QA exposure, camera reset and explicit scaling caption. No animation/interpolation or playback control should appear. Rejection of accepted evidence must clear bodies, arrows, selected load table, exact record and qualification state.
5. Verify failure handling in isolated request-intercepted tabs, without editing saved files: corrupt native binding/display bytes must reject the scene and keep the original failure visible; unavailable or corrupt audit result bytes must preserve the native scene while showing pending/unavailable audit and no qualified rows. Avoid intentional 404 requests in the main happy-path run.
6. Check one mobile viewport: controls wrap, the failure banner remains readable, tables scroll horizontally inside their containers and details/links stay accessible. Capture at most one representative desktop overview and one mobile overview unless a specific defect warrants an additional image. No redundant camera screenshots.
7. Collect console/page errors and failed requests separately for the happy path and intentional corruption scenarios. Require no unexpected errors, warnings or requests. Record exact tested source hashes, attempt status and any remaining limitations. Stop after the single preauthorized bounded attempt unless the parent explicitly authorizes a repair attempt.
8. After browser exit and cleanup, verify the frozen original native and independent audit records are byte-for-byte unchanged. Browser QA only supports the presentation and binding claim; it creates no additional scientific qualification, benchmark, physical validation or online certificate.
