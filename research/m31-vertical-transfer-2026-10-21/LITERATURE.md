## Verified published antecedents

**Bauersfeld, Leonard; Muller, Koen; Ziegler, Dominic; Coletti, Filippo; Scaramuzza, Davide. _Robotics meets Fluid Dynamics: A Characterization of the Induced Airflow below a Quadrotor as a Turbulent Jet_. arXiv:2403.13321v3, 12 December 2024.** [Version read](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13321v3). Local key `bauersfeld2024jetv3`.

Relevant portions of III-D, IV, VI, VII and VIII were read. Section VII demonstrates compensation by converting modeled inflow into additional power through vehicle-specific throttle behavior. This is an antecedent for downwash compensation, not a source for this accumulator recurrence, gain, clamping policy or a stability guarantee. The accepted RA-L manuscript is the verified version; the author laboratory lists journal publication in 2025. The journal DOI page failed verification and no journal DOI is asserted here. The smallest measured vehicle is 230 g, so the present 27 g moving coupled model is outside direct vehicle validation from this evidence. See A2 in both retained ledgers above.

**Shi, Guanya; Hönig, Wolfgang; Shi, Xichen; Yue, Yisong; Chung, Soon-Jo. _Neural-Swarm2: Planning and Control of Heterogeneous Multirotor Swarms Using Learned Interactions_. IEEE Transactions on Robotics 38(2), 1063–1079, 2022. DOI 10.1109/TRO.2021.3098436.** [Verified institutional record](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/psxc9-vps89). Local key `shi2022neuralswarm2`; technical reading used [arXiv:2012.05457v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.05457v2).

Relevant sections III-A/B, IV-A, VI-A/B and VII-B were read, with journal metadata checked. Online publication was 2021; the journal volume is 2022. Nominal rigid-body and actuator-delay dynamics are combined with learned interactions for planning/control; the reported interaction implementation learns vertical residual force although the general equations include torque. This supplies context for interaction-aware control, not validation of the current aerodynamic closure, rotor moments, Boids policy or memory controller. Its assumed bounded model errors are not measured guarantees for this implementation. Proofs and all flight results were not exhaustively audited, and code/training were not reproduced. See A4 in both retained ledgers above.
