# Background addendum: August 2026 primary preprint

Read 2026-10-15: Anoop Kiran, Nora Ayanian and Kenneth Breuer, [The Structure of Merging Turbulent Jets Beneath a Small Quadrotor, arXiv:2608.30900v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30900v1), posted 31 August 2026. Treat as a preprint; peer review is not established.

Sections 2, 3.2, 3.3 and 4 describe tethered Crazyflie measurements: 32 g, center-to-hub arm l=46 mm, rotor diameter 45 mm, two PIV cuts, and extent z/l up to 17.5 (about 0.805 m). The mean wake approaches canonical scaling while turbulent stresses retain cut dependence. Section 3.3 reports measured profiles below the analytical curve for xi approximately 1 and above.

Implication: our “core” label means the code's untapered radial region, not an empirically accurate region. This paper strengthens the small-vehicle context but validates neither independent multi-vehicle addition nor 8 m continuation. Its within-vehicle rotor merging is a different aggregation problem. Frozen CORE-EXTENT-CERTIFICATE fixtures and formulas remain unchanged.
