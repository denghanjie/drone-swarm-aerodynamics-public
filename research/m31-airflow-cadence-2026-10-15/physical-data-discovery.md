# M31 physical-data discovery audit

Audit: **2026-10-15, 14:36–14:41 UTC**. Scope: four primary research records, author/lab pages and linked repositories. Read the existing literature ledger and physical-validation access records first. This is a bounded discovery audit; failure to locate a public dataset is not proof that none exists.

**Result:** no compatible, independently measured force/moment-versus-flow dataset with verified access, configuration and reuse terms was secured. **Neural-Swarm flight telemetry is publicly fetchable**, so “no data are accessible” would be too broad. The fetched example is explicitly unsuitable for force estimation. M31's physical-validation gate remains open; this audit changes neither the airflow-off checkpoint nor its failed numerical trajectory criterion.

## Smallest defensible next comparison

**Conditional recommendation:** obtain the Kiran dataset's accessible files, reuse terms, geometry/actuation metadata and transducer uncertainty, then match one static aligned pair and one offset pair at the published 4 V/hover-PWM condition. Compare normalized receiver mean thrust and pitch moment with case-level repeats; reserve a case from any fitting. Compare velocity profiles separately in the actual laser plane, not as synchronized force–flow samples. Keep the paper's unresolved “65% of nominal” versus conclusion wording discrepancy out of numeric calibration. This is a proposed comparison, not a result.

Neural-Swarm telemetry could support a later check against an **inferred residual-force** baseline after rights, vehicle identity, actuator calibration and valid flight windows are resolved. It cannot by itself certify a force-versus-flow law, six-axis wake response or the 27 g M31 configuration. No compatible comparison was run here. No simulation, plot digitization, author contact, paid acquisition or large download was performed.
