WHAT WE LEARNED

Updating less often changes the loads.

At a 10 ms update interval, the lateral pass’s largest lift discrepancy was about 1.85 mN. The offset approach’s was about 0.019 mN. Shorter intervals reduced peak errors in both tested paths. These two cases do not establish a general error bound.

The sampled peak checks agreed between observation grids. The 5 ms average-error checks failed the preset sampling screen. The report preserves that limitation.

WHAT THE ANIMATION MEANS

The poses are controlled test inputs.

Both drones follow prescribed poses here so we can isolate stale airflow. This is a recorded model diagnostic, with no flight controller or integrated flight response. The moving receiver and fixed-hover wake are not experimentally validated.

Timings cover two-drone model queries on an Apple M4. Rendering, Boids, flight dynamics, contacts and GPU work are excluded. They do not measure a 5,000-drone browser simulation.
