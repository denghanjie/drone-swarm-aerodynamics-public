# Prescribed-motion airflow cadence: prospective protocol

Question: within the unchanged M28 mean-wake and receiver equations, what sampled thrust/roll-pitch-moment discrepancy and evaluation cost result from holding rotor inflows for 10, 5, or 2.5 ms instead of recomputing at each query? This is a deliberately interpretable development diagnostic, not a novel method, a flock-performance result or physical validation.

## Prior work and purpose

The main branch at `1336f5c416875a9651f92b58b2ed06649f25b04e` already contains [temporal sampling](https://github.com/denghanjie/drone-swarm-aerodynamics-public/tree/1336f5c416875a9651f92b58b2ed06649f25b04e/research/campaign-temporal-sampling): both airflow-reference screens failed and no cadence was qualified. The [joint-stage reference](https://github.com/denghanjie/drone-swarm-aerodynamics-public/blob/1336f5c416875a9651f92b58b2ed06649f25b04e/research/campaign-joint-stage-reference/MILESTONE.md) remained incomplete despite passing its J screen. Its known seeds and all M31/M32 states remain exposed. The R8 correctness/performance gate also remains incomplete. We preserve those results and do not repeat temporal sampling as a novelty claim.

This smaller experiment uses two explicitly prescribed analytic paths, no ODE integration of either prescribed path, no Boids/controller feedback, no obstacles, and an independent arithmetic implementation of the same equations. Thus integrated-trajectory convergence is not needed to evaluate the instantaneous formula at a known pose. Sampling extrema and time discretization of the held approximation remain separate questions. It does not replace either historical reference gate.

## Frozen cases and treatments

`config.json` defines two one-second paths: a receiver passes laterally from x=-0.25 to +0.25 m at z=1 m under a source at z=1.65 m; a receiver at x=0.07 m rises from z=1 to 1.25 m under a source at z=1.8 m. Both vehicles remain upright, with zero angular velocity and constant nominal hover rotor thrust. These are kinematic query fixtures, not trajectories generated by physically consistent actuation. Source amplitude is fixed hover strength exactly as in M28. No random seeds are used; these new chosen analytical fixtures are development evidence, not statistically independent flight trials or held-out mission confirmation.

Use M28 `sum-rotor` unchanged: four receiver-rotor queries, original finite-support fades and domain errors, linear source addition, ideal constant-induced-power receiver law, changed net force and roll/pitch moments, nominal yaw moment retained. The source mean wake is vehicle-centered, not four individual source-rotor wakes. `sum-force` is not a cheaper-field baseline because it computes the same four inflows and only cancels roll/pitch changes.

Time uses exact integer ticks at 0.5 ms. Primary observation every 2 ticks (1 ms); refined observation every tick. Refresh periods are 20, 10 and 5 ticks. When a 2.5 ms refresh falls between primary observations, evaluate its exact prescribed pose at that refresh tick; never delay to the next observation. Compare each held value at a query against the instantaneous field at the same pose. Recompute receiver thrust response at every query using current nominal thrust and the held winds. Instantaneous primary comparison means field queried at every primary observation; it is not an integrated continuous-flow trajectory.

## Verification before outcome interpretation

An independently coded all-pairs oracle uses its own quaternion rotation, explicit wake formula, own rotor wrench and bisection receiver solver. It must not call the production field/kernel/receiver functions. Verify all refined-path fields, net forces and moments within absolute 1e-12 in their respective SI units; tolerance checks implementation consistency at double precision, not physical accuracy. Verify zero inflow/base identity (two verification-only 2.5 ms single-vehicle RK4 calls, 0.005 s aggregate; no integrated scientific trajectory), self-exclusion, centered symmetry, mirrored offset moment signs, translation covariance, domain exits, and source-strength invariance to source motor-state changes. The last check documents a limitation of the fixed-hover-source hypothesis, not a desirable dynamic property. Compare deterministic extra poses near fades, tilted poses and three-source aggregation. Preserve every mismatch; do not patch and rerun under this cap.

## Outcomes and decision criteria

For each path/cadence, save every refined-time inflow and modeled total thrust/roll/pitch moment, plus instantaneous values. Report maximum absolute discrepancies over the observation grid augmented by exact old-hold left limits at every refresh event (including the terminal event), with worst time and side. These left-limit wrenches use the current prescribed pose and the previous held inflow; both observation grids include the same event limits even when an event falls between primary samples. Report uniform-sample RMS separately; it is not continuous quadrature. also normalize force by mg and moment by mg times opposite-motor distance. Do not hide absolute values or use relative error at a zero reference. Include all cases and the zero-error instantaneous baseline. Record actual field calls, input domain margins and dimensions.

Primary maximum/RMS estimates are compared with the refined grid. Mark sampling as unresolved for an observable if the maximum or RMS changes by more than max(5% of refined result, 1e-9 N for force or 1e-11 N·m for moment). This diagnostic threshold is explicitly not a physical tolerance. Passing it is sampled self-consistency, not a rigorous continuous-time bound. The query phase relative to refresh is fixed here; no all-phase guarantee. If sampling fails, retain and report it; do not refine again or promote the chosen cadence.

For cost: one warmup and five recorded passes per path/variant on the primary observation grid. Rotate the four variant execution orders across passes to reduce simple order bias. Record field-evaluation milliseconds and complete query-loop milliseconds separately, with field-call counts and a numeric checksum. Total includes pose generation, scheduling, field and receiver/wrench calls; excludes recording, oracle checking, rendering, controller, flight integration, contact checks, GPU/data transfer and browser overhead. Component timing itself has overhead. Summarize median and min/max; five technical repeats are not five independent encounters or device samples. No browser/5,000-drone/low-end claim. Saving fewer field evaluations is known by construction; whether measured loop time improves is empirical.

There is no operational cadence acceptance threshold and no optimization or fitting in this campaign. Any later predictor/interpolation/adaptive scheme requires another frozen protocol and fresh challenge conditions, explicitly compared with prior temporal work. If prerequisites pass, these cases provide numerical baseline evidence only. If any verification fails, stop before timings; retain failure and do not change tolerance.

## Limits and stop rules

Independent campaign: at most 120 executable seconds, 40,000 total calls across production and independent field evaluators (including warmup and timing), 32 MiB raw outputs, one process, no paid resources. At most one complete invocation; never overwrite `started.json`. External watchdog counts all execution and terminates/reaps children; cooperative call/storage checks precede computations/writes. Save an incomplete failure record on error. No retries, no expansion, no full flock matrix, and no restart of the closed M31 diagnosis.

Physical limits follow the verified [literature ledger](../m31-restart-2026-10-15/literature/annotated-ledger.md): Bauersfeld et al. arXiv:2403.13321v3 near-hover/far-field mean-wake equations motivate the field; the 27 g scale, receiver law, overlap and motion remain unvalidated. Kiran et al. RSS2025 motivates separate offset force/moment and actuation-matched measurements; it does not calibrate this model. No independent compatible raw measurement dataset has been obtained. Novelty is not established.
