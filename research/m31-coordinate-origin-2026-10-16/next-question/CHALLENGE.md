## Disposition

**Close and publish the negative coordinate campaign. Stop its position-only candidate choice. No candidate earned fresh confirmation.** The next useful progress is a production-interface and cost decision using existing evidence, not another numerical exposure chosen because time remains.

The inherited challenge explicitly says: “If C also fails, stop the position-only design.” The frozen coordinate protocol, “Failure dependencies and decision,” likewise says: “If none qualify, stop the position-only choice.” Both conditions apply. Renaming a fourth variant a “compiler fix,” changing one constant or offset, adding a barrier, moving an operation into another helper, or changing the seed does not bypass that decision. The missing translated A/B cells and C serial reduction must remain missing for their declared reasons. No rescue, permutation sweep, new origin, tolerance relaxation, per-field hybrid reference, or next-best-arm promotion follows from this note. [E1, E2]

A materially different architecture can remain a future research question, but it needs an application contract and a resource case before a fresh experiment is justified. The most plausible such question is whether the application can supply a bounded common coordinate frame before GPU packing at an acceptable preparation cost. That is a change in data ownership and interface, not a successful continuation of C. A storage-materialization design is a more expensive alternative whose benefit is not established. If neither has a credible whole-query case, retain the current qualified CPU query and redirect effort to a specific unresolved browser/production or physical-validation requirement. Do not rerun the already measured full Node CPU baseline merely under a fresh seed. [E3, E4, L1, L2]

## Observed vectors, possible causes, and missing evidence

The frozen source writes C as high-position difference plus low-position difference, then subtracts the receiver rotor offset. The actual saved C center-vector maximum discrepancies from Q1 at the translated origin are:

`[9.5367431640625e−7, 9.5367431640625e−7, 0]` m.

Its rotor-displacement maxima are:

`[9.909272193908691e−7, 1.0617077350616455e−6, 1.1781230568885803e−7]` m.

These are observed materialized-vector discrepancies. They precede downstream profile quantities in the written dataflow, but maxima can come from different records and cannot prove that one vector discrepancy causes every failed rotor's accumulated error. No post-hoc expected pair calculation was performed here. [E6, E8]

At the canonical origin, B and C have identical reported vector words in every compared reached component. A and B have equal center vectors but differing rotor-vector words in 8,637 / 2,343 / 16,929 component records, with component maximum `1.1920928955078125e−7` m. Thus the A/B source intervention had an observable vector effect in that origin, where both passed. It does not establish a translated B result; those cells were skipped. It also does not establish that C behaves identically to B when low words are nonzero. [E8]

WGSL's fixed 21 September 2026 draft §15.7.5 permits reassociation; §15.7.4 gives operation-specific accuracy rules. Therefore the source expression is not proof of the backend's operation graph. Reassociation is a plausible explanation to investigate, **not an established root cause, compiler defect, or proven standards violation**. The present records contain no instruction trace or counterfactual backend. SAPPORO's published high/low formula is relevant prior art, not a guarantee that its CUDA result transfers to this WGSL implementation. [L1, L3]

Equally, the adequacy of Q1 does not qualify the delivered C kernel. Q1 preserves semantic position information; T_C is what the GPU actually returned. That separation is precisely why both gates were required. Nor does the failure establish that no common-frame architecture can ever work, that all f32 arithmetic is inadequate, or that all remaining error comes from packed axes/constants. Those are broader claims than this experiment supplies. Goldberg's distinction between error in operands and later arithmetic supports this separation, without diagnosing this specific run. [L2, E1]

## A common-frame interface: a conditional architecture question

A defensible future question would be: **Can the existing host/state owner provide a bounded common local frame to the complete wake query, with representation error and frame-maintenance cost explicitly included, under the same required physical model and numerical outputs?** This is an engineering proposal inferred from the prior arithmetic literature and the failed interface experiment; none of those sources supplies its application-specific success. [L1, L2, E6]

It needs a static contract answering all of the following before a new geometry is exposed:

- Who owns the authoritative position state, its precision, and the frame origin? Is one common origin already known from map/state semantics, or would it be newly selected and maintained? A frame must not be chosen from a failing rotor, error ranking or favorable output.
- What bound on whole-scene extent makes one common frame useful? Removing a common translation does not reduce a very extended scene's diameter. Receiver-specific or tile-specific frames change the architecture again and need their own neighbor, replication and consistency costs.
- What exact host subtraction/conversion error is admitted? Arbitrary binary64 positions minus a chosen origin are not automatically exact. Preserve original R, a decoded interface reference, exact/explicit representation residuals and full class/domain gates. Do not spend the observed R-origin drift as additional error budget.
- Is the frame just a representation of one static snapshot, or does the implementation rebase evolving state? A future moving/rotating frame would require a separately coherent state/dynamics contract. Do not silently change velocities, integration, controller coordinates or external geometry while presenting this as a packing modification.
- Which work is paid on every query, and which is reusable under a stated lifetime? Charge the full-node coordinate pass, admitted ranges, conversions, any allocation/copy, uploads, shared metadata, invalidation and rebasing. A per-source common-frame representation can have O(N) preparation; pair-relative data costs O(KN) for K receivers and becomes quadratic when K=N. These are source-level scaling descriptions, not timings.

The immediate temptingly easy “test” is invalid as fresh evidence: subtracting the known `[64,−32,16]` generator shift from this already exposed exact construction would recover the deliberately favorable canonical frame. That would recycle the passing control and select an interface around known data. It cannot count as confirmation or justify broad precision claims. [E1, E5]

Only if the application can justify this interface independently should the parent consider a separately frozen question on genuinely fresh geometry, with broader coordinate representation than this canonical dyadic control, one fixed architecture, explicit preparation accounting, original matching-oracle gates and a terminal failure rule. This memo does not choose that architecture, create its generator, define a new run budget, or authorize a test. An eventual passed small component would still need an integrated workload comparison and would not validate flight physics.

## Storage boundaries: observable values, not a presumed cure

A separate producer/consumer storage interface could expose intermediate values as auditable words. WGSL §14.4.4 specifies host-shared numeric layout, and §17.2.1 defines bit reinterpretation. These support an explicit data interface; they do not establish that merely storing an already inaccurate C vector repairs it or identify the arithmetic that produced it. A `let`, local variable, helper, `bitcast` round trip or same-expression diagnostic write must not be advertised as a universal arithmetic-order fence. [L3]

Before considering this architecture, identify exactly which value is materialized, which stage creates it, what error bound that stage has, and which later stage reads those actual saved words. A prepass that calculates the same unsuccessful vector and stores it may preserve the same discrepancy. Materializing an earlier intermediate changes the proposed computation, stage count and memory traffic; that would need a new contract, not an extension of the closed C cell. [E6, E8]

For a complete query, center-vector materialization requires pair-indexed values or a justified compact-neighbor representation; rotor-vector materialization multiplies that scope. The four-receiver diagnostic's retained matrix is not a production all-receiver design. Charge dispatches, reads/writes, synchronization, ownership, peak storage and data movement; do not borrow a small diagnostic's memory footprint as full-query feasibility. SAPPORO §2.1 makes communication and task decomposition relevant, but its CUDA workload gives no throughput forecast for this browser. [L1, E2]

This is a second-choice design investigation, not the next recommended experiment. A common frame, if genuinely supported by the application, has a clearer route to avoiding pair-scale preparation. If it is not supported, the fact that storage makes a value observable is insufficient reason to pay the cost without a full-query benefit case.

## Existing CPU and DOT evidence must constrain the next decision

There is already a complete static-query Node baseline. The exact-zero campaign's saved 5,000-node marginal medians are M `1.299449480` s, Z `1.192637416` s, E `1.083176459` s and L `1.058974854` s, with eight paired timing blocks. The timed work includes query/grid/screening, rotor fields, roots, loads and checksum on the reported Node/Apple M4 environment. It excludes input generation, validation/persistence, flight integration, rendering and GPU transfers. The predeclared E/Z and L/E incremental-usefulness criteria failed; the smaller marginal medians do not reverse those paired decisions. [E3]

The earlier source-omission campaign already measured complete queries and its later ablation separated the positive-omission question. DOT's lean/support/reuse variants are also prior work. Its lean gains were mixed and scaling stopped before N=500; the independent corrected previous-zero comparison is 3/12 gates, not the stale 4/12 wording. Current M is not demonstrated to be the strongest possible CPU implementation, but that uncertainty does not authorize another CPU microtuning sweep. [E3, E4]

AUDIT-C1/R8 is terminal and resource-limited: `fullCorrectnessQualified=false`, `benchmarkPermission=false`, `gateExecuted=false`, `benchmarksExecuted=0`. It must never be promoted into a benchmark candidate or silently resumed. M8 mechanics and M9 airflow-off hybrid evidence are not full wake-query evidence. [E4]

Consequently “run a full CPU baseline” and “scale the old optimization” are not useful next questions by themselves. A truly missing engineering question could be a browser-worker implementation of the already fixed complete CPU query, or a production-mode accounting study that isolates diagnostic instrumentation without changing the model, output ownership or correctness contract. Either needs a documented product/research need, a compatible comparator and a separate prospective protocol; neither is selected here. Browser latency, Node query duration, GPU term-dispatch latency and Three.js frame time must not be substituted for one another. [E3, E4, L1]

The default next checkpoint should therefore contain the terminal negative result, independent saved review, clear viewer/claim updates, and a short architecture decision naming the authoritative state/frame owner and complete-query comparator. If those static questions cannot support a materially useful interface change, defer further precision experiments and pursue the already documented unresolved validation or workload questions. Preserving a well-supported negative result is progress; filling the remaining time with a weaker test is not a requirement.
