Static audit of the M31 component and whole-observable budgets

2026-10-16. Prepared for the coordinator as a standalone analytical memo. Repository inspected: the public research worktree. This audit read source text, protocols and existing JSON/report text only. It ran no generator, scientific module, numerical calculator/script, binary decoder, geometry/profile/reference/root evaluation, checker, browser, GPU work, benchmark or dynamics. No repository file was changed. Numerical comparisons below are dimensional desk arithmetic on declared constants, not new empirical outcomes.

The wake epsilon is materially stricter than the earlier body-observable targets. Its provenance is a separately chosen additional rotor-force allocation of `1e-7 N`; it was never derived by spending the original source-omission allowance. The coordinate campaign therefore establishes the failure of its frozen position-only candidate choice at that strict component contract. It does not establish that a complete GPU force query must exceed the earlier body-thrust or roll/pitch targets, nor that useful GPU force accuracy is impossible. Equally, it supplies no complete-query pass at those targets. The omitted stages must remain omitted.

The legitimate unresolved question is whether one fixed complete implementation can return the chosen body/world observables within a prospectively justified numerical/application contract on new, nontrivial geometry, after accounting for every stage and all required process/domain gates. This can be a distinct question even if it retains primitive A. It is not automatically authorized, and it is not a fresh confirmation earned by the failed coordinate campaign.

**Frozen provenance and meaning of the numbers**

1. Original source-omission targets. [PROTOCOL.md](../../../research/m31-source-omission-2026-10-16/PROTOCOL.md) specifies `epsilonT = .005*m*g` and `epsilonRoll = epsilonPitch = .0005*m*g*D`, with `D = 2*sqrt(2)*a`, `a=.028 m`, `m=.027 kg`, and `g=9.81`. The [candidate source](../../../research/m31-source-omission-2026-10-16/source/candidate.cjs) freezes the actual expressions, `L=f0/(3*vi0)`, the per-rotor numerical reserve `eta=1e-12 N`, and capacity calculation. The [published result summary](../../../research/m31-source-omission-2026-10-16/README.md) displays the targets as **1.32435 mN** body thrust and **10.488316895 micro-N m** for each roll/pitch moment. These were explicitly analyst-selected numerical targets with no safety or measurement significance. They govern a positive-omission approximation on static, fixed-nominal-thrust states; they are not general flight tolerances.

2. Original numerical equivalence gates are a different contract. The same source-omission protocol, line 41, requires B/M versus independent I inflow agreement at `1e-11 m/s + 1e-12 relative`, rotor agreement at `1e-12 N`, body thrust at `5e-12 N`, moments at `2e-13 N m`, and separate world-vector tolerances. The larger approximation allowances must not be described as the original binary64 equivalence gates. Neither later f32 campaign passes those old gates merely by passing its separately declared target.

3. Receiver allocation. [Receiver PROTOCOL.md](../../../research/m31-browser-receiver-2026-10-16/PROTOCOL.md) says the `1e-7 N` allocation was chosen to keep this arithmetic component small, approximately 1.51 ppm of nominal rotor thrust. It explicitly says this is not a measurement-based flight tolerance, use of the earlier omission allowance, or proof of practical adequacy. Its generic assembly statement is `4e` thrust and `4*a*e` moment for exact later assembly; GPU body/world assembly was outside scope.

4. Additional wake allocation. [The receiver next-question draft](../../../research/m31-browser-receiver-2026-10-16/next-question/candidate-protocol.md) explicitly proposes a distinct `e_w=1e-7 N`, requiring subsequent adoption. Its sensitivity argument is an ideal-equation derivation, not a GPU certificate. The [next-question decision](../../../research/m31-browser-receiver-2026-10-16/next-question/DECISION.md) additionally requires resolving a term/L1 criterion and states that sensitivity creates neither physical tolerance nor composed end-to-end guarantee. The earlier draft's treatment of L1 as a diagnostic is historical; it is not the final campaign's acceptance rule.

5. Wake adoption and exact encoding. [Wake PROTOCOL.md](../../../research/m31-browser-wake-slice-2026-10-16/PROTOCOL.md) and [host.cjs](../../../research/m31-browser-wake-slice-2026-10-16/source/host.cjs) use `sourceInduced=sqrt(.027*9.81/(8*1.225*area))`, `area=pi*.023*.023`, `f0=.027*9.81/4`, and the declared binary64 evaluation `epsilonWake=3*sourceInduced*1e-7/f0`. The [constant record](../../../research/m31-browser-wake-slice-2026-10-16/constants/constant-encodings.json) stores epsilon as `0.000018270441835956964 m/s`, f64 word `3ef3286f33e44f63`; allowance `1e-12 m/s` has word `3d719799812dea11`. Acceptance uses the exact dyadic values of these binary64 encodings, not their displayed decimal strings or packed f32 forms. The allowance connects to the stored numerical reference; it is not a certified error bound against physical truth. Source-induced and receiver-induced speeds coincide mathematically at the common fixed hover nominal force; different source-expression groupings must not silently be asserted to have identical floating encodings.

6. Coordinate adoption and stop rule. [Coordinate PROTOCOL.md](../../../research/m31-coordinate-origin-2026-10-16/PROTOCOL.md) reuses this exact epsilon and allowance. Its interface, term pre-gate and final gates respectively charge `L1(Q-R)`, `L1(T-R)`, and `L1(T-R)+abs(S-exactSum(T))`, each plus allowance. [Saved configuration](../../../research/m31-coordinate-origin-2026-10-16/results/result.json) retains `wakeForceAllocationN:1e-7`. Protocol line 52 requires every relevant condition at both origins, retains skips/failures, and says that if none qualifies, **stop the position-only choice**. Line 54 grants fresh confirmation only to an actually selected candidate. No such candidate was selected.

**Analytical comparison and composition**

For the ideal fixed-nominal receiver law `x^3+(U/vi0)*x-1=0`, `f=f0*x`, `U>=0`, differentiation yields

`abs(df/dU)=(f0/vi0)*x^2/(1+2*x^3) <= f0/(3*vi0)=L`, with `0<x<=1`.

The scalar factor `x^2/(1+2*x^3)` is nondecreasing on this interval: its derivative is `2*x*(1-x^3)/(1+2*x^3)^2`. This justifies the ideal-model sensitivity mapping. It does not establish bounds on f32 implementation arithmetic, physical rotor response, or the numerical oracle's distance from the exact equation.

The [independent wrench mapping](../../../research/m31-source-omission-2026-10-16/source/independent.cjs) is

`T=f0+f1+f2+f3`,

`roll=a*(f0+f1-f2-f3)`,

`pitch=a*(-f0+f1+f2-f3)`.

Here the four `f` symbols denote rotor outputs, not all the common nominal constant. If each arbitrary two-sided rotor error has magnitude at most `e`, triangle inequalities give `abs(deltaT)<=4e` and `abs(deltaRoll),abs(deltaPitch)<=4*a*e`. At `e=1e-7 N` the dimensional comparison is:

| Observable | Bound from this one component allocation, assuming ideal/exact composition | Earlier whole-observable target | Scale comparison |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Body thrust | `4e-7 N = 0.0004 mN` | `1.32435 mN` | Target is `3310.875` times this bound |
| Each roll/pitch moment | `1.12e-8 N m = 0.0112 micro-N m` | `10.488316895 micro-N m` | Target is about `936` times this bound |

This is a scale comparison, not a pass/fail assessment of saved GPU values. It demonstrates that the strict wake allocation is orders of magnitude smaller than the earlier output targets.

If, hypothetically, the entire earlier output allowance were devoted to one uniform generic rotor error, it would permit `e <= min(epsilonT/4, epsilonMoment/(4*a))`; the moment condition binds at roughly `9.36e-5 N`. That hypothetical exhausts the budget and is **not** a proposed allocation, authorization, or practical recommendation. Reference, wake, receiver, assembly and any other approximation all require explicit treatment.

The original source-omission proof used **one-sided** rotor errors in `[0,L*B]`, producing `2*a*L*B` for each moment, plus `4*a*eta` for the two-sided numerical reserve. It is invalid to reuse the favorable `2a` factor for arbitrary GPU roundoff. It is also invalid to spend a full omission allowance and a full GPU allowance independently while claiming the same final output target. Any composition must allocate their combined effects. Keeping positive omission disabled in a future comparator would avoid this particular double spending, but is a prospective design choice.

The wake and receiver allocations are separate contributions. If both were genuinely established on a matching domain/interface with original nominal parameters, a basic triangle bound would add them per rotor before assembly. The finite receiver campaign's pass on its saved/scalar U values is not a bound for every new GPU-generated U, and neither separate campaign tests that composition.

A useful future decomposition, expressed without committing to an implementation, is:

`abs(fhat_i-fref_i) <= abs(fhat_i-phi_i(Uhat_i)) + L_i*abs(Uhat_i-Uref_i) + abs(phi_i(Uref_i)-fref_i)`.

`phi_i` is the ideal receiver using the intended original nominal motor value; `Uhat_i` must be the actual value supplied to that receiver. The first term then includes receiver arithmetic and any nominal/constant representation effects; the second includes all inflow construction/representation, term, reduction, optional omission and interface errors once; the final term accounts for reference numerical error. One must define stage boundaries carefully so U packing is not counted twice or silently omitted. For different per-rotor bounds `e_i`, exact body assembly bounds are `sum(e_i)` and `a*sum(e_i)`, with separately budgeted assembly arithmetic. World rotation additionally needs orientation/rotation error and assembly terms. Quaternion norm tolerance does not license treating the actual numerical rotation as an exactly orthogonal matrix.

**Application ownership and frame scope from source text**

The application source does not currently furnish an independently maintained common local origin as a free invariant. [M28 sim.cjs](../../../outputs/milestone-28-source/sim.cjs) owns JS host `states`, rebuilds flow at `flowDt`, calculates controls, and integrates those states on the host. At lines 37–40 it refreshes the wake field at a fixed cadence and holds the resulting winds for integration. [aero.cjs](../../../outputs/milestone-28-source/aero.cjs) computes receiver loads from the current lagged motor states `y[13:17]`, retains nominal yaw torque, and holds the supplied winds through RK4 stages. These nominal forces vary; they are not universally the static campaign's `mg/4`. The source wake strength nevertheless remains prescribed isolated-hover amplitude.

The static query in [candidate.cjs](../../../research/m31-source-omission-2026-10-16/source/candidate.cjs) fixes the common `f0` and sets nominal yaw to zero. Its numerical qualification therefore cannot be transferred wholesale to varying motor inputs. A future static fixed-f0 question is still legitimate if named that way; a flight-facing question must admit and verify a stated nominal-force range, including the zero-force branch and the corresponding sensitivity/receiver behavior.

World positions are consumed by route lookup, fixed course obstacles, ground-height checks, crossing detection, neighbor grids, measurement and frame output, not just the wake. See M28 sim lines 8, 13, 17, 24, 28, 35 and 41. A floating-origin design would need an explicit ownership/conversion/update contract for these consumers, with its cost and consistency accounted. This is not grounds to implement rebasing during this audit. [M31 sim.cjs](../../../outputs/milestone-31-source/sim.cjs) uses `F.integrate` and reports `airflow:'off'` at line 50; the checkpoint's active M31 simulator is not already a GPU aerodynamic application.

**A distinct whole-observable question: legitimate, missing, conditional**

A separately frozen feasibility question with unchanged primitive A can be legitimate. A is already exposed and must be described as such; choosing it for a simple fixed complete architecture is not a fresh algorithm-selection finding. The new object of assessment would be the complete query's observable outputs and, if separately authorized after correctness, its complete cost. The claim would not be that A passed the old position-only contract. Nor would this experiment count as the candidate confirmation that the failed campaign did not earn.

The older body targets were frozen before the present failures, which is relevant evidence against inventing them solely to fit these failures. Their provenance is nevertheless an analyst-selected positive-omission contract. Reusing their magnitudes for generic two-sided full-pipeline arithmetic requires an explicit new decision about purpose and error accounting; it does not convert them into measured application tolerances. If a physical/application claim is intended, the relevant observable requirements must come from independent model/measurement/controller/trajectory needs and uncertainty, rather than from the errors the present implementation happened to produce. Literature can motivate model limitations and validation design; it cannot certify that these chosen numbers are physically adequate.

Existing work does not answer this complete browser/GPU question. The [existing-work audit](../../../research/m31-zero-screen-2026-10-16/EXISTING-WORK-AUDIT.md) distinguishes M8's independent mechanics kernel, M9's airflow-off hybrid loop, and CPU DOT wake work. Subsequent receiver/wake/coordinate campaigns add finite component evidence but still do not compose the whole query. Existing M/Z/E/L CPU work already provides complete static numerical query and cost evidence; merely rerunning that CPU exercise with a new seed would not answer a new question. The [zero-screen report](../../../research/m31-zero-screen-2026-10-16/README.md) reports complete-query medians at 5,000, including field/root/load/checksum, with its specific exclusions and negative incremental usefulness decision intact. Current M is not established as the strongest available CPU implementation; DOT's exact lean/support/reuse history and mixed gains must remain visible.

If “CPU-packed baseline” means Q0 or Q1 here, those are represented-input binary64 wake references on four selected receivers, not an already qualified complete CPU-packed force-query implementation. If another complete packed baseline exists, pin its source, interface, output contract and results before calling it established. Otherwise it is a prospective comparator. Original-input independent I remains necessary to expose total representation error; agreement with a packed-input CPU baseline alone can hide it.

A useful new contract would settle the following before implementation or execution:

1. Fix the actual question and consumed outputs: static fixed-hover force feasibility, varying-motor static application query, or dynamics are different scopes. State whether body thrust, roll/pitch, nominal yaw, world vectors, power or controller-consumed quantities govern. Do not infer trajectory accuracy, stability, physical validity or safety from one-query force accuracy.
2. Establish the numerical/output targets and their meaning independently of saved failure magnitudes. Document whether they remain analyst-selected model-fidelity requirements or have external application/measurement support. Allocate reference/model, packing/geometry, terms, sums, receiver, assembly and optional omission explicitly. Model discrepancy versus reality is conceptually separate from implementation error against the model; a budget label alone cannot bound it.
3. Choose one complete architecture and one matched, qualified CPU comparator before new outcomes, using the static resource/application decision. Freeze A if A is chosen; do not add local-first/high-low/rebase variants as rescue arms, tune thresholds to exposed errors, or switch after failure. Primitive A may remain unchanged while previously missing sum/root/assembly stages are prospectively specified and independently reviewed. That is new pipeline work, not permission to revive the skipped coordinate cells.
4. Fix a credible input domain, fresh geometry and coverage that cannot collapse to the intentionally exact canonical-origin control. Require nontrivial positive wake exposure, general coordinates and relevant translations, boundary/invalid cases, orientation and nominal-motor coverage, and all receivers required by the claimed complete query. Existing deliberately exactly representable canonical geometry can remain a named exposed control, not acceptance evidence for general accuracy. No regeneration, filtering or domain relaxation after outcomes.
5. Preserve exact required policy/domain behavior and original-reference semantics. Retain term/L1 and old strict compatibility results as separately labeled evidence; do not rename their old failures. Any new component allocations or observable-only acceptance have to be fixed in advance and justified by the new composition, not by observed cancellation or a tolerance sweep. Test final delivered outputs against the original-input reference, not merely packed-input agreement. Final-output checks do not excuse nonfinite, missing, invalid or wrongly classified results.
6. Define output ownership, host state/frame ownership, data freshness, input preparation, neighbor/grid work, packing, transfer, synchronization, readback and CPU/GPU assembly. If performance is later part of the question, count the complete work consumed by the application and compare a matching complete query; do not compare GPU kernel-only work with instrumented complete CPU time or reuse fresh geometry only on one side. Rendering, control, held-flow cadence and dynamics require their own scope statements.
7. Freeze bounded execution, independent review, evidence persistence, terminal failure rules and the precise claim permitted by a pass. Any fresh test is finite evidence for that contract, not universal adapter/domain coverage. This memo grants no launch, benchmark, tuning, extra arm or budget.

A disguised relaxation would instead rescore the old four-receiver data at a looser threshold, declare the strict component failure passed, run its skipped reductions/roots, deliberately select exactly representable new geometry, choose an output target just above a saved maximum, search architectures until one survives, or call a newly improved result the unearned coordinate confirmation. Those actions do not become a separate question merely by changing a document title.

The recommended decision is therefore to keep the position-only result closed negative, recognize that whole-observable feasibility remains unanswered and potentially useful, and finish the static application/output/resource contract before proposing any such new campaign. All earlier temporal/physical closure limitations remain unresolved. R8 remains resource-limited with `fullCorrectnessQualified:false`, `benchmarkPermission:false`, `gateExecuted:false`, and `benchmarksExecuted:0` as preserved in the [existing-work audit](../../../research/m31-zero-screen-2026-10-16/EXISTING-WORK-AUDIT.md). No old gate, result, failure or ledger is reopened.
