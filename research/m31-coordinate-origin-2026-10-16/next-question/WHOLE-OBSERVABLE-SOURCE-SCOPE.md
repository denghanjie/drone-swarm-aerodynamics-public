# Static scope: a possible complete GPU load query

**Not adopted, implemented or authorized to run.** A reusable binary64 reference and several GPU primitives exist; a qualified all-receiver GPU pipeline preserving actual motor states does not. A separate question could concern complete body thrust/moments on every receiver under a justified application contract. It must not relabel the coordinate campaign's failed gates, complete its skipped cells, or count as its unearned confirmation.

Only source text, existing reports and source hashes were inspected. No scientific binary was decoded; no model import, geometry generation, shader compilation, browser, checker, root solve or benchmark ran.

## Preserve the actual observable

The authoritative input is each original 17-state record: position, velocity, quaternion xyzw, angular velocity, and **lagged nominal motor thrusts at indices 13–16**, in newtons. Use those four motor states, not actuator commands or an invented `mg/4` replacement. Preserve unequal motors and nominal yaw. Admit the declared finite/nonnegative motor domain explicitly; include zero nominal and zero inflow semantics before any division. Do not silently clip values into the old receiver kernel's range.

In `sum-rotor`, assemble effective rotor forces into body thrust and roll/pitch, but retain the **nominal** yaw torque. Preserve rotor order/signs `[+,+,+],[-,+,−],[-,−,+],[+,−,−]`. Return all four effective forces, body thrust and all three body moments; world force/moment and nominal-relative changes should follow I's same quaternion convention if those are required observables. These are rotor support forces/moments; gravity and gyroscopic terms belong to dynamics and are excluded here. I's `loads` already implements this distinction.

Do **not** reuse `m31-source-omission/.../candidate.cjs:65`, `m31-zero-screen/.../control.cjs:83` or the zero-screen driver's `independent(...)` wrapper unchanged: they prescribe `f0=mg/4` and set yaw to zero. Their 17-column output is incomplete for unequal-motor body-yaw evidence. Calling both `receiverSolution` and `loads` would also solve roots twice; expose the actual same-pass solution diagnostics in a newly reviewed wrapper, or explicitly reserve and disclose the duplicate diagnostic work.

The source wake amplitude remains the model's fixed isolated-hover amplitude, **independent of source motor states**. Making source strength respond to motors would change the physical model. Constants to retain include mass `.027`, gravity `9.81`, arm `.028`, `kf=3.16e-10`, `km=7.94e-12`, density `1.225`, rotor radius `.023`, opposite-motor distance `2√2·.028`, wake `(10.11,.07668,−5.817)`, minimum downstream `2.5D`, downstream fade/cutoff `2/2.5 m`, radial fade/cutoff `2/3` half-widths, center radius `3 m`, near radius `.15 m`, and global tilt limit `20°`. Motor lag `.04 s` explains the existing state semantics; this static query does not advance it.

## Keep policy and invalidity evidence complete

I's `onReceiver` exposes `4N` source/rotor terms and codes in source-major storage, with same-pass ordinary/compensated sums. Codes distinguish self/outside, nonpositive downstream, cutoff, invalid near wake, policy near skip, radial cutoff, zero projection and positive support. I throws on the first invalid near-domain visit before emitting a completed receiver. Its uncomputed slots are not valid zeros.

A future GPU query must retain equivalent identity/coverage, policy classes, reached-validity, nonfinite/invalid statuses and deterministic failure/prefix evidence for **every required receiver/source/rotor**, including zero and invalid controls. Complete output coverage cannot be inferred from a checksum, counts or a few witnesses. Stream or tile evidence if necessary, preserving an indexed, reviewable record of visited versus unreached slots; do not silently drop offending sources. Reduction must consume the actual accepted term values in a frozen order. A different reduction tree, fused field/root pass or compact audit layout is new implementation work requiring review, not an unchanged reuse of `reduceMain`.

Keep original binary64 **R**, decoded packed-interface **Q**, actual GPU inflows/roots and final loads distinct. For variable motors, Q includes their represented nominal forces and induced-speed construction. Final-load acceptance cannot retrospectively erase an interface/class failure or convert the closed term campaign into a pass. If a genuinely new whole-observable contract changes which numerical quantities are primary gates, justify and freeze that contract before fresh data; retain old component failures as diagnostics with their original decisions.

## Costs and application bounds that must be settled first

A full query has `N(N−1)` ordered center checks, up to `4N(N−1)` rotor visits, and `4N` receiver requests. Naively extending the diagnostic retains `4N²` 104-byte records, plus copies/readback and reference evidence. The four-receiver memory/cost ledger therefore cannot license all-receiver feasibility. Batch size, storage layout, completion masks, byte ceilings, dispatch ceilings and external wall/process closure need a new static resource proof before implementation.

Charge input admission; quaternion/axis and all receiver-offset preparation; packing and motor-dependent induced-speed construction; allocations/copies/uploads; controls; every term and reduction visit; requested/actual root iterations; body/world assembly; readback/synchronization and caller-owned output construction. Distinguish cold allocation/compilation, reusable setup and per-query costs under a stated lifetime. Do not omit host preparation by timing only GPU dispatch. Evidence-only persistence/diagnostics must be explicitly and symmetrically accounted, not hidden in one comparator. I is an accuracy comparator, not automatically a serious optimized timing baseline.

The old host preparation bounds positions to ±128 m and quaternion norm error to `1e-10`; the all-receiver state owner must justify its coordinate extent, attitude, motor range, inflow range and update/copy lifetime. I itself does not enforce all those admissions. No new frame recentering, normalization, source policy or model bound should be smuggled into “A”. Whole-observable budgets need an application rationale: the old fixed-hover `1e-7 N` rotor allocation, binary64 equivalence gates, omission budgets and M8 mechanics tolerances are **different contracts**, not interchangeable acceptance targets. Motor-dependent sensitivity and GPU assembly rounding require explicit treatment. No physical tolerance or real-time benefit is established by source inspection.

## Is the question already answered?

- **M8:** `outputs/milestone-08-source/kernel.wgsl` is independent-vehicle f32 RK4 mechanics with motor lag; no wake field or receiver law. **M9:** `outputs/milestone-09-source/app.js` is a CPU-control/checking plus one-step GPU mechanics loop, airflow off. Neither answers a complete interacting load-query question.
- **DOT:** the retained audit `research/m31-zero-screen-2026-10-16/EXISTING-WORK-AUDIT.md` documents CPU binary64 spatial-pruning and exact-efficiency work. The inspected cached `research/campaign-exact-efficiency/src/exact-aero.cjs:116` has actual-motor `loads`, nominal yaw and a complete CPU field API; its source checkpoint is `c024dfc27b9e6b7cd5b39132164d1339cb95b12f`. This is relevant comparator/prior art, but not browser GPU evidence. Scaling stopped before N=500; do not extrapolate it to N=5,000.
- **Earlier M31:** full-flock binary64 reference and complete Node query studies already exist. Repeating that CPU baseline under a new seed is not a new question. Their recent static wrappers used prescribed hover loads. The browser receiver campaign qualified only a fixed-constant scalar component; wake-slice and coordinate campaigns tested selected-receiver terms/sums and ended negatively for their required gates. No complete all-receiver GPU roots/body-assembly result follows by combining these separate outcomes.

Thus the proposed missing question is technically distinguishable, but not yet a justified experiment or a new method. Its open prerequisites are an actual-motor application contract, one fixed architecture, a compatible full-query CPU comparator, justified output budgets, complete policy/invalidity retention and a feasible end-to-end resource ledger. Any later adoption needs a new campaign/case/invocation namespace and new authorization; no historical `fresh5000`, seed, lock, terminal job ID, omitted reduction or review budget may be reused as a launch identity.
