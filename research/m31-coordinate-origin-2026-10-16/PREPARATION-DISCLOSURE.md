# Preparation and source provenance

The host/reference and generator were prepared in an isolated author directory; the kernel/control/schema package was prepared by a separate author; the coordinator wrote browser/driver/config/supervision and integrated the immutable copies. An independent reviewer challenged the plan and inspected actual source. These are static activities, not empirical evidence. No source-commit seed, fresh position/angle/quaternion, numerical reference, new wake, shader compilation, GPUdispatch or scientific checker was evaluated before the freeze.

The host reference preserves the published independent-I arithmetic, adding saved vector fields and explicit structural validation. The actual generator source is authoritative. Its single commit-derived value is released only after independent publication verification.
