What changes between arms

A and B use exactly the same packed inputs. A forms an absolute rotor point before subtraction; B subtracts source and receiver first. C keeps B’s local-first structure and adds position residuals only. Its node buffer grows by 80,000 bytes for 5,000 nodes, from 160,000 to 240,000 bytes. That is a storage cost, not a measured speed difference.

Keep the limits visible

The two origins share one constructed geometry and attitude sample. The experiment is a coordinate diagnostic, not independent physical evidence. Returned-vector differences can accompany term changes without proving a universal compiler ordering rule. No fleet-wide accuracy, flight behavior, aerodynamic validity, portability or performance claim follows from this viewer.
