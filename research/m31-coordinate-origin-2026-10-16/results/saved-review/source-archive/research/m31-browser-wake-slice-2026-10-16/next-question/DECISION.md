# Next decision: a controlled coordinate-origin experiment

Adopt the independent challenge's three-arm, two-origin design for prospective source preparation after the current result checkpoint is published. Final source, protocol, budgets, independent static review and verified publication must precede any new numerical exposure. All old budgets stay closed.

The historical term failure is independently confirmed. Its cause remains unknown. The next question is whether the same newly constructed geometry becomes less accurate when represented farther from the coordinate origin, and whether changing subtraction order or storing a position residual prevents that loss. Published numerical methods motivate these interventions; we claim no new high/low technique.

Use one fresh 5,000-source dyadic construction, four receivers chosen by a frozen stratum/index rule without outcome ranking, and two origins: [0, 0, 0] and [64, −32, 16]. Compare exactly three implementations:

- A: current absolute binary32 positions and world-point arithmetic.
- B: subtract source and receiver positions first, using exactly A's input bytes.
- C: the same local-first approach with high/low position storage; other packed fields stay unchanged.

The source-minus-receiver sign convention is mandatory. Keep axes, rotor offsets, constants, wake/profile policies and epsilon unchanged. A and B share a decoded reference; C gets a reference with its own reconstructed positions. Preserve classification, finite-value, coverage and exact absolute-error gates. Only a passing cell can spend its separately reserved serial addition. Independent predeclared cells may proceed after numerical failure; no retries or extra cells are permitted.

The common 2^-20 coordinate denominator and bounded integer construction can make host translation exact and canonical positions easy for binary32. This is a deliberate diagnostic control, not a flight test or general coordinate sample. The generator must prove its integer bounds and retain zero exact translation residuals. Generic nonzero tilt/yaw, errors in packed axes/offsets/constants, and the original reference's own arithmetic drift between origins remain visible. Return reached center and rotor displacement components for every arm. WGSL source parentheses alone cannot guarantee a particular backend operation graph.

Prefer B only if it passes every valid cell and corrects a baseline failure. Consider C only if B is inadequate and C passes with its explicit storage and preparation cost. If the diagnostic is uninformative, all arms fail, or the baseline already meets every gate, preserve that outcome without a new microtest sweep. Operation counts do not establish elapsed-time performance.

A selected candidate earns one fresh, broader confirmation. Freeze that comparison's candidate, input family, seed rule, tolerances and stopping criteria before exposure. A failed confirmation does not authorize selecting the next-best arm. Neither diagnostic nor confirmation establishes physics, flight behavior or performance by itself.

The detailed implementation, byte, work and deadline ledger will be a separate preregistration. A commit-derived seed released only after source publication is acceptable if its rule is frozen and no commit/seed search occurs. It gives a fresh deterministic instance, not independent physical evidence. No old input or receiver selection is rerun. Eventual efficiency measurements must include required preparation, transfers, physics and steering under matched conditions.
