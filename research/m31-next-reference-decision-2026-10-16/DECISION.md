**Do not allocate or execute the proposed ENOLA power analysis yet.** Preserve the proposal and independent challenge. A conditional calculation is mathematically possible, but would not resolve the missing physical reference or compare swarm approximations. This is a priority decision, not a finding that the data are wrong or unusable.

The proposed consistency tests have an identification gap. Multiplying both CT and CP by the same positive factor k leaves `CP*T*D = 2*pi*CT*Q` and `J*n*D = V` unchanged. In contrast, at J=0 the proposed ideal-to-shaft-power ratio becomes sqrt(k) times its original value. Thus those tests cannot determine the normalization required to interpret that ratio physically. This is an algebraic statement under the stated definitions, not an empirical measurement or a novelty claim.
