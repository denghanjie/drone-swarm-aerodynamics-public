# Source and data provenance boundary

The new standard-library implementation and its synthetic tests were authored independently for the proposed finite ENOLA ideal-power comparison. They do not import an existing experiment, receiver model, analysis runner, supervision system or binary64 implementation. Importing `ideal_power.py` performs no formula evaluation and no filesystem, dataset, clock, network or process access. The test runner calls only synthetic values and mathematical constants.

The supplied planning memo describes the formula, proposed coefficient definitions, physical domain and fixed absolute 80-versus-120 precision agreement threshold. The derivation in `DERIVATION.md` is an explicit algebraic consequence of the ideal momentum relation and those stated definitions. No external primary physical reference was newly retrieved or verified during this implementation task. The planning memo attributes the conventional definitions to Deters, Ananda and Selig, AIAA 2014-2151; that attribution does not prove ENOLA uses them.

The intended future input is the previously retained experimental member `BBDD/T-Motor/MS1101/results/MS1101_exp_0deg.csv`. The proposal reports 3,155 bytes, 62 rows and SHA-256 `3a8d5fe5969a5b1578dcfd54fe78af487dcbbdc4c04cb02af6e698ce35c4f121`. Those are proposal-supplied data provenance claims, not rechecks made by this package. No CSV, workbook, dimensional channel or real operating-point number was read here. No real-row formula, ratio, compatibility screen or exclusion was calculated. The proposal's license, acquisition and measurement-provenance statements also remain attributed to that separate review.

The test pi expansion is a mathematical sanity-check constant and is not used by the implementation. All tested CT/J pairs are elementary constructed values. They are not measurements, selected dataset rows, fitted parameters or a validation sample. A deliberately huge pair checks that the prespecified absolute agreement screen can fail without changing its tolerance.

The coordinator's final desk-work decision is to defer the ENOLA numerical-data analysis. The proposed dimensional identities cannot identify a common multiplicative CT/CP normalization: multiplying both reported coefficients by the same factor leaves the torque/thrust identity unchanged. Definitions established for another specimen or setup do not fill this provenance gap. This implementation therefore remains unallocated source-only arithmetic preparation; it supplies no physical reference result and cannot rank swarm approximations.

Future use still requires a separately frozen protocol, exact dataset/source pins, the prespecified coefficient compatibility and physical-domain gates, and its own allocation. Keep every original measured row and reason for exclusion. This package introduces no scheduling, launch, supervision, timeout or result-publication infrastructure. Its numerical tests confer no experimental allocation.
