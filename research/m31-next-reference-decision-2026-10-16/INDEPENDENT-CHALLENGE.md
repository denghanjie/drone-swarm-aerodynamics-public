# ENOLA ideal-disk power proposal: desk challenge

**Decision: revise before allocation, then permit only a small assumption-conditioned descriptive analysis.** It can document the ideal-power factor and arithmetic sensitivity on one exposed measured-propeller slice. Its contribution to the accuracy–computation goal is modest: it can separate numerical error from a conditional shaft-power discrepancy, but it cannot rank swarm approximations, validate mutual aerodynamic coupling or establish a useful cost–accuracy trade-off. If the intended output is an independently established physical model-error score, defer until the coefficient and flow conventions are tied to this CSV.

No data formula, ratio, fit, score, timing or numerical experiment was evaluated in this review. The terminal GPU trial is unchanged and must not be retried.

## Material convention gap

The proposed identities are necessary consistency checks, not identification of the normalization. Replacing both reported CT and CP by a common positive factor k leaves `CP T D = 2π CT Q` unchanged, while the J identity does not involve either coefficient. Yet the ideal-power formula contains CT inside a square root, so the resulting ratio changes. A pass therefore cannot resolve the common scale. Unknown density cancels from the stated coefficient formula **only if the coefficients already use the stated conventional normalization**; eliminating density does not establish that premise.

The additionally requested retained ICAS text supplies relevant but incomplete evidence. In *Effect of popular additive manufacturing technologies on the performance and acoustics of UAV propellers*, Eq. (1), PDF p6/text lines283–293, the authors explicitly define `CQ=Q/(ρ∞n²D⁵)`, `CP=P/(ρ∞n³D⁵)`, `CT=T/(ρ∞n²D⁴)`, with n in rev/s, D the propeller diameter and ρ∞ the undisturbed density. However, §2.1 identifies the XOAR PJN9×7 specimen, §2.2 says the original COTS propeller broke and only printed versions are reported, and §2.3 describes an anechoic hover bench. This is not the MS1101 axial wind-tunnel member. No member-specific density, advance-ratio/sign mapping or explicit normalization linkage was found. The paper supports the plausibility of the authors' notation; it does not authenticate the CSV's absolute scale. Its calibration/repetition statements must not be transferred to the MS1101 rows.

**Necessary preregistration change:** call the checks “dimensional-channel consistency under an assumed coefficient convention.” Freeze the assumption explicitly. Failure still stops the comparison; passing permits only an assumption-conditioned result unless a retained member-specific source independently resolves normalization. Do not estimate density, rescale CT/CP or choose a diameter to force agreement. A seemingly plausible inferred density would not independently identify the scale.

## Other necessary changes

1. **State the physical branch and observable.** The formula is appropriate to a steady, normally propelling, positive-thrust actuator disk in an ideal axial stream, with V interpreted as undisturbed upstream speed and Q as shaft input torque. The README establishes an axial angle, not all flow-direction, sign, blockage, support/tare and torque conventions. Positive numerical columns are necessary but do not prove those physical premises. State them as assumptions and retain all domain exclusions. r is an ideal-power factor; it is not the usual propulsive efficiency `J CT/CP`. d combines idealization and any measurement/convention errors and is not an isolated induced-loss measurement. An apparent r>1 is an anomaly under these assumptions, not a universal energy-violation verdict.

2. **Make the format screen reproducible without adding a solver.** Determine the last printed unit from original numeric lexemes, including exponent notation and trailing zeros. Use one declared diameter and a fixed sensitivity rule; do not let individual rows pick adjusted diameters or densities. Specify how nonfinite and degenerate identities are reported. Treat a zero-containing residual envelope as “not refuted by the declared format envelope,” not proof that jointly consistent physical values exist. ±one printed unit is a conservative representation sensitivity choice, not a measured uncertainty bound. Flag positive nominal CP/CT values whose format intervals reach zero, since relative ratios can then be poorly resolved; do not silently trim them after seeing r.

3. **Compare numerical error in the same quantities.** Preserve binary64 versus high-precision differences for CPideal, r and d. The absolute CPideal error must not be directly interpreted as an error bound on d; division by CP changes the relevant scale. Construct high-precision inputs from the original decimal strings and π at the selected precision. State whether the binary64 comparison includes decimal-to-binary input representation as well as arithmetic. Agreement at80/120 digits within10⁻⁵⁰ is a prespecified implementation-consistency check, not a physical tolerance, certified rounding bound or protection against shared formula/convention errors. Retaining its failure without precision escalation is appropriate.

4. **Keep row summaries descriptive.** CT and CP may be derived from the same dimensional sensors; consistency is not independent experimental corroboration. Rows across RPM/velocity are operating points, not established independent repeats. Keep the planned full row table and exclusions, separate static/nonzero inflow and velocity labels, and avoid drawing an interpolated response curve or treating a pooled median as a population estimate. Report nominal r>1 counts plainly; no confidence interval or significance claim is justified by the available provenance.

5. **Narrow the accuracy–computation conclusion.** Measured thrust is an input, so this is not a thrust prediction test. Ideal disk power is not an asserted equality to shaft power in the existing M28 closure; nonzero discrepancy can reflect omitted swirl/profile and other losses rather than a falsification of that closure. Even a large discrepancy compared with floating-point error says only that arithmetic is not the dominant discrepancy for this particular conditional observable. It does not identify the error of wake approximations, command-to-thrust mapping, spatial superposition or 5,000-drone dynamics. Keep operation count as a source description, with no62-row timing extrapolation or speed claim.

A suitable preregistered claim is: **“Assuming the stated propeller coefficient and upstream-flow conventions, report the ideal-disk power factor computed from the measured operating points and its numerical representation sensitivity. These values are an exposed descriptive comparison, not member-specific physical validation or a cost–accuracy benchmark.”** If that narrow result is useful to the research ledger, the proposed one-shot CPU analysis is reasonable after these edits. Otherwise defer; another precision exercise alone does not advance the central physical-reference gap.

No added recorder, supervisor, acquisition or model fitting is needed. Keep the proposed10s/256MiB/1MiB limits as unallocated until the root explicitly adopts the revised study. Preserve the existing source hashes, row numbers, attribution and limitations.
