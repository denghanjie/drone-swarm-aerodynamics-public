Read the result carefully

Values below the replay come from the latest saved controller update, whose timestamp is displayed. They are held between updates and are not forces recomputed at the current endpoint. The original first second and continuation are joined only where all saved state words match. The final stopping endpoint has no new force evaluation.

Requested motion, commanded motor thrust, motor state and delivered aerodynamic force are different quantities. This trace compares them at recorded states. It can nominate a focused follow-up test, but cannot establish a causal cure, real-flight safety, aerodynamic accuracy or novelty.
