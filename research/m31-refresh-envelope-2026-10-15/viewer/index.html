THE ACTUAL TRADE-OFF

Fewer airflow calls did not settle the comparison.

The adaptive rule made 196 airflow calls and 615 bound checks per pass. Its median total time was 6.37 ms. A fixed 2.5 ms hold took 6.39 ms; their timing ranges overlap, so a meaningful speed advantage is unestablished.

The simple predictor made 201 airflow calls, took 3.57 ms, and had smaller lift and torque discrepancies on this one encounter. It is a baseline to challenge further, not a novel or universally safe solution.
