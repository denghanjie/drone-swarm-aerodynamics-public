# Public payload review

New evidence consists of synthetic prescribed poses, assumed model inflows/loads, numerical intervals, technical timings and original local research code/docs. No private chats, attachments, credentials, private filesystem paths, actual flight sites, hostnames or third-party raw telemetry are included. Bibliographic links reuse the verified source ledger; no third-party figures or papers are copied. Browser screenshots show this research page only. Existing vendor licensing is preserved.

Frozen sources and input bytes are unchanged. Raw experiment records are immutable; all comparison results, checker failure/correction history and limitations are retained. The first overwritten verifier JSON is not falsely described as preserved. Manifest hashes cover the reviewed delivery bytes. Text/path-pattern scanning is supplemented by provenance review, not treated as proof of universal secrecy.
