# Literature and novelty boundary

This is a lossless storage-format qualification using ordinary integer columns, offsets, run lists and bitmaps. None is claimed as a novel algorithm. It adds no aerodynamic model or published physical result. The motivating spatial-filter experiment retains its [verified literature record](../m31-native-tree-browser-probe-2026-10-17/LITERATURE.md).

No new research source was used to justify the representation's correctness or size. Those claims require the fixed saved-data comparisons and controls in this package. Passing them would be development-data engineering evidence, not a publishable scientific novelty claim.
