## The physically validated model gap needs measurements

The coupled source includes rigid-body dynamics, quaternion normalization, motor lag and delivered forces/moments. Its aerodynamic law remains a current-geometry/thrust surrogate with support/domain guards and a restricted descending source order. Existing project documentation explicitly notes the absence of wake memory and relative-airspeed response. More precise arithmetic and a correct RK implementation cannot validate those assumptions.

The existing physical-data admission review is explicit: all four inspected files passed structural screening, but `physicalLabelAdmitted` and `physicalEvidenceAdmitted` remained false. Unresolved issues include timestamp units/synchronization, acceleration frame and gravity, 32 g versus 34 g associations, actuator calibration/domain and lag, and firmware-force columns that are not measured external wake force. Long gaps remain. Do not use those fields as force truth or fit a wake model merely because a CSV has passed a finite-value check.

To avoid another sequence of provenance-only reports, give the physical track a concrete next deliverable: **one admitted calibrated source/receiver force comparison**, or an explicit blocked-data finding identifying the missing measurement. Prefer a small instrumented two-vehicle or rotor/receiver bench setup when no trustworthy existing force labels can be resolved. Retain an isolated-receiver/source-off baseline and source-on conditions at prospectively chosen geometry, synchronized pose and actuator observations, independently calibrated thrust/wrench measurement, mass/geometry/battery or RPM provenance, and repeated baseline controls. Keep the calibration dataset separate from held-out complete conditions, fix any model parameters and physical error/uncertainty budget before scoring, and retain negative results.

This is a proposed measurement design, not an instruction to run hardware or an assertion that suitable apparatus/data are available. Start in the stationary low-airspeed domain actually represented by the model. It can test a local wake-load prediction, not closed-loop flight or wake-memory claims. Do not apply the numerical 1e-8 tolerance as a physical accuracy target: the physical acceptance band must come from measurement uncertainty and an independently justified application error budget. If calibration/synchronization cannot be established, physical validation stays blocked while numerical engineering remains clearly labelled. No further numerical codec result removes that block.

## Novelty and decision discipline

Trees, conservative zero filters, typed columns, run lists, bitmaps and content-addressed storage are established techniques. This package's concrete contribution is a tested lossless representation for its saved witnesses. It is an engineering result, not a new aerodynamic model, a new generic spatial algorithm, or demonstrated publication-level novelty. Reuse the existing verified literature record; a priority claim would require a separate closest-prior-work audit, not citations guessed here.

A defensible future research contribution would need a specific additional result: for example, a new proved error/cost guarantee for the actual model, or a complete physically grounded coupled pipeline that improves a meaningful accuracy/resource tradeoff against relevant matched baselines. That would require admitted physical data or a carefully limited numerical claim, a complete workload and resource denominator, unchanged/standard competitor implementations, end-to-end cost accounting, and negative cases. None follows from the compression percentage.
