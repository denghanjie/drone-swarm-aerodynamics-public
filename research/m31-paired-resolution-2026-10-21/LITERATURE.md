**Bauersfeld, L.; Muller, K.; Ziegler, D.; Coletti, F.; Scaramuzza, D. _Robotics meets Fluid Dynamics: A Characterization of the Induced Airflow below a Quadrotor as a Turbulent Jet_. [arXiv:2403.13321v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13321v3), 12 December 2024.** Key `bauersfeld2024jetv3`, ledger A2. Relevant III-D, IV, VI, VII and VIII were read. The compensation example uses vehicle-specific inflow/power/throttle conversion. It does not establish our accumulator law or gains. The verified accepted RA-L manuscript is used; the journal DOI page was not verified. The smallest measured vehicle was 230 g, so this does not validate the present 27 g coupled model.

**Shi, G.; Hönig, W.; Shi, X.; Yue, Y.; Chung, S.-J. _Neural-Swarm2: Planning and Control of Heterogeneous Multirotor Swarms Using Learned Interactions_. IEEE Transactions on Robotics 38(2), 1063–1079, 2022; DOI 10.1109/TRO.2021.3098436.** [Verified institutional record](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/psxc9-vps89); relevant [arXiv:2012.05457v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.05457v2) sections III-A/B, IV-A, VI-A/B and VII-B were read. Key `shi2022neuralswarm2`, ledger A4. Interaction-aware control includes actuator delay; the reported learned interaction is vertical residual force. Code/training and all proofs/results were not reproduced or exhaustively audited. Its assumptions do not validate this controller or airflow closure.

These references supply aerodynamic/control context only. Neither is cited as integration-theory support for the present half-step comparison. No retained specifically verified PI/anti-windup source is asserted; integral clamping is not saturation-aware anti-windup. No novel integral-control claim is made.
