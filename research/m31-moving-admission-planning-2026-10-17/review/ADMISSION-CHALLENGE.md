## What upright geometry proves, and what it does not

For admitted identity or yaw-only attitudes, the prepared source axis is world-up and every receiver rotor point has its receiver center's z coordinate. Thus the exact scalar quantity in pair line15 is

`down = source.center.z - receiver.point[r].z = z_source - z_receiver`.

A positive scalar result has survived `down<=0` and therefore has strictly positive height difference. Every source-to-receiver positive edge consequently goes from earlier to later in descending-height order. Equal-height upright pairs cannot form positive edges; their stable-ID tie order is irrelevant. This source-derived implication guarantees the **ordering condition** for upright/yaw-only states, regardless of changing x/y/z centers or per-frame rank swaps. It does not guarantee separation from the near-rejection region, acceptable force/cap ranges, accurate finite arithmetic or a successful load certificate.

For tilted attitudes the source axis and receiver rotor height both matter. Positive `down` does not imply that the source center is higher in world-z. The fixed-order rejection can therefore appear even if the directed graph is acyclic and even if the tilt is small. Conversely, a tilted scene whose positive edges all follow the declared rank can pass the implemented ordering gate. No source theorem or result read here establishes an admission fraction for moving flight attitudes.

A positive directed cycle cannot satisfy strict rank increase around its edges, so every such cycle is rejected. The code does not distinguish a true cycle from an acyclic but differently ordered graph. If a future report attributes an `ordering` refusal to cycles, it must independently establish that distinction from the exact positive graph. Undecidable pair statuses leave the graph partially unknown; those edges must not be assumed zero. Removing a backward edge, changing IDs to rescue tied-height cases, swapping in a custom topological order or dropping a source would change the accepted problem and requires a separate prospective model/protocol.

## Three different meanings of moving Boids

**Kinematic three-dimensional motion.** A local steering rule can move positions/velocities in all three axes while its force-query attitude is held upright. Such snapshots can be genuinely nonrigid3D kinematics and enjoy the ordering implication above. They do not describe the attitude needed for horizontal acceleration in the existing multirotor dynamics. Rigid translation of a lattice, playback of a camera or merely assigning nonzero velocity words is even weaker: the current pair/load law uses velocity/rate only for finiteness, not wake history or relative-airspeed effects. Upright projection should be labeled a separate diagnostic, never counted as admission of the original flight states.

**Local3D steering with flight attitudes.** The relevant source is `outputs/milestone-31-source/sim.cjs`, not the older milestone10 steering file. Milestone31 lines9–25 compute separation/alignment/cohesion and route/avoidance steering in all three coordinates. Lines43–44 pass its desired velocity/height/yaw through the flight controller and integrate the full state. `outputs/step02c-source/physics.cjs:17–19,26–29` derives the desired thrust axis from acceleration, evolves quaternion/body angular rate and motor states, and has motor lag. Horizontal acceleration ordinarily requires tilt; positive commands and quaternion accuracy still require admission rather than assumption. Controller command saturation can request zero (`physics.cjs:16`), while the exact adapter requires the **state's motor force**, not the command, to be positive. No instantaneous equality between command and motor-state thrust is justified.

Milestone31 initialization (`sim.cjs:7`) starts with yaw-only attitudes and3D velocity/position variation; an initial-frame success is therefore a weak test of later attitude admission. The older `outputs/milestone-10-source/boids.cjs:7–18` uses3D distance for neighbor detection but returns horizontal steering only. It cannot establish a local3D flight-state claim and is included in the reading inventory to prevent that substitution.

**Airflow feedback into flight.** Evaluating certified steady loads on saved airflow-off states is a diagnostic of the hypothetical model along that trajectory. It does not change the trajectory and proves no feedback stability, collision behavior or integration accuracy. The old motion-feedback adapter (`research/m31-motion-feedback-2026-10-16/source/adapter.cjs:6–19`) adds rotor-force differences to translational and rotational derivatives, checks a different typed kernel's admission/descending eligibility, and projects quaternion norms at *each* RK stage. Its experiment (`.../source/experiment.cjs:9–17`) explicitly uses local3D controls and states limitations including nominal yaw torque, stage normalization and absent wake history. These are source facts, not evidence that the new exact backend has already transferred successfully.

There are additional integration questions. The ordinary physics integrator normalizes only the final RK4 quaternion (`physics.cjs:19`); its intermediate raw stages are not thereby guaranteed to meet the exact adapter tolerance. Borrowing stage projection changes the integrator and must be explicit. The current exact certificate emits world force/moment using offset cross axis (`loads.cjs:60–68`); the physics equations use body torque and include rotor drag yaw torque (`physics.cjs:15,18`). A new feedback adapter must specify frame conversion, nominal-force subtraction, the yaw term and motor-state/command meaning to avoid double counting. No such new exact adapter was inspected or established here.

Locality also needs a declared meaning. The Boids radius is1.8 in `sim.cjs:4`; aerodynamic support is governed by the exact pair constants and ordered guards, not that neighbor list. A fixed64-drone group is a closed subsystem. It is not a justified extraction from a5000-drone population unless external source influence and guard obligations are separately discharged. Selecting only an ego agent's steering neighbors can omit aerodynamic sources.

## A falsifiable successor admission gate

The parent proposed the following concrete **successor design** after this source review began; it is not an allocated run. Before any generation, the parent would freeze and publish the complete generator/controller/integrator/geometry dependency closure, raw-state capture schema, exact source backend, independent checker, resource limits and refusal accounting. Several generator dependencies remain unread at this review's cap, so this report alone is not a source-execution approval.

1. Freeze two whole64-drone groups with literal seeds26171001 and26171002, the existing milestone31 simulator in airflow-off local-Boids mode, its unchanged flight controller/integrator, and a declared factory variant and wall-priority setting. Use the original17-component state, fixed `dt=0.0025`, duration10seconds, and full-state captures at `t=0,5,10`: exactly six planned group snapshots and at most twelve once-only method calls (hierarchy and filtered for each reached snapshot). The source accepts this timestep (`sim.cjs:34–37`); this is configuration compatibility, not a resource-feasibility measurement. A completed trajectory's0.2second pose stream has51 frames including both endpoints; two completed trajectories have102 such pose frames.
2. Use the existing progress callback, not an integrator hook change. `sim.cjs:41` passes the full state arrays at the initial sample, each five-second sample and a stop; lines47–49 preserve stop/final sampling. Copy/serialize the full17 words at capture, preserving signed zeros and the actual velocity, quaternion, angular-rate and motor fields. The separate `frameSink` records only time plus position and quaternion (`sim.cjs:41`); it cannot reconstruct the omitted fields. Extra off-schedule terminal states/poses are retained as failure evidence and never substituted for a scheduled snapshot. Freeze/hash all captured raw inputs before running either exact method, so model admission cannot influence trajectory generation or the seed/frame selection.
3. Generate each trajectory once. Every scheduled slot remains in the denominator. Early stops retain their reason and terminal state, and later scheduled slots become `not-reached-after-generator-failure`; do not manufacture51 poses or infer a completed horizon. All available scheduled snapshots are assessed even if an earlier one is rejected, unless a frozen hard resource/safety rule terminates the allocation; then every unattempted slot is marked accordingly. No reseeding, attitude flattening, force clipping, frame selection or source-subset selection. On each reached snapshot independently validate preparation, every logical pair, the exact positive graph/order and full load/certificate output. Keep all refusal classes and canonical witnesses. A global-guard fallback is a separate counter. Diagnose an ordering refusal as acyclic-incompatible or cyclic only if all required edges are resolved, and retain unknown-graph cases.

4. Primary admission gate: all six slots must exist, satisfy unchanged preparation/pair/order gates and achieve all four load tolerances with independent validation. No successful-frame-only throughput statistic. If any slot fails, the answer is that this unchanged model has not admitted the **whole frozen trajectory schedule**; the refusal pattern is the result, not an excuse to weaken the test.
5. Freeze nonvacuity checks before exposure. Proposed checks per seed: retain positive source coupling at two distinct scheduled times; exhibit a post-initial tilt exceeding0.1degree; and exhibit changes exceeding1mm in relative pair displacement in each of x,y,z, thereby excluding rigid translation. Across the schedule require at least one independently verified interaction-branch or positive-edge change. These proposed thresholds are frozen choices for the next protocol, not observed properties of the two seeds. Failure of these checks is “uninformative for changing flight-state transfer,” even if every numerical call certifies. Do not add a case to manufacture the missing witness. Source inspection must also confirm that the actual steering path includes local social terms and three-dimensional avoidance, rather than relying on visual movement alone.
6. Report pointwise results only. Six snapshots do not prove admission between samples or at RK substages, and cannot authorize feeding the loads back into derivatives. If admission and nonvacuity both pass, a later protocol may test all required integration-stage states and an explicit force/moment adapter with convergence/feedback checks. Browser transport timing remains a separate engineering question.

The existing static cost checker cannot simply be applied unchanged: it requires declared scenes to be admitted and treats unresolved outcomes as unqualified. The successor must separate **pipeline verification** from **model admission**. A correctly reproduced raw/preparation/pair/order rejection can be verified evidence while failing the admission gate. A fixed96 `unresolved` with a decisive fixed192 result is a numerical refusal/status gap, not proof of model-domain rejection or a certified success. Checking its exact cause may need a separately reviewed independent fixed96 trace; the present higher-precision oracle alone does not establish that cause. Tolerance failure, oracle uncertainty, resource termination and unavailable generator slots each need their own retained category. “All refusals checked” must not erase these distinctions. No old control suite is reopened; the new refusal/control contract must be prospectively reviewed.

This parent schedule is a more direct test of actual flight-state applicability than another upright speed benchmark. The two methods run once each from identical captured words to check unchanged results; there is no speed-benefit claim, warmup/median selection or reason to condition inclusion on faster frames. The longer flight horizon may expose more attitude/domain changes, but source inspection cannot establish whether the chosen trajectories finish or meet the nonvacuity witnesses. All six snapshots are still sparse samples of a continuous trajectory.

The strict primary gate is intentionally not a universal claim: a passing set supports those seeds, groups, times and source versions only. If the product instead needs a specified refusal rate, that alternative criterion must be written before exposure and report the same full denominator. No snapshot success should be described as a proof of all continuous-time flight states.

## Decision risks and missing evidence

Source-derived risks are near/cap and preparation refusals, tilted edges violating the fixed rank, motor-state domain changes, quaternion-stage mismatch and resource cost of repeated exact all-pair/certificate work. Their incidence is unknown. The new package's prospective scope supplies the inherited observation that the published filter had a finite static upright benefit but still took seconds at180 drones; I did not reread or recompute those result artifacts and make no new timing estimate.

This review has not read the milestone31 factory, grid, geometry or wall-filter implementations, current browser transport, the full old typed coupling kernel, result datasets or any new physical validation. The twelve-file cap has been reached. Their source closure and any proposed trajectory capture/refusal ledger need fresh authorized review before the successor gate. No evidence here establishes that two chosen flight trajectories will admit, that they generate the required dynamic interaction witnesses, or that a general tilted/cyclic solver/reference exists.

If the admission gate fails because of rank incompatibility, classify the graph before choosing between a general DAG ordering extension and genuinely cyclic-model work. If it fails on near/cap/physical-domain assumptions, prioritize those model questions. If it passes but is nonvacuous and still too costly, batching/reuse/approximation can be considered under a new accuracy protocol. Another successful small upright speed comparison would not resolve any of these moving-state obstacles and would add little to the5000-drone objective.
