## Source-derived facts that change the priority

Locations below are relative to `research/m31-filter-cost-2026-10-17/`; exact file identities and this review's reading scopes are in PRIORITY-READING.json.

1. **The present frozen harness cannot simply be relabelled a 5,000-drone test.** source/spec.json has `maxDrones: 180`; source/methods.cjs:6 enforces that cap. The 17-word input carries position, quaternion and rotor forces. source/adapter.cjs:11–14 constructs geometry from position and quaternion, validates preparation, and rejects nonfinite state or nonpositive rotor force. Other state words are checked for finiteness but do not enter this static pair geometry. A movie of changing positions can be a valid kinematic subproblem, while saying nothing about force-driven flight integration.
2. **Admission is more restrictive than “some 3D points are drawable.”** source/pair-prepared.cjs:14–18 has ordered guards, finite-support branches and a near-interaction rejection. source/methods.cjs:68–76 refuses rejected/unresolved pairs, then ranks drones by descending center z and ID and rejects a positive source edge that does not precede its receiver. A rejected `ordering` witness is not itself proof of a directed cycle: an otherwise acyclic graph can violate this particular ranking. Distinguish these cases before proposing a general cyclic solver.
3. **Upright is an experimental restriction, not a universal preparation test.** The adapter checks quaternion/axis/offset validity rather than demanding identity orientation. Some tilted states might satisfy every admission and certificate obligation; this source inspection does not establish a generally tilted domain. Conversely, moving local 3D Boids coordinates with forced upright quaternions test a kinematic subgoal, not the flight-attitude states that a force-coupled simulator would produce. MODEL.md:7–11 explicitly calls the coupling hypothetical and disclaims general tilted/cyclic or physical validation.
4. **There is concrete shared exact work to examine before adding approximate positive coefficients.** source/methods.cjs:8–17 already prepares each drone's exact/fast representations inside each call, and :51–61 issues four rotor queries per receiver. source/pair-prepared.cjs:14 recomputes center displacement and its squared distance in each residual scalar query; those quantities are independent of rotor. At :25, source nominal force and its beta normalization depend only on the source and frozen constants, while nu depends on receiver rotor force and frozen constants. They are computed inside each positive-query path. These are source-level reuse candidates, not measured bottlenecks. A cache must preserve exact operand identity, operation order where relevant, branch priority and exceptional outcomes.
5. **The existing solver does not close a flight loop.** source/loads.cjs:28–37 makes a fixed native iterative proposal; :40–73 certifies loads and all four error categories for the supplied state. It does not integrate those forces back into motion. Replacing the proposal, widening the admitted graph, or carrying a previous solution forward changes obligations even if the final certificate remains an acceptance gate. The certificate's presence is not permission to silently drop its premises.

I did not read the historical Boids implementation in this role; implementation feasibility and the exact state mapping belong to the separate reviewer. No conclusion here assumes that mapping is already faithful.

“Batching” must mean named shared expressions or a different certified traversal, not merely issuing the same scalar work in a loop or worker. Moving the current work off the UI thread may improve responsiveness, but does not establish lower total computation or a sufficient update cadence. A future end-to-end cost comparison must include the chosen preparation, cache maintenance, transport, recomputation, fallback and output obligations consistently.

## Smallest useful falsifiable next decision

Proposed decision question: **Can the unchanged numerical model serve as the oracle for every scheduled frame of one fixed, nontrivial local-3D-Boids state sequence under an explicitly stated state-to-flight mapping?** This is a finite admission/semantic question, not a new speed-benefit claim.

The minimum successor protocol should freeze one generator/controller version, one independently selected seed, one group size within the reviewed resource envelope, and a finite list of checkpoints including the initial state. Select size and checkpoints before seeing admission results; do not choose the smallest successful group afterwards. State whether the contract is merely upright kinematics or actual attitude/force states. Freeze the exact raw-word identity and transport rule, handling of every 17-word field, all resource caps, the complete independent reference obligation and the meaning of an incomplete run. These details are not supplied or generated in this desk review.

Use one declared candidate method and its independent numerical reference. A second hierarchy-versus-filter timing competition, warmup pairs and repeated speed measurements are unnecessary for this decision. Every scheduled frame belongs in the denominator. Count preparation rejection, pair rejection, ordering failure, unresolved result, tolerance failure, resource stop and hard input exception separately. Preserve the first witness and required diagnostic evidence; do not treat it as a count of all possible causes. Missing frames after a bounded stop make the finite sequence result incomplete, not successful.

Predeclare a nontriviality requirement: relative geometry changes, rather than only rigid translation or replaying one frozen state, and the scheduled states exercise actual positive aerodynamic coupling. Require an explicit motion/interaction diagnostic chosen before exposure; if it is absent, label the result inconclusive for coupling transfer. All-zero spacing, airborne graphics with airflow off, fixed attitudes substituted for an actual controller, and selecting only certifiable frames cannot satisfy a broader flight-state claim.

Decision consequences should be prospective:

- If a required state has a valid refusal or cannot meet its error limits, the unchanged-model-oracle claim is falsified for that sequence. Retain the result and choose a documented narrower kinematic scope, a state-mapping correction supported by source, or an admission/model-theory project. Do not “repair” the experiment by increasing spacing or deleting hard frames.
- If all frames qualify and the nontriviality requirement holds, admit only that finite sequence as an optimization/reference target. Then make a source-reviewed within-frame sharing design with explicit dependency keys, invalidation and independent equivalence controls. A later cost test should test whether that architecture addresses an application-derived absolute budget at meaningful support density; a favourable percentage at tiny n alone is insufficient.
- If Chrome-specific state preparation or transfer is the identified gap, execute the exact same frozen inputs across runtimes in that future protocol and make bit identity/qualified outputs the primary outcome. A mismatch is an adapter/runtime investigation. A match is portability evidence, not numerical-model validation or 5,000-drone feasibility.

A source-derived impossibility for the intended mapping can itself justify stopping before any new numerical allocation. If a proposed sequence would artificially preserve exactly the static restrictions that already guarantee easy admission, the parent should document that limited educational/engineering purpose or decline the experiment.

## What the verified literature does—and does not—supply

This stage reuses the pinned prior literature review and bibliography; I did not retrieve or reread private paper assets. The prior reviewed versions are Shewchuk's CMU-CS-96-140R long report for the 1997 article, BBP2001's HAL v1 author manuscript, and MP2007's HAL v2 manuscript; the latter's publisher-layout bytes were not verified. Their prior correction searches were bounded, not blanket clearance.

Shewchuk §3 supports adaptive error tests with exact fallback and warns that additional stages impose overhead (DOI 10.1007/PL00009321). Brönnimann/Burnikel/Pion §2 and §§3.1–3.2 support certified “unknown” outcomes, inclusion arithmetic and dependency/width limitations (DOI 10.1016/S0166-218X(00)00231-6). Melquiond/Pion §§2.2–3.4 support expression-level error and exceptional-range obligations (DOI 10.1051/ita:2007005). These principles support preserving the exact oracle while redesigning work. They do not prove this aerodynamic law, broad moving-state admission, temporal-cache certificates, positive approximation, general scalability or novelty. The prior MPFR and ECMAScript documentation clarifies arithmetic semantics only; adopting a new backend does not discharge model or integration obligations.

Necessary future literature work is conditional and separate from this closed desk allocation:

- Before physical or general-flight claims: primary validated multi-rotor interference models and measurements covering the intended separation, orientation, motion, uncertainty and unsteady regime. Compare their assumptions to this hypothetical coupled law, not only their visual resemblance.
- If ordering/cycles are the obstruction: existence, uniqueness and certified solution/error results for the actual coupled nonlinear equations and relevant nonnegative interaction graph. First determine whether the obstacle is the chosen z ordering, a genuine cycle, or a different pair refusal; they require different work.
- Before temporal reuse beyond exact identity: certified kinetic/spatial data structures and invalidation bounds under the actual state changes; prior numerical-filter papers alone are insufficient.
- Before positive approximation/compressed interactions: error-controlled approximation for the actual finite-support, anisotropic kernel and propagation through coupled loads and moment tolerances. Generic fast-summation names or published timings are not a transferable error proof.

Any novelty claim additionally needs a targeted comparison against relevant exact reuse/batching, kinetic filtering and aerodynamic solvers. No current evidence warrants calling the next integration or cache optimization a new general algorithm.
