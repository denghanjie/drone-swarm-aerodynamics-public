## Question and motivation

Before optimizing mutual aerodynamics, what does the current source field actually conserve, discard and respond to? We will measure three narrowly separated quantities: source-state sensitivity in an extracted current field function, the squared-profile integral removed by finite support, and one already-published front/back rotor comparison. The source profile is `u=Uc/(1+a*(r/b)^2)^2`, where `a=sqrt(2)-1`, `Uc=C*UH/(s/D-origin)`, `b=S*D*(s/D-origin)`.
