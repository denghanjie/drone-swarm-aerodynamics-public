Read the colors as evidence

Contribution colors show valid stored term values for the chosen stage and receiver–rotor pair. Error colors show differences of those stored values. Scales use the current slice’s maximum, so color intensity is not a numerical pass/fail signal. Status colors separate unwritten, invalid, early-zero, evaluated-zero and positive records.

What this slice can establish

These four receivers were chosen from exposed saved data. The diagnostic checked prepared inputs, policy classes and materialized terms on one environment. Its planned serial reduction was skipped after a term-error gate failed. It does not establish fleet-wide accuracy, cross-device portability, speedup, coupled-flight behavior, or aerodynamic validity.

Original evidence and SHA-256 checks

Every linked file used here is checked against its published byte length and SHA-256 before use. Hashes establish consistency with the published manifest, not a cryptographic signature. The viewer does not execute the host reference, shader, or review checker.
