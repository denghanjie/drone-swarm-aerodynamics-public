The dyadic conversion is correct by source inspection: binary64 coefficients use units `2^-1074`, binary32 coefficients use `2^-149`, normal mantissas include their hidden bit and shift by exponent−1, subnormals use their stored fraction, signed zero becomes integer zero, and binary32 coefficients shift 925 bits into common units.
