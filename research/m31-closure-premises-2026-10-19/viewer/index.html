What the source check changes

The older printed formula and the later formula are not interchangeable under the same definitions. Their zero crossings differ, so one multiplier cannot make them identical. We have documented the discrepancy without calling it a proved typo or changing the saved model.

The original model was fitted using a probe that measures airflow speed regardless of direction. Treating that fit as downward velocity is an additional assumption. The measurement files describe both components as sharing one speed unit, but their original conversion steps are unavailable.

A more targeted next test

Imagine a hollow sleeve around the missing core. Comparing flow through its inner wall, outer wall, top and bottom can avoid guessing what happens at the axis. This still requires assumptions about rotational symmetry, swirling flow, measurement geometry and error. A fixed experimental plan must settle those before calculation.
