# Scope of this public checkpoint

This is an intentionally partial public research checkpoint. Internal review records and inventory metadata are omitted. No sanitized derivatives of those omitted records are supplied. Some unchanged scientific records refer to supporting artifacts that are unavailable in this public copy.

The public files are evidence for inspection, not a complete frozen execution bundle. They do not support complete fresh-checkout reproduction or a rerun of the recorded attempt. Missing supporting artifacts must not be reconstructed from assumptions or treated as a passed verification.

The one-shot attempt ended in a schedule-integrity rejection before any numerical child ran. No exact-equivalence result, speedup measurement, new trajectory, or physical validation was produced. The original failure receipts and their before-write accounting qualifications are preserved. Publication grants no retry or new execution release.

Read [the current status](../CURRENT-STATUS.md), [terminal report](../postrun-report/MILESTONE.md), and [static diagnosis](../postrun-diagnosis/REPORT.md) for the outcome and limitations. The [demonstration](../postrun-demo/index.html) displays original failure records alongside historical trajectories; browser rendering remains unverified.

Selected existing files are published without rewriting their contents. The complete local evidence remains unchanged. Historical prospective wording and links to omitted supporting artifacts do not imply that the omitted materials are publicly available or that the failed experiment became qualified.
