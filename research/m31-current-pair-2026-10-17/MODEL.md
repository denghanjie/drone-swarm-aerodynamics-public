# Adopted pair target and conditional enclosure argument

This package operationalizes a limited portion of the adopted M-current track. It does not certify the old native query. The previous exact-model vs native-program distinction remains; no historical simulation or query result is changed.

The exact constants are fixed by the unique binary64 encoding rule in PROTOCOL.md and source/spec.json, including exact RN-even square roots. Their emitted bit table is checked against that rule before the output can qualify. The independent checker evaluates square-root rounding by exact rational midpoint comparisons, not an assumed one-ulp library pad. Native output is only a proposal: mismatching constant bits fail the checkpoint. These uniquely determined prospective encodings satisfy this experiment's constant gate; later users must use those encodings or requalify a new target. This is an explicit operational adoption of an encoding algorithm, not a result-dependent constant selection.

The raw-state adapter is the documented binary64 cross/rotation expression graph. Every prepared field bit must agree with an independent IEEE implementation of that graph. Rounded positions, axes, offsets and points become the mathematical model inputs. No conclusion about ideal quaternion rotation error, true vehicle attitude or legacy pair rounding follows. Exact squared norm predicates and exact orthogonality predicates validate declared prepared geometry. Positive nominal totals and weights are exact mathematical sums/ratios; no rounded total is substituted. This pair interface needs beta and nu; complete weight/candidate aggregation remains future work.

Exact rational arithmetic decoded from supplied dyadics determines all geometric guards and their order, retaining actual near-unit axes. The radial residual is d-(d dot axis)*axis; radial squared is the sum of its exact squared components. Comparing its square against positive threshold squares exactly decides the norm guards and support cutoffs. No orthogonality identity is assumed. The source center minus the already rounded receiver rotor point is the displacement. Center guard precedes neighbor exclusion; down/radial guards precede near-field rejection; unsupported near-field remains rejected even for zero projection. After exact support and positive projection, mathematical K is positive. Earlier zero exits need not evaluate a normalizer or source force scaling.

The positive rational profile is therefore exactly computable before the radial fade and normalization square roots.
