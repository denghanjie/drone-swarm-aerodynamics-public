What this means

Before building a faster swarm calculation, we need to know which individual interactions are valid, zero, or too uncertain to certify. This checkpoint connects raw drone state to that decision and checks the returned airflow ranges.

What it does not establish

This is a numerical test of the declared mathematical model. Physical accuracy, complete coupled loads, browser calculation speed and novelty remain open. The old native program and this exact-real model are separate targets.

Scope: all 322 declared cases remain in the record, including invalid and unresolved cases. No new flight or performance benchmark. The old timed queries already excluded dense classification tensors; that work cannot be credited as a future speedup.
