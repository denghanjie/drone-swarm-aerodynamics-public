# Saved-evidence validator qualification — prospective protocol

This is a new diagnostic allocation on an already exposed corpus. The previous moving-state attempt failed and remains permanently closed. Its complete result is public at 5a5efeef2d216188fa93a8034c4573d7327c5411; its result manifest was independently retrieved and all 128 local/committed file pins verified before this protocol was written. No new qualification driver or mutation has been executed at protocol creation.

## Research question

Can the same validator reject a false zero-node claim through its intended semantic check when the mutation's unrelated filter counters are kept consistent, while retaining its previous counter-interception behavior and all other fixed regression checks?

The old experiment captured two 64-drone local 3D Boids flights and all six scheduled full states. All six received individual positive numerical checks, but one of 20 negative controls failed to reach its required error family. The old corruption replaced a mixed leaf, discarding a fast-zero list without maintaining two filter counters; the wrapper rejected that mismatch before reaching its semantic zero-node obligation. That is the observed motivation for this prospective repair. This study is knowingly retrospective qualification on exposed records, not new evidence of generalization.

## Decision rule

A qualified result requires all of the following: every source/input/runtime binding intact; all ten saved baseline outcomes reproduced with their required individual validation, status and branch; complete six-state denominator and original captures checked; all 21 negative controls rejected by their specifically intended families with their fixed witnesses; all requested new references saved within budget; successful process closure and post-execution identity rechecks. A missing record, unexpected baseline, unrelated early rejection, absent witness, cap breach or exception fails this allocation. Preserve failure and never retry this allocation.

A pass means only that the repaired mutation harness and unchanged validator pass the prescribed tests on this exposed corpus. It does not retroactively change the failed original attempt, constitute fresh moving-state replication, authorize scientific browser transfer, prove universal validator correctness, or establish physical validity, aerodynamic feedback, novel mathematics, real-time operation or 5,000-drone scalability. Overall research remains open. Any later transfer requires a new prospective protocol.
