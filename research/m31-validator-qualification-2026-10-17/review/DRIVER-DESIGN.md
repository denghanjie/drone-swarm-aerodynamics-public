## Twenty-one controls and the sole semantic repair

The new explicit recipe table contains the previous nineteen successful recipes unchanged, the corrected `false-zero-active-leaf` construction and a separate `false-zero-counter-interception` regression. All old expected failure families are retained. The regression has its own expected family `filter-work-counter-reconstruction`; it does not alter the prior failed control's verdict.

For the two node controls, the witness is fixed exactly: scene positive, query0, terminal0, entry0, receiver0, rotor0, source1, node0, one removed fast-zero record. Witness construction occurs once before the node control is completed; later positive leaves create no extra gate. A different or absent witness fails rather than selecting a substitute.

Both controls start from independent deep copies of the original saved positive method. Each replaces that mixed leaf with the same false neighbor-zero node. The corrected control additionally decreases only `work.filterZeros` by the removed list length and increases only `work.exactScalarCalls` by that length. All base hierarchy work counters and every other field remain unchanged. It calls the complete frozen filter wrapper and must fail with `node-zero-rule` or `node-zero-oracle-member`. A direct inner-helper test cannot satisfy it.

The regression performs the original replacement and leaves its counters untouched; the same complete wrapper must intercept it with `filter-work-counter-reconstruction`. Thus the node semantic obligation and malformed-counter interception are separate named prospective controls with distinct expected layers. There is no extra hidden direct-semantic invocation. Exactly21 negative control operations are expected, in addition to ten baseline validations; a failure to find any other preregistered original witness also fails without fallback. Method copies are disposable and only one is evaluated at a time.
