The original moving-state source, calculation records, failed checker and public result identity are historical inputs. New source and qualification results form a separate chain. A positive result can qualify the corrected mutation harness on this exposed corpus. It cannot convert the old failed checkpoint into a pass, establish replication on fresh states, support transfer to Chrome, or establish flight/physics/performance/novelty claims.

Background numerical-filter citations are already verified in the prior moving-state RESULT-CLAIM-LEDGER.md and the filter-design bibliography. No new literature evidence is needed to diagnose this software test-construction interaction. Those sources do not prove that this repair works; only the newly registered experiment can answer the fixed qualification question. No new paper retrieval or absence-of-errata claim is made here.
