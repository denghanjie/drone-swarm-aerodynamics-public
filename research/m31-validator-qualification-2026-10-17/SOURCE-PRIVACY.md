# Source and input publication scope

Only synthetic simulation states, algorithm source, public provenance, scoped review documents and a display of saved research evidence are included. Old input records are already public at a verified exact commit. New machine paths use generic templates or environment variables; actual local runtime/log paths remain private. No original user attachments, private paper PDFs, credentials or third-party personal information belong in this package. Inspect final staged bytes and screenshots before publication. Source-only scans are not empirical validation.
