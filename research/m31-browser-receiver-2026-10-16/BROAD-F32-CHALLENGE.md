# Static challenge: a distinct browser f32 wake/receiver question

2026-10-16. Static source/protocol review only. No scientific module was imported or executed, and no browser, case generator, field, root solver or benchmark was run. The parent's report that the sole E/L campaign passed its runtime numerical gates but missed both usefulness criteria on both fresh sizes is supplied context; its independent review remains pending.

**Recommendation:** after closing that review, consider a separately frozen, small-first f32 feasibility campaign. Ask: *On one declared browser/adapter and one bounded input domain, can an f32 implementation preserve the static model's domain decisions and deliver receiver loads within prospectively chosen error limits, with attributable errors?* This is a new representation/arithmetic question. It is not another exact-zero CPU optimization, an R8 retry, a 5,000-drone throughput claim, or an integrated-flight experiment.

## What to port first

Keep M28's static additive sum-rotor model, fixed nominal hover motors and nominal yaw zero. Each source drone supplies **one centered hover wake**; the receiver samples that wake at its **four rotor positions**. Do not introduce four separate source wakes. Preserve the strict 3 m source-center neighborhood, self exclusion, global tilt rejection, near-domain exception, downstream/radial fades and cutoffs, and unnormalized quaternion rotation algebra.

Start with exhaustive source-ID traversal and no E/L center-envelope screening, grid, positive omission, dynamics or controller. A receiver-owned invocation can accumulate four rotor inflows in ascending source order, solve four receiver roots and assemble its row without force atomics or cross-workgroup reduction. Freeze the exact work decomposition and arithmetic spelling before exposure. This removes reduction scheduling and spatial indexing as initial confounders; it does not establish an efficient large-N algorithm. Shader compilers can still use permitted fused operations, so source order is not a cross-adapter bitwise guarantee.

M8's five-vec4 packing, dispatch and synchronized readback are useful engineering examples. Its shader performs independent RK4 mechanics, including quaternion normalization, and cannot serve as a wake/receiver implementation or precision qualification. Do not carry its normalization into this static model.

| 1. Scalar receiver and geometry branches | Fixed scalar inflow probes, including zero and the declared maximum; tiny geometries on both sides of every strict boundary, at equality where constructible, plus invalid-input/expected-rejection cases | Root bracket/range/residual/load gates and all required rejection/classification gates pass. Iteration exhaustion or stagnation without the prescribed accuracy certificate is failure; stopping alone is not a certificate. |
| 2. Complete small static queries | Predeclared interacting and sparse cases at two small sizes; a proposed first envelope is N=32 and N=128. | 3. Bounded cost characterization, only if selected in advance | Same approved complete-query contract, fixed warmups/repeats/order, returned and consumed output, all timing samples | All accuracy gates have passed first. Do not put N=5,000 into the minimum campaign. A small direct-kernel pass would justify designing a separate scale/cost campaign; it cannot predict that campaign's result. Include genuine interaction in the small cases so a zero-wake workload cannot qualify the arithmetic path. Treat existing fixtures and every previously exposed seed as regressions, not fresh holdouts. Freeze new seeds/shapes and the complete boundary list without evaluating them, with no replacement after exposure.

## Separate representation, field arithmetic, roots and loads

Schedule and budget these controls explicitly for each admitted complete-query case:

1. **Original reference:** independent binary64 field, receiver and load assembly on the original inputs and original model constants.
2. **Represented reference:** independent binary64 evaluation on the exact decoded f32 inputs and the chosen represented constants. Its difference from (1) measures input/parameter quantization, including domain changes. If input and constant effects need separate attribution, add and budget that control prospectively; otherwise label them combined.
3. **GPU calculation:** save its inflows, roots/rotor forces and assembled outputs. Compare its inflows and classifications with (2) to measure field arithmetic. For fixed tiny fixtures, save per-source terms and sequential partial sums in the same scheduled diagnostic pass; a binary64 sum of those saved GPU terms separates accumulation error from per-term arithmetic. Instrumented diagnostic latency is not kernel timing.
4. **Root isolation:** scheduled independent binary64 receiver solutions on the **actual returned GPU inflows**, using represented receiver parameters. Compare GPU roots/forces with this control, not only with roots at the reference inflows. Evaluate residuals independently in binary64 at the returned GPU root. Save brackets, widths and iteration counts from these scheduled solutions.
5. **Assembly isolation:** binary64 load assembly from the **actual returned GPU rotor forces** and decoded f32 orientations. Compare with GPU assembly. Also retain total original-reference versus GPU load errors; passing separate components does not excuse failing the total budget.

Store original and packed input bytes, constant bit patterns, references and GPU results before subsequent comparisons. Saved-only review must not regenerate a field or solve a new root. Every diagnostic/reference pass is a charged scientific call, including expected rejects and repeated scalar batches.

Specify whether derived constants are uploaded or recomputed, and count their rounding in the correct layer. Separately rounded redundant quantities (for example induced speed and its nominal-force/density/area expression) can already perturb the power identity; do not mislabel that as root error. A JS implementation using `Math.fround` is useful diagnosis but is not an independent GPU arithmetic oracle.

The exact receiver sensitivity bound `|delta f| <= f0/(3 vi0) * |delta U|` can allocate a field-to-load error budget for the same positive represented constants and nonnegative inflows. Quantization and f32 roundoff are signed errors, so the old one-sided positive-omission moment shortcut does not apply. With per-rotor absolute error at most e, generic body-thrust and each roll/pitch moment bounds are 4e and 4ae, respectively; world-frame bounds must use the actual rotation operator. Independently bound root and assembly errors as well.

## Domains and numerical gates that must be frozen

- **Representation contract:** finite original and decoded states, explicit local coordinate envelope/origin convention, finite parameters and allowed inflow/alpha range. Specify treatment of overflow, subnormals and nonfinite intermediate values. Do not inherit DOT's binary64 ±1e6 m envelope or E/L's 1e-12 m padding for f32. Recentring, if chosen, is a declared transform with cost and original-to-represented error included.
- **Quaternion contract:** retain original-input validation, add a justified decoded-f32 norm admission rule, and preserve unnormalized rotation algebra. The old 1e-10 norm tolerance will generally reject rounded orientations. Neither silently relaxing it nor silently renormalizing is acceptable. Tilt classification uses the declared represented algebra.
- **Boundary contract:** compare original versus represented and represented-reference versus GPU classification separately. Include the strict neighborhood, tilt, down=0, near-downstream limit, near radial radius, downstream cutoff, radial cutoff and fade boundaries. For the initial claim, require original/represented agreement on ordinary valid cases and matching expected rejection on all designated invalid cases. Any broader guarded-domain policy must be defined before exposure, with excluded boundary probes still reported. Never retrospectively drop failing fixtures, pad predicates or accept a near-domain miss because its load error is small.
- **Trace semantics:** retain self/outside, zero-return, domain-error, projection-zero evaluation and profile-evaluated classes. A profile-evaluated term can numerically equal zero. Counts alone cannot prove identity. Record complete classifications at the bounded small sizes or use a predeclared equally verifiable encoding. Parallel failures need a deterministic aggregate rejection/witness rule; “first thread to finish” is not a stable first-error contract.
- **Output gates:** all U finite and nonnegative; receiver range respected; U=0 returns the packed nominal force exactly; prescribed binary64-evaluated cubic residual and ideal-power error; separate absolute/relative inflow, rotor, body/world force and moment limits, plus total error against the original reference. Reference brackets must be tight enough for the new budgets. Near-zero moments need absolute limits. Maximum-component errors and witness indices are primary; RMS alone is insufficient.

The current binary64 force gates and M8 mechanics tolerances do not supply these f32 limits. Select new numeric gates from the intended load accuracy and an explicit error allocation **before execution**. This challenge does not invent a measurement-based tolerance or claim application adequacy. If acceptable tolerances and a supported input domain cannot be stated prospectively, the next step is protocol design, not a browser run.

## Fair cost scope and terminal rules

Measure one complete static query from the same declared host-side inputs to usable host-side output. Include CPU packing/conversion, uploads, parameter updates, command submission, synchronization, map/readback and output materialization/consumption. Include analogous CPU output construction. If an upstream stage already supplies packed input, report that as a separate explicitly narrower scope. Diagnostic independent-oracle checking can sit outside both timers; declare ordinary API validation consistently. Setup/compilation/allocation may be amortized only under a fixed reuse contract, with cold costs reported separately. A slow allocation-heavy reference establishes only a comparison with that reference, not GPU superiority over viable CPU implementations.

Use a complete browser CPU comparator on identical represented inputs, approved for the same numerical contract. Pin its code and any scratch reuse. Freeze timing order, warmups, repeats, synchronization, rendering state and measurement resolution; retain all samples. GPU timestamp queries, if supported, are a separate diagnostic and do not replace end-to-end timing. No renderer FPS arithmetic or extrapolation from M8's 50-step dispatch ratios. Repeated timings are technical repeats, not independent swarm draws or cross-device validation.

The new protocol needs its own exact stage/call ledger, source/rotor/root work reservations, wall-clock and per-dispatch limits, input/CPU/GPU buffer caps, raw-evidence cap and failure reserve. GPU work may not be promptly cancellable; keep each dispatch bounded and define the external browser/device-loss termination route before launch. Reserve diagnostic passes and evidence storage up front. These numbers, seeds and accuracy thresholds are not supplied by this review, so **this note is not a runnable protocol or authorization**.

One attempt on one declared adapter; no exploratory “warmup” science before the freeze. Unsupported capability ends as unsupported. Compile/validation failure, device loss, watchdog/resource exhaustion, nonfinite value, unexpected rejection, domain mismatch or any numerical gate failure ends the attempt with a bounded saved witness and blocks timing. Expected invalid fixtures pass only through their specified rejection. No tuning, retry, seed replacement, larger cap, threshold relaxation or opportunistic backend switch. A predeclared cost-criterion failure is a completed negative result. Any later revision needs a new question/protocol and preserves this result.

R8 stays terminal resource-limited with full correctness and benchmark permission false; its missing cases and budgets are untouched. The E/L checkpoint must be reviewed and closed on its own result. A successful new test would establish only bounded static numerical feasibility on the recorded environment—not physical validity, flight stability, portable f32 agreement, real time, or 5,000-drone feasibility.
