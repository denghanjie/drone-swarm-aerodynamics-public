# Public-payload review

All scientific inputs are previously sanitized public synthetic state, geometry and command records. No private chat, attachments, raw tool logs, credentials, hostnames, serial numbers, private home paths or third-party measurement data are included. Hardware reporting is limited to CPU family and relevant software versions.

Raw stream/index/context files were inspected for metadata and scanned for private paths and credential patterns. Tar owner names, IDs and timestamps were normalized; file contents and scientific values were not modified. Page-only screenshots show synthetic results, without account or browser UI. No new third-party full papers, figures or code were imported; existing Three.js vendor notices remain.

Text scans cover newly published files. Such scans cannot guarantee detection of every secret format; provenance and explicit review are also used. Manifest hashes pin the actual public bytes. This additive branch preserves historical evidence and newer main/DOT work.
