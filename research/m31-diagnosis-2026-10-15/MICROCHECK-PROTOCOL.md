# Temporal harness gate — frozen before integration

The four 0.05 s verification traces are exactly the allowance in the published bounded plan: frozen controller uninterrupted; adapted controller uninterrupted; adapted split at 0.02 s; adapted split at 0.025 s. Split segments total 0.05 s each. All use 1,000 narrow seed-2002 drones restarted from the original coarse 20 s state, 2.5 ms integration and 100 Hz control.

First verify both A and B's initial motor targets against their retained M32 command streams, without integration. Pure-state force-on/force-off pair fixtures then verify that the directed intervention bypasses only the selected distance gate and leaves another pair present. These are implementation checks, not new trajectory claims.

Require exact equality of all four final 17-state arrays, held actuator objects, integer scheduler ticks, crossings, termination flags and saturation accounting. Concatenated tick-tagged motor-command streams must be byte-identical, including at both split boundaries. Preserve the entire held actuator object and prior control diagnostic state across off-boundary restarts. Controller diagnostic payloads differ between frozen and expanded instrumentation; their extra fields are not themselves an equality target. Every original diagnostic field was already checked in the fixed-state probe.

`source/engine.cjs` retains original physics, 34-solid geometry, local Boids, control arithmetic and swept-contact traversal. All loads are zero. Every logged time is an integer tick at 1/1600 s. Commands are recomputed at multiples of 16 ticks and held across RK4 steps. Saved states and commands use Float64LE streams; indexes retain valid-frame counts even if a job is interrupted. This changes data recording, not a physical model. Contact records preserve the preceding/detected endpoints and preceding held control. No interpolated event-time claim is made.

The same cumulative supervisor counts this gate against 1,200 seconds and 2 GiB. Each intended verification trace reserves 0.05 s in `raw/micro-started.json` before execution; a failure never resets that allowance. Raw data are retained under `raw/` for later checksum packaging, not committed as incidental working files. No study trajectory is launched by the microcheck script.

The instrumented uninterrupted trace supplies the frozen launch estimate: 2× the estimated physics/safety work for 88 coarse-step-equivalent flock-seconds, natural steering for 44 flock-seconds, I/O for 64 flock-seconds, and measurements for 650 samples, plus 120 s reserve. Both earlier recorded executable time and the supervisor's final microcheck elapsed time must be subtracted before accepting the gate. This is a conservative scheduling estimate, not a speed benchmark or promise of completion. A failed gate prohibits the full study matrix.

The first two retained motor schedules, both 20 s checkpoints, and their original mission JSON files used to recover crossing history are included in `freeze-micro.json`, along with all executable dependencies. The commit containing that record precedes temporal execution. Existing M31/M32 cases remain development evidence, and all historical 1 cm failures are preserved.
