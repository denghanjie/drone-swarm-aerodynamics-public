**Supported within these two saved states:** the large immediate order sensitivity belongs to sequential pair-constraint processing. Reversing only the Boids accumulation loop produces differences below 5 × 10⁻¹⁶ m/s. This does not identify the cause of the historical trajectory divergence.

Natural order exactly matched every original requested velocity and original diagnostic field for all 2,000 drones. Input states were unchanged. Both median pair-order differences were zero. The social-loop intervention reverses accumulation of separation, alignment, cohesion and weights together; it leaves constraint order fixed. The pair intervention leaves that entire social loop and wall order fixed. Pair-only maxima reproduce the previously observed joint-reversal maxima at displayed precision.

These are exact descriptive evaluations of two deterministic state arrays. There is no sampling confidence interval or estimate of deployment risk. The 0.01 m/s reporting scale was already exposed in the previous audit and is not a newly optimized acceptance threshold. Finite precision effects smaller than those measured here could still amplify in a subsequent feedback trajectory; this test does not establish their global irrelevance.

No physics trajectory or aerodynamic load was generated by this probe. M31's original three 1 cm path tests remain failed. Novelty is not established.
