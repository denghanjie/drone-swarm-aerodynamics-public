# Independent challenge of the restart proposal

M31 is strong enough to keep as an engineering baseline and too weak to call a validated simulator. It demonstrates prescribed airflow-off passage under ideal observations, while all three original 1 cm trajectory comparisons fail. The primary question now is which scientific claims can survive uncertainty, not how to manufacture a pass by changing the comparison.

1. **The integrator has not been globally exonerated.** M32 shows small local step differences from one selected common restart and much greater sensitivity to two inherited full states. Earlier integration error may still have created the perturbation. A fine trajectory is not ground truth. Retain local same-start refinement and explicitly crossed state/step comparisons; avoid rerunning every historical mission to settle a question the local evidence cannot answer.
2. **The supposed neighbor mechanism is not identified.** Boids membership is smoothly weighted at its outer radius; pair constraints have another cutoff. Grid output is already sorted by ID. The newly exposed reversal probe changes both social summation and sequential filtering order, and does not show a natural reorder occurred. Isolate the two operations on identical saved states before interpreting it. Even a large pair-order effect is design sensitivity, not a demonstrated causal explanation of the historical divergence.
3. **Full motor replay is an intervention on the entire feedback system.** It removes neighbor, wall, flight-controller and saturation response simultaneously. Use it to bound an explanation, then use an actual one-tick paired intervention on one decision if indicated. Never call replay an improved policy.
4. **A 17-component checkpoint is not always a complete restart.** At the exact 20 s boundary the existing controller is stateless and recomputes its commands. General restarts need schedule phase, held commands, mission history and future airflow caches/history. Without split-run equivalence, a restart study may measure its own harness.
5. **Macroscopic agreement is a different question.** Existing crossing distributions can remain similar while individual crossing times differ by tens of seconds. That is a useful basis for a future preregistered estimand, not permission to replace the failed same-ID criterion after seeing the data. Contact identity, completion count, crossing distribution, connectivity, load histories and path errors should all remain visible.
6. **The statistical unit is a scenario/seed, not a drone or frame.** M31's few cases do not support a safety rate. Wide/narrow used different seeds; future geometry comparisons must cross seed and geometry. Exposed M31/M32 cases remain development data. Reserve genuinely unused, frozen initial states for any subsequent confirmation.
7. **Aero validation cannot borrow its reference from an unconverged numerical model.** A brute-force evaluator validates the implementation of the same kernel. An independently refined solver can support a limited numerical comparison. Force/moment/wake measurements under matched geometry and actuation are needed for physical accuracy. Receiving-rotor response, multiple-wake overlap and walls are separate assumptions.
8. **Engineering integration is not a novelty claim.** Combining Boids, a standard flight model, a borrowed wake model and WebGPU may be an excellent product or benchmark. A research claim needs a specific gap and falsifiable benefit: preferably an independently validated error–cost tradeoff or an approximation whose error indicator predicts held-out outcomes. Literature resemblance alone neither proves nor rules out novelty.
9. **Do not let diagnosis consume the aerodynamic project.** The next proposed tranche has a 20-minute computation cap, zero cloud spend, ten short trajectories maximum and no controller tuning. On completion or cap exhaustion, return a single candidate hypothesis or an explicit unresolved diagnosis. The next useful experiment is a small matched aerodynamic sensitivity comparison with basic coupling checks, not an indefinite sequence of smoother-controller campaigns.
10. **Do not merge later work into the baseline silently.** Parent integration context identifies historical base `c591ce2b195fa01b895d4ba881a44a4b92fdd703` and newer remote main `1336f5c416875a9651f92b58b2ed06649f25b04e`, which includes the DOT wake-pruning campaign and R8 partial stop. Its identity, assumptions and evidence should remain a separate branch/comparator. It is not part of the frozen M31 baseline or evidence that M31's trajectories were accurate. The proposed historical checkpoint must not duplicate that optimization and claim novelty.

The concrete proposal is in `bounded-restart-plan.md`; `next-tranche-proposal.json` makes its jobs and execution caps reviewable. Neither is an instruction to start those experiments in the present checkpoint. `read-only-evidence-audit.json` contains the only executed work in this subtask: artifact hashing and recomparison of existing recorded arrays.

Final adversarial review corrections: the budget now specifies four 0.05 s validation traces, including an off-boundary held-command restart, within the 0.2 s aggregate allowance. The watchdog and all retries share the original cap. The local intervention changes one directed inclusion with the cutoff bypassed and sorted placement retained; L1/L2 must be identical negative controls. A-driven replay is explicitly conditional and the first observed event can be left-censored at 20 s. Historical-runtime parity is distinguished from same-runtime instrumentation parity. The wider checkpoint did six single-drone RK4 contact checks but no new full-swarm trajectory campaign. A portable read-only audit script reproduces the secondary evidence, and absolute source-root metadata has been replaced by `PUBLIC_REPOSITORY`.
