# Bounded restart plan from M31

Status: proposal for the next authorized tranche; this checkpoint executed no new full-swarm trajectory campaign. The separate evidence audit did six single-drone RK4 contact-replay consistency steps. This protocol subtask comprises source inspection, existing-data comparisons and planning, with no simulator execution. All mechanism explanations below are hypotheses or deductions from source code, unless explicitly identified as existing evidence. This document does not supply a literature-based novelty claim; the separately verified literature map must support any such claim.

## Decision to make

Retain M31 as a useful airflow-off engineering baseline. Do one bounded diagnosis to determine whether a specific feedback decision merits a targeted correction, then return to the aerodynamic accuracy–cost question. Do not make long-term same-ID trajectory agreement a prerequisite for every exploratory aerodynamic calculation, and do not drop it from the historical acceptance record. The requested future system remains local 3D Boids, rigid-body flight, aerodynamic interaction and WebGPU at up to 5,000 drones. This first tranche neither implements that system nor establishes its accuracy.

## Evidence available now

A read-only reanalysis of the saved Float64 pose files reproduces M31's three maximum synchronized position discrepancies: 5.166609755 m (1,000 wide, 180 s), 32.767460348 m (1,000 narrow, 180 s), and 7.981792207 m (5,000 wide, first 60 s). The first saved times at or above 1 cm are 35, 30 and 31 s. All three original tests remain failed. Primary wall-priority runs passed all prescribed passages with airflow off; the three matched baseline runs stopped at obstacle contact. The small scenario set supplies case evidence, not a safety probability. Different seeds were used for the wide and narrow 1,000 cases, so an across-scenario geometry effect is confounded with seed.

The already exposed M32 development results are useful prior evidence, not fresh validation. In the selected narrow 1,000-drone 20–30 s interval, two step sizes restarted from the same coarse state differ by at most 0.879 micrometres; changing the inherited full state while retaining the coarse step gives 48.510 mm. All state components differed initially, including up to 0.003351 rad/s in angular velocity and 1.337e-5 N in rotor thrust. Position-only perturbation language is therefore incorrect. M32's same-command refinements agree to about 9.85 micrometres and 0.602 micrometres for consecutive halvings; these are not bounds on error against physical truth.

The accompanying `read-only-evidence-audit.json` contains independently recomputed M31 and M32 comparisons and 20 matching protocol source/input hash checks. It checks artifact consistency, not independent replication of trajectories or physical validity. `reproduce-evidence-audit.py` reproduces this audit using the standard Python library and an explicit source-root argument; it imports no simulator. Its output replaces the original machine-specific source path with `PUBLIC_REPOSITORY` as a metadata-only redaction. Relative path spellings are normalized; input bytes, numerical comparison values and hash results are unchanged. The audit checks raw paired timestamps as well as matching sample-time keys and reports any discrepancy.

## What can be identified

|Question|Useful comparison|What it can establish|What it cannot establish|
|---|---|---|---|
|Does step size matter locally from this state?|Same complete restart state, same physical update times, three physics steps|Local differences and whether adjacent-step differences contract|Truth of the finest solution; all-time convergence; accuracy before the checkpoint|
|Does inherited state matter?|Two historical full states, same step and policy|Sensitivity to this joint state perturbation in this window|Sensitivity to position alone; a Lyapunov exponent; chaos; representative probability|
|Does feedback contribute?|Both starts driven by one identical recorded motor-command schedule, compared with their natural feedback runs|Whether removing all feedback changes this particular separation|Which feedback component caused it; whether replay is a safe autonomous policy|
|Does a neighbor-set switch matter?|One-tick crossed evaluation of states and membership masks, followed by bounded paired continuations|Local causal effect of that selected membership decision under a stated intervention|Global dominance; a viable replacement controller; generalization|
|Are scientific outputs stable enough to compare models?|Matched scenarios at multiple steps and field cadences with specified observables|Robustness of those observables in those cases|Long-trajectory accuracy; physical model accuracy; rare-event safety|

The code does not support simply blaming “neighbor switching.” `grid.cjs` sorts neighbor IDs, so CPU hash traversal ordering is not an established cause. The social weight tends smoothly to zero at 1.8 m; separation also tends to zero at its 0.85 m boundary. Pair constraints enter at a separate strict 1.5 m cutoff, and their effect depends on whether the added constraint binds. The finite sequential projection, wall repair, max-speed projection, wall nearest-feature changes, route-field kinks, low-horizontal-speed heading sensitivity, mixer clipping and saturation are competing candidates. A heading angle wrapping by 2π alone does not demonstrate a physical discontinuity: the controller uses its sine/cosine and quaternion sign handling. Merely counting membership differences does not explain trajectory divergence.

A separate read-only checkpoint audit in this checkpoint has now exposed an additional artificial ID-order probe. On the two 1,000-drone 20 s states, reversing the provided neighbor IDs produces requested-velocity changes on the order of 0.17 m/s in the wall-priority cases. Its script reverses both social accumulation and pair-constraint iteration, so attribution to the six-sweep projection alone is not established. The frozen grid always sorts IDs; this does not show that natural runtime order reversed. Treat the probe as exploratory prior exposure. Source and exact results: `../evidence/audit_controls.cjs` and `../evidence/audit-controls.json`.

Before temporal jobs, add a cheap fixed-state decomposition: natural order; reverse only pair-constraint iteration while retaining wall order and social accumulation; reverse only social accumulation while retaining every constraint order. Run the same three evaluations on the two saved states, record complete outputs/residuals, and verify natural mode matches the frozen function exactly. This is a deterministic controller evaluation, not a new mission or a correction. If pair-only reversal reproduces the large change and social-only reversal does not, it supports an order-sensitive implementation effect at those states; it still does not establish the cause of the historical step-size divergence. If the effect disappears under pair-only reversal, the proposed projection attribution is falsified. No candidate is selected merely because a permutation gives a smaller residual.

The wall repair solves a velocity-request problem. Feasible wall residuals do not establish actual vehicle tracking or pair safety. The current 0.3 m margin / 1.2 s horizon yields a conditional ideal continuous-time calculation only; the implementation has no verified uniform tracking-error bound, sampled normals, motor lag, finite thrust and softened pair requests.

## Next tranche: at most ten short trajectories

This is a diagnostic development experiment. It contains no fresh-seed mission performance claim. Its necessary job matrix and caps are fixed below; do not automatically add further rounds when a result is ambiguous.

Let A be the M31 coarse narrow seed-2002 1,000-drone state at 20 s, and B its historical fine-step state at the same absolute time. Keep M31 control gains, local Boids, geometry and airflow-off physics unchanged.

|Jobs|Start / forcing|Step|Interval|Purpose|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|D1, D2|A and B; natural closed loop|2.5 ms|20–30 s|Two matched detailed decision traces; repeat known development outcomes with added passive instrumentation|
|D3, D4|A and B; natural closed loop|1.25 ms|20–30 s|Complete the explicit start × step matrix, with the same instrumentation contract|
|D5, D6|A and B; identical exact four-motor commands from D1|2.5 ms|20–30 s|True same-forcing comparison across inherited states; D5 must reproduce D1 exactly|
|L1–L4, conditional|A-derived and B-derived states at one selected tick, each with natural and one-tick common-membership forcing|1.25 ms|At most 1 s each|Paired local intervention; no altered subsequent policy|

Existing M32 A-start 0.625 ms traces provide prior third-step evidence only after exact overlap checks with D1/D3. They are not a freshly measured third level. If these records cannot be matched, the convergence claim is unavailable; do not substitute a new unbudgeted run. The inherited-state contrast in D1/D2 does not assign truth to either start. At each common time report the within-start step difference, the within-step state difference, and their crossed interaction using componentwise vectors before taking the stated norms. Do not subtract maxima and call the result an additive error partition. D5/D6 use the A-derived command schedule only: they estimate an A-forcing-conditional contrast, not a general or symmetric fraction of divergence caused by feedback. A second B-driven replay pair is outside this budget. The existing third-level path data support three-level position comparisons only; other state components have endpoint information or two-level comparisons and must be labeled accordingly.

Select the local event deterministically: the first 100 Hz tick in the intersection of D1/D2 valid pre-control records with a different actual pair-constraint membership, then the lowest affected drone ID i and lowest differing neighbor ID j. This is one directed constraint (i,j), not both directions. Derive masks using the same production distance arithmetic and strict cutoff; verify full IDs at the selected tick rather than relying only on hashes. If the first difference occurs at 20 s, its onset is left-censored: it may have arisen before the restart. If no such tick occurs, record “no eligible pair event” and omit L1–L4. This selection is descriptive and post-hoc in a known development case, not a success metric or proof that the earliest differing pair is the main amplifier.

Before L1–L4, evaluate the crossed four-way map on the actual stored states: state A or B × pair-membership mask from A or B. Recompute the selected normal and lower bound from the receiving state; change only inclusion of directed pair (i,j). The override must bypass that pair's original `d < 1.5` membership gate, otherwise forcing an absent pair on can silently do nothing. Insert the forced pair in its original ascending-ID position after the unchanged walls; do not append it or reorder other constraints. The intervention may access j directly from the full state but must not change the social-neighbor list. Check nonsingular separation before constructing a forced normal; if unsupported, record the local intervention as ineligible and omit it without substituting another event. Preserve social neighbors, all other pairs, walls, order, gains, speed ball and flight controller. A single `ids` override shared by Boids and pair filtering would confound this test. The A-start natural/common-A-mask pair is an exact negative control: L1/L2 must have identical first commands and full continuations, because the one overridden decision already equals its natural value and the policy then reverts identically. It is not independent replication. Evaluate both force-on and force-off unit fixtures to ensure the gate override is active; these are static controller calls, not added trajectories. Use the A mask as the common intervention and apply it for exactly one 10 ms control interval, then return to the unchanged natural policy. Start all four local runs from the stored A/B pre-control states at the selected absolute tick, not interpolated poses or a state after that tick's command has already run. Hold their first computed commands for exactly eight 1.25 ms steps, then recompute the unchanged natural policy at the next 10 ms boundary. Each counterfactual is paired with a natural continuation from its own identical state at the same step; reusing a coarse natural trajectory as a fine-step control would not be a valid pair. Record exact mask differences and treat the local intervention as explanatory, never as a navigation candidate.

Instrumentation stores pre-control states in binary for D1/D2, motor targets, requested velocities before/after pair filtering and wall repair, ordered membership hashes/counts, active deficits, saturation flags, repair residual/iteration counts and actual tracking error. Reconstruct exact membership from retained states at selected ticks. Do not store a full all-neighbor JSON list at every tick. All added diagnostics must leave states and commands identical to the uninstrumented run. The fixed-state decomposition must also pass an exact natural-mode regression before its modified-order results are interpreted.

## Observables and interpretation gates

Preserve the original M31 same-ID 1 cm criterion with its original scope. New diagnostics report continuous results, rather than choosing a new pass boundary after seeing a discrepancy.

At exact common times report maximum, median and nearest-rank P95 position and velocity discrepancy, sign-invariant attitude-angle discrepancy, angular-rate and motor-thrust discrepancy; per-unit component magnitudes remain separate. If a combined state norm is useful, predefine physically meaningful scales and report sensitivity to them; do not mix metres, radians and newtons without normalization. Report all drones and the identity attaining each maximum. Finite-time amplification is a descriptive ratio under the chosen norm; the mixed historical perturbation does not estimate a Lyapunov exponent.

For scientific outputs preserve first event type/identity/time bracket, crossing-time distribution, number observed to cross by time t, horizon completion, minimum swept clearance and separation, sampled connectivity/polarization, commanded versus actual velocity, saturation occupancy, force/moment histories and vehicle/model-domain exits. A sampled minimum is not an all-time minimum. One whole-swarm stop is one dependent scenario outcome, not N independent failures. Model-domain exits must remain distinct from contacts.

Decision gates:

- **G0 — provenance and restart:** hashes/configurations match; source changes are passive; D1/D3 reproduce retained M32 common-time results, D2 reproduces the retained fine-start/coarse-step M32 continuation, D4 matches the original fine trajectory on shared saved times, and D5 reproduces D1. A same-runtime passive-instrumentation or D1/D5 mismatch is a harness issue first. A historical-only mismatch may instead involve environment differences. In either case the affected historical/replay causal comparison remains unavailable until resolved; do not silently loosen exact parity or replace missing evidence.
- **G1 — local discretization:** compare A-start adjacent refinements with the already exposed third level; check event and branch changes as well as norms. Contracting differences support limited local consistency. Noncontracting differences or event disagreement make this interval unresolved. Neither result certifies the preceding 20 s or later mission.
- **G2 — mechanism:** compare D1–D4 state/step effects, D5/D6 same-forcing effects, exact branch traces and the selected one-tick contrast. If the isolated membership intervention has no effect at the affected control tick (for example, the constraint is nonbinding), it falsifies that selected event as the proposed immediate source of a command jump. It does not falsify all membership effects or identify the source before 20 s. Report exact numerical differences without inventing an after-the-fact appreciability threshold. If amplification persists under identical commands, inherited rigid-body/actuator sensitivity is also relevant. If similar divergence develops with identical membership, membership switching alone is insufficient. No universal dominance ratio is predeclared because this tranche estimates effects rather than accepts a controller.
- **G3 — bounded next action:** propose at most one justified controller change for later tests, or explicitly conclude “cause not isolated within cap.” Do not launch another diagnosis loop automatically. Numerical uncertainty remains attached to subsequent results.

A further exposed reanalysis of the original M31 crossing records illustrates why observables must be chosen explicitly: the narrow-case maximum same-drone crossing-time change is about 53.55 s, while the largest empirical finish-time CDF difference is 0.006. The wide values are about 6.63 s and 0.009. These post-hoc observations (see `../evidence/audit-data.json`) can motivate a future frozen distributional estimand, but are neither independent tests nor substitute acceptance criteria. Similar completion distributions can coexist with large changes in individual assignments and paths.

A mechanism that survives these checks is only a development hypothesis. Any replacement controller must be evaluated against frozen M31 in true matched trials, including both policy variants at both numerical steps, with the same initial states, geometry, observation conditions, field schedule and stopping rules. Improving a selected divergence trace alone is not acceptance.

## Route toward 5,000 drones and a publishable claim

The current combination of Boids, an existing rigid-body controller, wall projection, a borrowed wake approximation, Three.js and prospective WebGPU is not itself demonstrated methodological novelty. Fast visualization and a successful airflow-off prescribed passage are useful deliverables but do not establish a research contribution.

A defensible candidate contribution would be a reproducible, physically anchored comparison of aerodynamic approximation error versus computation and mission-level sensitivity for large local flocks, with source/receiver assumptions, domain limits and uncertainty explicit. It would require independent force/moment or velocity measurements; held-out geometries and actuation conditions; model-versus-implementation error separation; and performance measured at a fixed accuracy criterion. Another possible direction is an approximation that adapts wake evaluation or cadence to a justified error indicator, but novelty requires a literature comparison and the indicator must predict held-out errors better than simple fixed settings.

Falsifiers include: no held-out prediction gain over a cheaper force-only model; claimed torque effects absent in independently measured asymmetric configurations; the performance gain disappears at matched error; a ranked aerodynamic tradeoff reverses under reasonable field cadence or model-domain treatment; errors grow with local density so spatial hashing fails to deliver the promised workload; or GPU arithmetic/order changes scientific outcomes beyond the frozen tolerance. Negative findings remain publishable only with a rigorous, useful scope and credible external validation, not merely a large number of runs.

For WebGPU, first verify gravity/hover, quaternion rotation, motor lag, thrust mixing, momentum/torque accounting, grid completeness, self-exclusion and simultaneous updates. Compare one step and short windows against CPU at identical physical cadences, test repeated-run behavior and workgroup/reduction order, and keep binary32 arithmetic error separate from integration-step error. Benchmark physics, steering, aerodynamic evaluation, transfers and rendering separately using GPU timestamps where supported, warm-up plus repeated measurements and workload statistics. A local grid does not guarantee linear work when neighbor occupancy grows. Count scaling in M31 increases the starting cloud at fixed nominal spacing; it is not density scaling. A frozen 5,000-pose workload benchmark can measure compute cost before a full 5,000 mission, but cannot establish closed-loop trajectory accuracy or real-time safe flight.

## Optional independent physical-validation track

In parallel with software work, prepare a measurement protocol for a geometrically matched source and receiver: separated and offset rotor/drone positions, isolated baseline, controlled supply/PWM or measured RPM, thrust/torque, relative pose, measurement uncertainty and repeatability. Split calibration configurations from held-out validation configurations before fitting. Measure or bound both the wake field and receiving-rotor force/moment response; a wake-velocity fit alone does not validate the thrust-loss map. Begin with a stable free-air hover envelope, then separate tilt/motion, multiple-source interaction and wall proximity. If raw independent measurements or equipment are unavailable, document that limitation and produce the protocol/dataset specification; do not replace physical validation with simulated agreement or another model treated as truth.

A complementary research direction is to test whether distributional or event-level observables remain reproducible under controlled state perturbations while long same-ID trajectories separate. It must use independent seed clusters and justified observation scales. It can change the question for a future study, but cannot erase the historical 1 cm failures or claim statistical convergence from this one selected interval.
