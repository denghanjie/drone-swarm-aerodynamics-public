# Bounded experiments suggested by the verified literature

These are proposals for the checkpoint owner to incorporate into a frozen protocol. **None was run in this literature task.** They do not establish novelty. First inventory the already-existing internal spatial-wake-pruning work and its evidence; reuse relevant implementations/results instead of assuming pruning has never been tried. M31 remains a historical airflow-off baseline, with its failed trajectory criterion retained.

The source IDs below refer to [annotated-ledger.md](annotated-ledger.md). All experimental choices and gate logic are our inferences. The paper observations supporting them are kept in that ledger.

| Priority | Bounded question and proposed development budget | Comparison and recorded outputs | Stop / interpretation rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can the receiver formulation express offset loading under the stated actuator conditions? One vehicle specification; at most three model classes; 24 prescribed static states: four normalized vertical separations × six lateral offsets. Select separations from verified data coverage before fitting. | No interaction; aggregate receiver response; four-rotor response. Record mean vertical force, pitch moment, rotor forces, units, signs, input settings and domain flags. Keep wake-velocity residuals distinct. | Without accessible observations and actuation metadata, this is an implementation/identifiability audit only. Never fit arbitrary body-force gains and call the result physical validation. [A1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.09463v1), [A2](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13321v3) |
| 2 | Does a cheap update policy preserve the chosen equations on a prescribed encounter? At most two geometries (stacked and offset), two prescribed motions and three update schedules; no controller tuning. | Full refresh versus one fixed-cadence and one already-implemented or explicitly specified adaptive candidate. Hold motion, source strengths and receiver law fixed. Record instantaneous/max/RMS force and moment differences, stale-field duration, calls and wall time. | Separate cadence error from physical dynamic-model discrepancy. Quasi-static agreement cannot validate wake memory. Retain every failure; stop after the fixed comparison budget. [A1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.09463v1) |
| 3 | Which search/evaluation implementation is cheapest for an unchanged interaction model? One frozen snapshot family at N = 64, 512 and 5,000; sparse and crowded configurations; two implementations initially. | Tiled direct evaluation versus exact spatial enumeration of the *same specified support*. Check neighbor-set identity, overflow, directed interactions, reduction/precision error; time build, force evaluation, synchronization and transfer separately. | At most six workload configurations before review. A model with infinite nonzero support needs an explicit truncation study; grid lookup alone cannot justify removal. Failed allocation/device limit counts as a result. CUDA timings cannot be substituted for measured WebGPU timings. [C1](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-v-physics-simulation/chapter-31-fast-n-body-simulation-cuda), [C2](https://developer.download.nvidia.com/assets/cuda/files/particles.pdf) |

Before any of these experiments, freeze the exact equations/version, geometry, actuator interpretation, development/held-out grouping, input-state hashes, device/browser and timing definition. Numerical tolerances must be justified from precision/refinement evidence; physical tolerances require measurement uncertainty and the intended observable. This note supplies neither a universal error threshold nor a guaranteed real-time budget.

A small learned vertical-force residual can be an additional baseline only after suitable observations and frozen splits exist; its training/inference expense must be included. A4 already establishes that model class, and A3 establishes that aggregation warrants a separate check. Moving from two to several neighbors is a held-out configuration question, not an automatic consequence of permutation invariance. [A3](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03488v1), [A4](https://arxiv.org/html/2012.05457v2)

Only after numerical and receiver gates should one fixed local-3D-flocking workload be repeated with coupling enabled. Preserve acceleration/actuator constraints and local rules, and report clearance, contact, tilt and group structure alongside the unchanged historical trajectory criterion. Flocking behavior itself is prior work; constrained 2D results are not evidence of successful volumetric coupled flight. [F1](https://www.red3d.com/cwr/boids/), [F2](https://research.vu.nl/ws/files/118802354/Optimized_flocking_of_autonomous_drones_in_confined_environments.pdf)

The potentially useful result is an empirically supported boundary: where a particular approximation saves measured computation and where it fails a justified observable requirement. Whether that boundary is new or publishable remains an open literature-and-evidence question.
