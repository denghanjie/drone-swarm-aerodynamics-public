# Source review plan — before fresh exposure

This is a review checklist, not a completed review or launch authorization.

1. Compare the candidate against frozen F and the qualified H/H0 sources. Account for every numerical, ordering, guard, evidence and timing difference. Require unchanged coefficient, root, load and positive-term arithmetic; compact capture must describe the actual array consumed.
2. Compare the injected flight adapter with the qualified projected adapter. Preserve quaternion expression order, current-stage input identity, load/ID binding, single replacement of force and moment, nominal yaw and motor state. Check the four fixed seed/count/order recipes and one-step count without generating them.
3. Inspect compact tree reconstruction independently: stable split topology, rounded width comparison, exact extrema, descendant bitsets, candidate-order reconstruction, first-success predicate masks and actual rounded cull words. Ensure every retained/culled source belongs to one complete disjoint partition. Check the specific midpoint inclusivity proof rather than relying on comments.
4. Inspect lossless transport path coverage, shard bounds/types/order, byte-equality deduplication, codec canonicality, actual encoded length limits, descriptor journal and strict decoder rejection. Verify source-derived logical bounds separately from compressed sizes.
5. Inspect all stage/derivative/RK and four64 reference bindings. Require complete large-case native equivalence; do not describe large cases as independently exact-load verified. Controller provenance/consumption is the declared boundary, not a new independent controller oracle.
6. Inspect saved42/126 regression preflight and fixed corruptions. Require successful preflight and observed child exit before fresh initialization. Include its 15-second cap within shared 60-second overhead; protect all old source/results from writes.
7. Inspect independent host deadlines, fresh exclusive output directories, selected runtime hashes, Chrome root ownership/termination/exit, file/aggregate reserves, failure records and prohibition on retries. Keep server8876 unchanged.
8. Verify a syntax-only parse, exact bundle source slices, full dependency pins, public privacy scan and exact-public source receipt. Parsing is not a scientific test. Review comments cannot claim dynamic behavior not yet observed.

The native/adapter author cannot independently certify those edits. The transport/supervisor author cannot independently certify containment. Review provenance and any remaining blockers must be stated in the completed source review. If a material uncertainty remains, retain disabled source and checkpoint.
