What this tells us—and what it leaves open

  • No curve was adjusted after opening these airflow values.
  • The two formulas use different distance units. We converted those units explicitly.
  • The paper’s curve describes this measured plane. It is not yet a replacement for airflow everywhere around a drone.
  • The original source paper measured speed magnitude. Here we test our model’s downward interpretation, assuming a shared reference air speed; that assumption is not independently calibrated.
  • This test does not settle how airflow changes propeller thrust, or whether a swarm can fly safely.
