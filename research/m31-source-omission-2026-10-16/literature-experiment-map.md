# Literature-to-experiment map

[Verified source details and access limits](prior-work.md), [BibTeX](sources.bib).

- Raykar & Duraiswami2007, DOI10.7551/mitpress/7496.003.0010, inspected author-draft §1.8.6/Eqs1.27–1.28: established per-source omission errors and summed absolute error allowances. Motivates budget accounting; isotropic Gaussian formulas are not reused as wake bounds.
- Gray & Moore2003, *Rapid Evaluation of Multiple Density Models*, official PMLRr4record: established source/query region bounds. Motivates common bounds over four rotor targets; its density likelihood target is not a force/moment budget.
- Dehnen2014, DOI10.1186/s40668-014-0001-7, §§4.1/5.3: distinguishes strict bounds from practical error estimators. Motivates explicit separation of real-arithmetic derivation, frozen numerical margins and finite empirical checks; no gravitational kernel transplanted.
- Chen,Joo&Song2021, DOI10.3390/en14217326, §3.2/Eqs8–9: published weak wake-edge pruning. This defeats a generic novelty claim for wake pruning; the inspected wind-farm criterion does not validate this microquadrotor model or establish novelty of its specific bound.

Current experiment implements/tests a conditional M28 adaptation. Mathematical inequalities are in MATH-REVIEW.md; they do not establish real-world force accuracy. The unchanged wake/receiver assumptions and original aerodynamic citations remain in the earlier M31 restart/force-reference ledgers. No current empirical claim relies on an inaccessible paper's unseen content.
