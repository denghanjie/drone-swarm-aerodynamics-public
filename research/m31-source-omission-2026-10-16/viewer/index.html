WHAT THE TEST SUPPORTS

A limited numerical trade-off.

The fixed targets are 1.32435 mN in body thrust and 10.4883 µN·m in each roll/pitch moment. All valid cases stayed within those targets, and invalid cases were rejected consistently. These tolerances have no flight-safety meaning.

The fresh 5,000-drone candidate was about 41% faster than the original implementation and 16% faster than the matched implementation. Five timed repeats per method support only this tested setup; one query still takes over a second.

THE NEXT QUESTION

Where does the saving come from?

The candidate skips both provably zero contributions and small positive ones. We need a separate controlled comparison to isolate the benefit of approximating positive contributions.

Agreement with these equations does not validate the fixed source strengths, additive wakes or constant ideal-power receiver response. The analytical bounds have frozen numerical allowances and finite empirical checks; they are not a universal floating-point certificate.
