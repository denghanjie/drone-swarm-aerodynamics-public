# Draft whole-science lifecycle observer

New campaign only: `research/m31-body-runtime-replication-2026-10-16`. This is an unexecuted static draft. Do not invoke these files, a science module, runtime helper, source sealer or browser during review. Old campaign sources, pins, locks and results remain unchanged.

The observer supervises the whole scientific lifecycle, with an external caller recording its actual completion. Its monotonic clock starts before evidence I/O; an absolute hard timer is armed before remaining imports and stays armed through final receipts, stdout and interpreter exit. Science work stops at 296 seconds, cleanup must complete before 299 seconds, and the hard timer exits 124 at 300 seconds. The existing worker's 285-second cooperative deadline, 30-second GPU batches, 768 MiB raw, 32 MiB results, sampled 4 GiB RSS and GPU/work counters remain unchanged. Caller-measured full observer process elapsed must also be under 300 seconds, covering interpreter startup and exit beyond the internal sample. A timer signal/hard exit or missing/truncated receipt cannot pass.

`capture-science.py` is the sole launch entry point. Its permanent exclusive `capture-launch.lock` is outside the initially empty results directory; the observer retains its separate permanent science lock. The caller launches only its same Python executable, the fixed campaign `source/observe-science.py`, and the single supplied absolute Node executable. It records monotonic timestamps immediately before `Popen` and only after an actual observer wait/reap, and captures observer stdout/stderr nonblockingly to private regular files with a combined 16 KiB cap. The observer binds the direct caller PID and includes its RSS in the same sampled 4 GiB scope. The caller stops at total 299.5 seconds, permits bounded direct-child reaping/capture through 299.75 seconds, and hard exits at 300 seconds. This is a conservative restriction within the same allowance; it may reject a late observer completion. It does not change observer296/299/300 or worker285. Caller timeout can kill only its retained direct observer child and never certifies relay or detached browser cleanup.

Exactly one observer creates the permanent science lock and launches one relay; the relay launches exactly the supplied absolute Node executable and the fixed campaign `source/experiment.cjs`, once. The relay's Node child shares its observer-created session/group. The directly owned relay PID remains unreaped until cleanup; its numeric group is never signaled after reaping. Chrome still uses Playwright's unchanged launch options and detached group. The private registry records its exact executable and unique user-data-dir argument **before** spawn and binds its PID afterward. Unknown detached auxiliary spawns are rejected. The observer can discover a browser by that pre-spawn intent if the post-spawn append was interrupted. Final missing/partial/mismatched registry evidence fails.

Every browser group signal requires a fresh full process-table match of its leader, executable and unique profile, plus current PGID equality. A group without that proof is not signaled and closure fails. After observed absence, a group is never signaled again. Unregistered detached descendants fail admission. Neither the observer nor relay signals shared process groups. Private identities stay in the supervisor-created temporary directory.

Cleanup errors are isolated by resource: failed registry reads retain earlier admitted browser identities, valid record prefixes can preserve pre-spawn intents, each browser check is independent, and retained process-probe children still receive bounded direct cleanup attempts. An error remains a failure even if later scans show absence. The hard-deadline handler can kill only the still-owned relay group and direct probe children; it cannot safely rediscover detached Chrome at that instant. A hard exit or ownership uncertainty therefore requires separately verified cleanup and never produces a passing closure receipt.

Node pipes are nonblocking, drained to EOF, and persisted under a combined 1 MiB ceiling within the unchanged 32 MiB envelope. Relay pipes are nonblocking and have an 8 KiB combined ceiling; their actual bounded bytes are preserved privately, including failure diagnostics. Their bytes are counted through private file sizes exactly once, without adding observed pipe counts a second time. `/bin/ps` probes have a 0.25-second/4-MiB output ceiling, and every wait is finite; probe children are directly killed/reaped if necessary. Probe failures fail closed. Raw and result files are counted as flat regular files. Private registry/error/cleanup bytes and observer/relay output are conservatively included in the 32 MiB result accounting; 128 KiB is reserved during work for final provenance. This tightens diagnostic headroom, not the scientific limits. RSS and filesystem polling are sampled observations, not proofs against every transient peak or byte-write overshoot.

The relay records only actual Node exit and complete pipe persistence. The observer records the relay's actual exit, complete registry/group/pipe closure, terminal worker identity, exact final bytes including its budget and bounded summary, and conditional lifecycle status. It does not independently recompute numerical gates. The final record always keeps `wholeLifecycleVerified:false`, `qualified:false` and external completion required. `candidateLifecycleVerified:true` plus actual observer exit 0 and caller-measured full elapsed under 300 seconds are necessary for lifecycle acceptance; scientific qualification remains subject to the distinct saved review. A valid completed numerical failure may return Node exit 0 and retains its reported scientific flag; lifecycle success does not change that flag.

`outputAccountingComplete` starts false and becomes true only after complete process closure and a successful terminal directory scan following the attempted private receipt write. A failed private receipt write still fails the candidate even if the following scan accounts for all bytes actually present. When accounting cannot finish, final raw/result/private fields and `accountedTotalResultBytes` remain null; only explicitly named last-observed samples and `observedPrefixResultBytes` are available. Those samples cannot certify a final cap. Budget/summary sizes converge together before writing; both final budget and stdout writes must return the full intended byte length. These lifecycle fields belong in separate lifecycle metadata; numerical `schema.json` and `BYTE-ACCOUNTING.json` remain byte-identical.

The caller retains the complete observer stdout summary, its actual final exit and externally measured full elapsed. It validates the inner summary/budget hashes, exact byte lengths, source identity, complete accounting and candidate status before making its own conditional candidate. `results/capture.json` is a sanitized public receipt: private paths, PIDs, executable/profile ownership and raw diagnostics are never included. Its final stdout summary binds that receipt by SHA-256 and exact length. The caller's combined result total equals the observer's exact accounted total plus caller stderr, the permanent capture lock, its public receipt and final stdout summary. The observer stdout summary is already included in the inner total and its captured persistence is counted once. Exact receipt/summary sizes converge together; missing inner final accounting leaves the combined final total null. The retained private captures remain available for failure diagnosis.

The final authority conjunction is `capture.json.candidateWholeLifecycleVerified:true`, a complete hash/length-matching final capture stdout summary, and the tool's actual completed capture exit0. The capture records always retain `wholeLifecycleVerified:false` and `qualified:false`; the independent saved reviewer applies the external conjunction and numerical gates. A tool yield/session ID is not a final exit observation. No direct observer/relay launch and no retry is permitted. An interrupted `Popen` assignment, hard exit, truncated capture or unverified detached identity is a failed attempt; the caller cannot claim universal descendant cleanup and must preserve evidence for independently verified owned cleanup.
