Where the calculation stops

We tried to save time by reusing a list of nearby drones. Before we could compare speeds, a larger movement brought some interactions into a region the airflow formula does not support. The calculation stopped as designed.

Checking saved evidence…

These are eight prescribed test states, not a flight simulation. The dots mark 1,000 drone centers. Changing the state shows the exact saved test positions. This test was designed to compare computer cost and numerical agreement, but stopped before the timing panel. It cannot establish real-world airflow accuracy or safe flight.

What the saved evidence says

Twelve small baseline/shortlist control pairs matched. Six large candidate states succeeded; the seventh was refused. The large baseline sequence never ran, and no speed comparisons completed.

The refusal records 186 unsupported source-to-rotor interactions. That is not 186 drones or 186 collisions. This view shows all saved centers; it does not identify the exact rejected interaction pairs.

We preserved both stopped attempts and did not shrink the movement to make the test pass. These results do not establish physical accuracy, safe flight or a novel speed improvement.
