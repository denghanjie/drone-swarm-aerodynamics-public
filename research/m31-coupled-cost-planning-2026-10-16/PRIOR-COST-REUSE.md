## What is genuinely new in the proposed comparison

The new law makes source amplitude depend on each vehicle's solved total load and uses the complete saved unequal motor-lag nominal forces. The latest transfer established admitted fixed coefficients and agreement of ordered versus synchronous solutions on the four selected historical states. The old fixed-strength complete-query timings did not measure that coupled fixed point, its coefficient-to-solve split, or the cost of order construction and residual verification.

A new **2×2 comparison** can therefore answer a distinct question: exact all-pairs versus conservative grid coefficient construction, crossed with ordered versus synchronous coupling solves, at identical model, input, numerical tolerance and complete-refresh scope. The crossing matters: all-pairs/Picard versus grid/ordered alone would combine two changes and would not identify their individual or combined costs. The numerical agreement of old grid and all-pairs implementations is reusable prior evidence; a controlled timing comparison of these four newly coupled paths was not found in the inspected studies.

The new contribution would be a workload-specific measured implementation result, not discovery of spatial hashing, exact support screening or topological evaluation. Source-dependent feedback changes the algebraic workload, not the novelty status of those established methods. It also does not authorize another series of minor E/L or positive-threshold variants.

## What can be reused, with explicit adaptation

- **Grid mechanism:** historical `outputs/milestone-26-source/grid.cjs` is used by the M28/M31 fixed-source comparisons; `outputs/milestone-31-source/grid.cjs` is also available. They provide Map-backed cell bins, conservative box-cell enumeration, strict Math.hypot radius filtering and ascending IDs. Existing 1.5-m cell/3-m search choices are a reasonable pinned starting point. Reuse the simple construction rather than inventing a new index or sweeping cell size after exposure. Keep directed interactions, self-exclusion, canonical source order, unbounded cell occupancy and exact final center criterion.
- **Critical current guard adaptation:** the newly frozen all-pairs census marks symbol 11 when a directed center distance exceeds 512 m or a displacement component exceeds 257 m, **before** outside-neighbor rejection. A neighbor-only grid would skip those far invalid pairs and change admission. Preserve this with a conservative whole-input proof that every pair satisfies the guard, or an exact fallback when that proof is inconclusive. A conservative failure to prove the guard is not itself grounds to reject an otherwise valid snapshot. Likewise, bound cell-coordinate arithmetic so enormous coordinates cannot cause a nonadvancing enumeration loop.
- **Exact geometry contract:** preserve the parenthesized historical xyzw rotation, original rotor/motor identities, all four rotors, full near/invalid policy and exact source-ID accumulation. Previous screening code has distinct tolerance/tilt/coordinate/first-error contracts; DOT support is not a drop-in replacement. Its retained huge-translation false cull and coordinate-domain repair are useful adverse design evidence, not permission to broaden current admission silently.
- **Reference and correctness evidence:** use the completed coupled transfer's pinned states, symbol tensor, positive CSR and forces as the retained reference, with an independently reviewed coverage check for the new grid. Grid physical center tests will differ from N(N−1); distinguish actual executed tests from logical pair coverage. Do not report the baseline's exhaustive count as work actually executed by the grid. A complete classification/CSR comparison belongs to qualification even if producing the full audit tensor is separated from the timed runtime interface.
- **Timing protocol:** reuse balanced paired blocks, one declared warmup, fresh geometry/index/coefficients per complete query, source/input pins, exclusive invocation lock, failure reserve, fixed stopping and successful-output comparison. Keep validation/persistence outside identically defined compute timers, but measure/report scientific evidence I/O separately. Include load assembly and required residual work equally. Reuse reporting logic, not old timing values or old unspent budgets. All four paths need matched instrumentation so allocation/diagnostic differences are not misattributed to the index or solve method.
- **Do not carry over positive-omission bounds:** the old per-receiver error budget was derived for fixed source strengths and equal nominal forces. With coupled amplitudes, a changed load changes downstream emission and can propagate through the dependency graph. Old one-step inflow/load budgets cannot simply be relabeled as a global coupled error guarantee. The proposed conservative grid retains every in-model positive term, so no approximate omission extension is needed for this next question.
