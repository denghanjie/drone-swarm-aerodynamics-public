What this comparison can tell us

The change includes all later consequences of adding controller memory: altered motion can change neighbors, steering and controller commands. Each flock tracks its own changing Boids request. The memory is bounded, but it is not a fully saturation-aware anti-windup design. Both arrangements use a 0.005 s integration step. This allocation includes no finer-resolution comparison. The seeds are fixed examples, not a random sample of a population. Each arm follows its own changing Boids request; differences in own-request error do not establish tracking of an identical reference.

A native model refusal is distinct from an endpoint contact/vehicle-limit stop. For refusals, the outcome time is the last completed endpoint; the readout separately gives the attempted trial time, status and reason. Refused trial positions are never inserted into the completed path. If a saved run stops, its pane becomes empty after that point. We do not extend the path or keep a stopped drone hovering. Differences beyond that time cannot be treated as a matched flight comparison. Neither arm is proof of real-flight safety, physical aerodynamic accuracy or a new research contribution.
