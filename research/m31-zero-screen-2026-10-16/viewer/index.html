WHAT CHANGED

Skip work with no effect on the output.

Z retained every positive contribution while still computing unused approximation decisions. E removes that bookkeeping. L then moves a radial distance calculation after a cheaper downstream check, so many sources exit before needing it.

E and L preserved all 17 output values bit for bit relative to Z on every valid recorded input. An independent full calculation agreed within the frozen precision limits. No positive contribution was omitted.

WHAT WE LEARNED

Fewer operations do not guarantee useful speed.

L avoided roughly half the classifier’s center radial norms. Its paired median time improved by about 3.86% and 2.24% relative to E, below the preset 5% threshold. E/Z also missed its joint criterion.

This is a limited engineering result, not a new algorithm or physical validation. Prior DOT work already explored related screening. The next question is whether browser arithmetic can preserve the necessary forces.
