# Next decision after the small solver verification

The result justifies further computational testing of an ordered solver within the assumed steady law. It does not justify integrating unvalidated force changes into a public flight claim. Keep this campaign closed and preserve all frozen files.

The next most informative computational question is a fresh, prospectively defined set of geometries with substantially different dependency depth and density, comparing complete coefficient construction plus solving against fair baselines. Include a condition where the tighter weighted ordinary contraction bound is at least 1, or explicitly report that admitted geometry prevents it; a synthetic coefficient stress case may test the algebra but must be separately labeled as lacking admitted wake geometry. Measure full computation after correctness verification, with repeated timing, warmup and specified hardware. Do not choose fixtures by looking for favorable speedups.

Before a scaling campaign, decide whether all-pairs coefficient construction dominates and which spatial neighbor mechanism is an appropriate exact baseline. Preserve four receiver roots, explicit near-field rejection and the Boids/dynamics scope. Frozen snapshots can test a kernel, but cannot establish moving-flock fidelity or real-time operation.

The parallel physical gap is still traceable fixed-command baseline/target force data and an independently justified source-strength/shape relationship. NASA methods were admitted but no numerical target acquired. These physical and computational questions must remain separate. No new numerical campaign is allocated by this decision; its protocol and scope must be saved first.
