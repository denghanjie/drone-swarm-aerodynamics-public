## Controls and ownership

Controls are literal source-defined full17states; JSON retains string tokens for every value to preserve NaN/Infinity/signed-zero. `results/control-ID.json` contains id, n, all state's tokens, complete A/L/T bundle literals: symbols(uint8 list), offsets, sources, K, a, input, admission, fullGraph, certificate, symbolCounts, geometryWork, ordinaryLmax, assemblyStats when present. No control solves or timings. A/L/T canonical fields compare exactly (nonfinite metadata serialized null; token records preserve intended inputs). Assembly instrumentation is separate and may differ.

Ownership records retain literal canonical results before and after subsequent interleaved dense/sparse L/T builds; they do not rely solely on hashes. Frozen sequence and chunk controls are in controls.cjs. Returned arrays must own their storage independently of reused scratch. Unsupported model controls may reject consistently; control agreement is not proof of physical validity.
