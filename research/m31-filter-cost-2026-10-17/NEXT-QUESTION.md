# Next question: transfer to changing local Boids geometry in the browser

This is a planning direction, not a frozen allocation or permission to execute a new suite. The present scientific and display allocations are closed.

The static Node benefit justifies asking whether it survives actual Chrome execution on fresh positions produced by a local three-dimensional Boids process. That should bring the investigation back toward moving group behavior rather than treating a static lattice benchmark as the final goal. The comparison must continue to charge the complete declared calculation, with the same exact hierarchy as its filter-off ablation.

A new prospective package should first define the generation/geometry contract and what the current aerodynamic model can admit. Record every scheduled state, including model rejections and unresolved outputs. Never discard difficult Boids frames or choose successful frames after exposure. Report admission rate separately from conditional calculation accuracy and speed. Genuine local interaction rules and a visible evolving group should be demonstrated, while distinguishing kinematic Boids motion, flight dynamics and aerodynamic feedback.

The browser source/runtime path needs its own bounded admission and correctness checks. Node results do not establish browser equality or timing. Import adapters, preparation, rounding and data transport must be reviewed; exact source publication must precede any new numerical generation or scoring. Existing failed browser/transport allocations remain closed and cannot simply be retried. Preserve a fresh failure as evidence.

Before selecting fixtures, independently challenge whether the proposed transfer actually advances the original 5,000-drone accuracy–computation question. The present 3.891-second dense180 result leaves a large absolute-cost gap. Exact shared-work reuse, batching and a justified approximation with an error budget remain alternatives. A transfer result alone will not provide independent physical measurements or algorithmic novelty; verified literature and those separate validation obligations remain necessary.
