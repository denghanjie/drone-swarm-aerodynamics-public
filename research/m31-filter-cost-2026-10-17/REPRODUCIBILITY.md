```sh
export RESEARCH_NODE=/absolute/path/to/the/pinned/node
export RESEARCH_PRIVATE_RUN_DIR=/tmp/m31-filter-cost-private-run
/absolute/path/to/the/pinned/python3 research/m31-filter-cost-2026-10-17/source/supervise.py kernel
/absolute/path/to/the/pinned/python3 research/m31-filter-cost-2026-10-17/source/supervise.py checker
```

The checker starts only after successful contained kernel completion, process closure and unchanged source/runtime/input checks. A zero exit status alone does not establish scientific qualification. Inspect the independent report, control outcomes, full recorded timings and common scientific equality. Report the practical benefit gate separately. Never remove attempt markers or overwrite failed evidence to rerun this allocation. A third-party reproduction needs its own newly declared attempt namespace and independently verified source receipt.

The fixed inputs and call schedule are in spec.json. Timing starts after shared literal ingestion and ends at the complete method record, including its proof/work metadata. Serialization/hashes are separately reported harness work. The saved index and journal pin six representative scene shards, every completed call and any retained first mismatch. Independent reference scenes retain reconstructed scientific evidence. Private raw logs remain private; scoped sanitized public copies and hashes document provenance.
