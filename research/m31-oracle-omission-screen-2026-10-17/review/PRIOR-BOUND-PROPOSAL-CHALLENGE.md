# Challenge to the proposed omission experiment

Desk recommendation, 2026-10-16 UTC. The parent reports six replication numerical-integrity/equivalence/admission passes and six speed-gate failures; the baseline remains accepted. That report is the result premise here. No raw target/result data, source import, scientific execution, browser, or new allocation was used. This memo supersedes the earlier recommendation to build the complete omission solver/interface immediately.

## Decision

**A small node-bound soundness/opportunity probe is the better next experiment.** My earlier proposal asked for substantial solver, certificate, interface and stopping-policy work before establishing that the current tree supplies usable groups. That order is premature after another exact optimization failed its speed gates.

The leaf-size concern is plausible, not a demonstrated impossibility. `tree.cjs` stops splitting at at most eight members. The tree partitions source centers; it does not align groups with each receiver rotor's down/support boundaries or source-axis variation. At a receiver-dependent active-bound frontier, a leaf can therefore mix upstream, near-region, active and cutoff cases. The existing traversal then scans that leaf: it cannot discover a favorable two-source subgroup unless a new refinement rule is introduced. Some internal nodes or eight-member leaves may nevertheless be uniformly admissible. We have not measured either outcome.

There is also an important correction to my wording: **upper bounds do not require every member to have a strictly positive coefficient.** The adopted construction allows admitted radial/projection cutoff zeros under its stronger down-domain assumptions. A strictly positive *lower* bound needs the stronger all-active conditions. Requiring that stronger condition for an upper-only probe would reject legitimate opportunities. Conversely, permitting zeros does not permit hiding an unsupported near-field member, an earlier guard failure, a cap uncertainty or a possibly positive backward edge.

The probe should answer three separate questions: is the implemented node upper bound sound under its stated premises; does the current tree expose multi-source groups with genuinely positive members; and is any apparent shortage caused by leaf granularity rather than the formula? It should not yet claim a certified load tolerance or net speedup.

## Numerical tiers

I support replacing the proposed middle grid with **`1e-8`, `1e-4`, `1e-2`** for this first opportunity probe. The exact tier preserves the existing target; `1e-4` tests a materially looser numerical contract; `1e-2` is a deliberately permissive stress ceiling that can reveal whether the mechanism has any practical room. Spending the first diagnostic on `1e-6` is less informative if the main obstacle is structural admission or very loose boxes. The three points are a coarse screen, not a resolved accuracy–cost frontier. A later positive result could justify filling its gaps prospectively.

Keep two meanings distinct. Future load tolerances would use the current normalizations: rotor error divided by `mg/4`, collective/world-force by `mg`, and world-moment by `mg*R`. Thus `1e-2` means 1% of those reference scales, not 1% relative error in every actual load or moment, and says nothing about flight safety or centimetre-scale trajectory error. In this probe there is no solved candidate and no global `Kbar` certificate, so those labels must be called **prospective load tiers**. Passing a row-budget proxy is not passing that load tier.

## What to implement and record

Implement only a proof-producing node query, its deterministic traversal/refinement controls and an independent checker. Do not add a candidate solver, Q-current load interface, adaptive stopping schedule, moving-state reuse or rendering.

The producer independently prepares current geometry and node summaries. For each receiver rotor it uses the adopted sufficient down-domain enclosure, actual axis boxes and monotone factors to produce a rigorous nonnegative `U` with `K_irj <= U` for every admitted member. Include allowed cutoff/projection zeros; keep lower bounds zero. Prove earlier guards, self exclusion, whole-source-cube caps and ordering before marking the group eligible. Split or use exact fallback when those sufficient premises fail. Loose `U > 128` or `U*max(beta) > 128` is uncertainty, not a proved cap violation. At these small sizes the all-pairs count already discharges the actual two-million-positive-term cap; it does not discharge the other admission obligations.

For every eligible node, record its member count, bounds and proof status, without treating overlapping ancestor/descendant nodes as jointly omitted. For a fixed opportunity-selection summary, retain the previous proposed rule: existing traversal order, multi-source bounded terminals only, and a per-row cumulative worst-source-amplitude mass budget `tau/16`, where

`E_C = U_C * sum_C(beta_j) / nu_ir`, using `0 < y_j <= 1`.

This produces a disjoint row partition separately for T8 and T2 at each tier. The `1/16` factor is a frozen screening heuristic; it is not established by the load certificate. Also publish the unthresholded eligible-node mass distribution and exact-reference aggregate comparison, so failure of this particular proxy is not confused with absence of admissible groups. Do not tune the factor within the probe.

The independent audit checks every asserted member bound and admission status, then identifies genuinely positive coefficients in selected groups. Report at least:

- Rows with an eligible multi-source node; selected disjoint node/member counts; selected actually positive terms; groups containing at least two actually positive terms; selected all-zero members and groups.
- For nodes with positive exact aggregate, the rigorous upper mass and independently enclosed exact coefficient sum at `y=1`, with their gap/ratio and reference uncertainty. For all-zero groups, report that classification instead of an undefined ratio.
- Refusal reasons, including down/near-domain overlap, caps, order and arithmetic range; distinguish a failed sufficient box proof from an actual scalar rejection.
- Extra T2 nodes, bound evaluations, arithmetic/guard work, exact fallbacks and any per-member work required by the producer. Reference-audit work is separate. No free reference truth may establish producer eligibility or select nodes.

No timing-derived speed claim is necessary in this probe. Counts are opportunity diagnostics, not estimates that a BigInt bound costs the same as a scalar query. Large avoided counts still require the later complete-cost comparison.

## Minimum soundness controls

Freeze a small source-defined fixture catalogue before target reads, with independent scalar truth and exact/adjacent-binary64 boundary witnesses where relevant. The minimum controls are:

1. A compact, ordered, nonself group with at least two positive members, ensuring a nonvacuous successful upper-bound path.
2. An admitted positive/zero mixture, ensuring upper-only eligibility does not accidentally demand a positive common lower bound.
3. A near-region rejecting member mixed into a seemingly small-contribution group, including a later projection-zero condition, ensuring rejection precedence is preserved.
4. An earlier geometry-guard failure combined with an apparent neighbor/support zero, ensuring a later zero cannot bypass the guard.
5. A possibly positive member with forbidden order, ensuring grouping cannot hide a backward edge.
6. A conservative cap-upper-bound failure whose members do not establish a cap violation, ensuring the result is unresolved/refinement, not proved rejection.
7. An arithmetic refusal case, ensuring overflow/domain failure cannot return an eligible finite bound.

Use membership/self and bound-corruption variants of the same fixtures: omit a member, duplicate one, include self, mismatch the current input identity, or shrink an accepted upper endpoint. The checker must reject them. Existing exact-source regressions remain applicable; do not multiply target cases until a favorable outcome appears.

The checker must use an independent reference path/enclosure and validate all members, not sample them or merely check interval overlap. If reference precision cannot decide a comparison within the fixed budget, report unresolved. Finite fixture/audit success alone is not a general proof: the source-level inclusion argument and arithmetic qualifications must also be reviewed before the probe is called sound.

## Decision after the probe

Any unsound bound, hidden rejection/order violation, false admission, or missed corruption stops the probe's scientific interpretation. Repair qualification before discussing opportunity or performance.

For a fixed engineering go/no-go screen, I recommend requiring selected groups with at least two audited positive members on **both** moving states and coverage of at least **10% of their positive scalar terms** at one predeclared proxy tier, with all soundness checks passing. Count only positive terms inside such groups toward this screen. The 10% is an explicit opportunity hurdle motivated by the desired later roughly 10% complete-cost improvement; it is not a theorem that smaller coverage cannot pay, nor that this coverage will pay. Report all measured coverage, including lower tiers and failures.

- If T8 passes that screen, a separately reviewed solver/certificate/interface experiment is justified, using the accepted baseline and shadow-omission control.
- If only T2 passes, investigate that fixed refinement's complete cost before adopting it. Do not build the full new interface on an assumption that refinement is free.
- If neither passes even at the `1e-2` stress ceiling, stop this proposed upper-only/tree policy at this budget rule. The unthresholded mass and refusal data should determine whether a different bound, hierarchy or certificate deserves a new desk proposal. Do not widen tolerances or search policies inside the same allocation.
- If the probe reaches its resource limit, report incomplete. There is no automatic extension or implied failure/pass of the mathematical idea.

Because `y=1` and `tau/16` are conservative proxies, a negative opportunity result is specific to this screen; it does not prove a later candidate-dependent scheme impossible. That limitation is preferable to spending on an entire solver before checking whether the intended cheap mechanism has visible headroom. A positive result still establishes neither `Kbar` tightness, certified physical-load error, total runtime savings nor 5,000-moving-browser feasibility.
