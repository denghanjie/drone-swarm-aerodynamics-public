# Next distinct question — planning only

Current candidate/reviewer allocations are closed. This note creates no new numerical allocation and authorizes no rerun. The overall research goal remains active.

Ask whether **full-wrench temporal reuse in feedback motion** can reduce joint-load evaluations without a consequential sampled trajectory change on the same exposed 16-drone seed. Compare the every-RK-stage full model with a fixed 10 ms refresh at the same three timesteps. Six paths maximum would suffice for an initial diagnostic; do not add 64 drones, extra seeds, predictors, adaptive scheduling or the old analytical envelope in this question.

A candidate contract worth reviewing is to cache the four dimensionless solved rotor-load ratios x = T / f0 at each joint own-state refresh, then use T = current nominal rotor state × cached ratio between refreshes. Compute the effective-minus-current-nominal collective force and full roll/pitch moment from these loads, rotating the collective force with the current orientation; retain nominal yaw torque, normal attitude/controller/motor dynamics and the existing projection scheme. This is explicitly a stale-ratio approximation; between refreshes it does not solve the instantaneous source/receiver closure. Preserve the full moment because removing it changed this seed's motion by 0.511 m.

Before implementation or execution: define refresh event ordering, treatment of an RK endpoint at a refresh boundary, cache ownership, exact baseline-repeat fields, same-state pre-refresh instantaneous-load diagnostics, denominator/tolerance definitions, rejection handling, call accounting, evidence/storage limits and a fresh frozen independent checker. Report trajectory differences and complete evaluation counts. Count observer/reference work separately and do not equate fewer calls with measured speedup. Existing exposed data may inform a prospective design, but cannot become fresh confirmation.

Why distinct: prior m31-airflow-cadence and m31-refresh-envelope studied prescribed two-drone M28 field/inflow paths with fixed nominal thrust and orientation. They did not feed this later source-total coupled closure into 3D Boids/rigid-body motion. Their allocations, failures, cadence conclusions and analytical-envelope results remain unchanged. The new question would test a different approximation in closed-loop motion; it would not retroactively validate those old methods.

Why not prioritize another browser benchmark now: the closed typed-assembly study already measured lean 5,000-drone queries around 0.475 s, and browser transport/monitor qualifications remain unresolved in their separate failed packages. A 10 ms refresh alone would still imply roughly 47.5 seconds of such query work per simulated second if that cost persisted; this arithmetic is a rough scale comparison, not a benchmark of the unimplemented method. The static Node measurements exclude moving-flight integration, steering, rendering and transport. Browser integration needs a separate admitted plan and must not reopen the failed GPU/transport allocations.

A successful small reuse diagnostic would still leave physical validation, unseen seeds, longer flights, scaling, real-time delivery and a novel publishable accuracy–computation result unresolved. Its purpose is to decide whether a more substantial, separately preregistered computation/accuracy study is warranted.
