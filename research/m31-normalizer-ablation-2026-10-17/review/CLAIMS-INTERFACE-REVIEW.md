## Closed-call correctness rationale

The method constructs a fresh exact-object map and a fresh candidate query closure for each call. Beta is keyed by the invocation-owned source object; nu is keyed by invocation-owned receiver object and rotor index. Both use the same rational operands and directed interval square roots as the baseline. The cache stores only after a successful calculation and returns frozen interval objects. It does not store exceptions or pair status, coefficient, cap, solver or certificate results.

All geometry guards, profile arithmetic, radial square root and K construction remain before beta and nu. Beta precedes nu, and both precede cap and normalizer decisions. A successful beta can therefore be retained across a later nu exception; both successful normalizers can be retained across a later cap/normalizer refusal. Early zero/refusal paths never access the normalizer operands. Unexpected exceptions propagate from the same qualified catch boundary.

The inspected method, rational, interval, tree, boxes and filter code does not assign into source/receiver force rationals or relevant constants during assembly. The worker's phase callback only appends timing observations. Cached objects are not exposed through that callback. Exact prepared objects and exported constant rationals are mutable representations, but the actual closed synchronous worker path has no state-mutating callback or concurrent consumer during the cache lifetime. The proof is scoped to that path; an arbitrary caller holding `createQuery()` while mutating supplied objects is not covered.

Both arms preserve the method label and scientific schema, so the arm/source envelope identifies the implementation. Pair/filter work counters must remain equal. They are not normalizer cost measurements. This source argument establishes a narrow semantics-preservation rationale, not runtime benefit, general API safety or performance for larger populations.

## Timing claim and decision

Primary timing starts immediately before `spawnSync` and ends after it returns. It charges process/module startup, identical source-manifest verification, raw-input ingestion, admission, cache creation/lookups/misses, unchanged computation, serialization, fsync/close and worker exit. Parent post-exit output hashing and journal bookkeeping are outside that pair window. Method-entry-to-return time is a separately labelled secondary observation. Generation and rendering are outside the paired cost.

This is deliberately narrower than the earlier advisory design's potential resource comparisons. Sampled process-group RSS establishes containment only; no per-arm CPU cost, per-arm memory peak, persistent-process throughput, browser cost or rendering speed claim is supported. Starting a fresh process for every measured arm also means that a finite result applies to this isolated-worker path, including its fixed validation/I/O overhead. No subtraction of supposedly common overhead is justified.

The decision uses the median of four exact rational candidate/baseline wall ratios and at least three strict wins on every one of six states. The required median is at most 0.9. Ratios are not ratios of separate medians. Frames share two trajectories; the protocol supplies a descriptive fixed-suite decision, not independent population samples or a confidence interval. All admission, nontriviality, numerical, equality, corruption, provenance and containment gates are additional requirements. A failed speed gate can coexist with correct equality; a correctness pass alone cannot support a speedup.

Repeated expressions suggest possible savings, but neither combined assembly timing from older work nor the cache hit counts identify a causal primitive bottleneck. Successful normalizer reuse reduces a repeated subcomputation; it leaves per-interaction work and worst-case interaction growth. No measured benefit is available in this source-only review.

## Failure handling and launch gates

The source requires the current public commit, exact manifest and receipt, active runtime pins, a permanent single-use attempt marker and canonical pinned files before scientific imports. The supervisor verifies committed and local closure before and after the stage; the runner and workers bind the same manifest. Environment preloads/search overrides are rejected or cleared. Python supervision/checking requires `-I -B`. Every new stage, generation, worker and direct-control batch is gated before the fixed 23:00 UTC cutoff; already-started bounded calls may finish afterward under the declared rule.

The guardian retains its owned process-group identity until the scientific runner and any sequential worker are gone, and kills the owned group on failure. Supervision permits at most one inherited-group worker beneath the kernel runner and no checker descendants. The wall/RSS/output claims remain sampled, bounded containment rather than hostile-process isolation or continuous peak measurement.

Kernel/checker limits remain 600 seconds each, guardian 610 seconds, sampled group RSS 1 GiB, worker timeout 60 seconds, kernel output 192 MiB, checker output 64 MiB, and combined logs/metadata 16 MiB. Individual records, generation files, index/check reports, journal and supervision reports have separate caps. Missing/extra files, runtime/source drift, worker errors, timeouts and output violations cannot pass. Partial artifacts and permanent attempts remain; there is no retry or post-exposure cap increase. Worker failure stderr is sanitized before being embedded in the public journal, separately from supervised raw-log redaction.

The supervisor's successful closure is not itself scientific success. The checker independently gates `passed`; its failures and any kernel failure must remain public failures. The public source receipt and launch gates have been reviewed prospectively, not asserted to exist already by this review.

## Claims boundary

The permissible prospective claim is a finite comparison of standard within-call memoization on six fresh 64-drone moving snapshots, with exact scientific equality and independent numerical validation if all registered gates actually pass. The unchanged maxDrones=180 rejects a 5,000-drone input. This allocation does not test 5,000-drone admission, physical accuracy, coupled aerodynamic feedback, integration convergence, general tilted/cyclic solvability, browser portability or real-time operation. Fifty-one displayed pose frames per run are not fifty-one certified force evaluations.

Memoization is established engineering practice. No new general algorithm or novelty claim is supported; no new literature was searched to imply one. The model's numerical 1e-8 bounds do not become physical prediction-error bounds. Previous failed results and closed allocations retain their original status. Updating the recorded-flight viewer from future saved evidence requires its own honest evidence/display checks and does not extend the scientific allocation.
