What this can tell us

The radial-flow interpretation assumes rotational symmetry and no mean swirling motion. Actual velocity calibration and the stitching of measurement sections remain unresolved. An imbalance does not prove that air violates conservation; it could expose a limitation in that interpretation or in the measurements.

The samples were already examined and are a retained grid taken every fourth original row and column. This is not a new independent experiment. It does not improve the wake model, establish a safe swarm, or demonstrate novelty.
