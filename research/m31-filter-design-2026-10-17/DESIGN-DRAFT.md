Its combined traversal/scalar/assembly stage dominates; it does not isolate the cost of any primitive. This is a hypothesis, not a measured speedup or novelty claim.

Candidate scope: scalar zero classification only, after unchanged exact raw-state preparation/admission. Prepared native dyadic geometry is decoded directly from its frozen binary64 words into singleton intervals; do not round an exact rational expression into an unqualified point. Threshold expressions are interval expressions over the same constant words. Use finite outward binary64 add/subtract/multiply/divide and exact endpoint comparisons. Every uncertainty or arithmetic range/domain failure calls the unchanged exact query. The global guard, hierarchy, positive profile, roots, loads and four accuracy tolerances remain unchanged.

A fast zero is allowed only if all earlier guards and branch exclusions are proved. Otherwise exact fallback must occur, even if a later geometric exclusion looks decisive. The ordered candidates are neighbor, upstream, down-cut, near-outside, radial and projection. Near-field invalidity must never be hidden by zero projection. Radial residual retains actual near-unit axes: d-(d dot axis)*axis, with no unit-vector simplification. No sqrt is needed on the fast path.

Endpoint argument: correctly rounded binary64 +,-,*,/ on finite operands is enclosed by the adjacent representable values around its rounded result, including gradual underflow. NaN, infinity, an infinite adjacent endpoint, malformed interval and a denominator enclosing zero refuse the filter. Signed zero is interpreted as exact zero but both nextafter directions must enclose subnormal results. Special exact identities may tighten enclosures only with an explicit argument and independent tests. No fast-math or fused operation substitution is allowed. This argument is conditional on the declared runtime semantics; finite tests cannot prove all platform behavior.

Competing approaches include exact shared center/projection/normalizer caching, batching and less expensive exact dyadic operations. These could win when filtering overhead or ambiguity dominates. ## Integration and coverage caveats added before execution

The exact and fast prepared structures supplied to the internal wrapper must originate from the same admitted adapter result in the same invocation. The experiment runner constructs both locally from those identical word arrays; the independent checker must reconstruct and validate the full binding. The wrapper is not a separately exposed raw-input admission API. No re-use across changed state is allowed.

Unconditional widening sacrifices tightness. Exact zero products and cancellation can create intervals straddling zero, so same-plane upstream zeros and exact threshold equalities can fall back. The closed suite's remaining logical zero count is only an opportunity count, not predicted filter coverage or a time-saving estimate. New evidence must report hit and fallback counts by branch, including whether an expected witness was absent. No threshold tweaking is authorized after observing a result.
