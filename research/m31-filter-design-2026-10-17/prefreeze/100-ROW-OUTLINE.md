# Preserved initial finite-suite outline

The initial proposal was 100 ordered rows: 32 next-neighbor word rows over 16 IEEE edge words, 32 arithmetic rows (8 each add/subtract/multiply/divide), and 36 full-raw pair queries. Fifteen controls covered enclosure failures, zero/preparation/fallback mistakes, row coverage and journal binding. No full load solve or timing claim was proposed.

Before source freeze, the parent requested 8 square rows because square has a distinct zero-crossing branch. The final prospective suite therefore has 108 rows and 110 journal events. One matching square-lower-bound control is added, making 16 controls. This is a source-only expansion before any scientific execution.
