What this tells us

Ordering the calculation helps. But making one part faster cannot remove the much larger cost of rebuilding interactions. The measured construction phase includes finding candidates, sorting them, evaluating their effects and storing the coefficients.

What it does not tell us

This is one Apple M4 / Node process on four exposed snapshots. It excludes Boids steering, flight integration, rendering and transport. It establishes neither live browser performance nor accurate physical flight.
