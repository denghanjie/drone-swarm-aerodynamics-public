## Timing boundary and fairness

The common raw-word-to-complete-method-record boundary is meaningful if every invocation includes its own identity/domain validation, preparation, exact operand decoding, global proof when applicable, tree construction/serialization, all traversal and scalar work, canonical outcome assembly, candidate solve and complete certificate/load record construction. Both methods must start from the same immutable raw words; neither may reuse another trial's prepared objects, tree, pair answers or certificate.

**Concrete inherited-wrapper issue:** `methods.solve` currently validates scene size, unique stable IDs and state length outside `method`. Instrumenting only the current `method` function would omit that admission work from a claim that includes full raw-word admission. Put those per-call checks inside the timed operation, or prospectively narrow and name the boundary. The draft's intended complete boundary favors including them. Literal-to-binary64 conversion can remain common harness setup as declared. Classify one-time module/spec loading and constant construction explicitly as setup and retain their cost separately.

Start timing before the first included allocation/validation and stop only after the full return record is materialized. Timestamps must surround actual work rather than a cached-record lookup. Tree wire conversion, integer/rational encoding and creation of compact entries belong inside because they construct the method record. Final JSON serialization, hashes, output field checks and journal I/O may be outside if the final protocol names that choice consistently, reports their harness cost and limits the claim accordingly. The existing `run.cjs` also performs field validation and per-scene serialization/fsync outside `solve`; these costs should not silently disappear from the harness accounting.

Freeze a small common set of adjacent timestamp boundaries. Stage durations must be nonnegative integer nanoseconds whose exact sum is the outer method duration, including any named bookkeeping remainder. Assign preparation/decoding, proof/build, pair assembly and canonical admission/solve/certificate consistently. Specify the treatment of early model refusals and failed calls even though admission is expected. Do not subtract estimated clock overhead, time individual pairs, omit garbage collection occurring inside the interval or relabel measured wall time as CPU time.

Only the instrumentation, scheduling and required interface exposure are allocated changes to candidate code. Review the final source delta to ensure that arithmetic, iteration counts, scan order, pruning predicates and preparation caching remain unchanged. Exporting a callable method is not permission to tune it for the timing suite.

## Schedule, lifetime and descriptive statistics

For four repetitions, `AB, BA, BA, AB` gives each method two first and two second positions and balanced mean ordinal position. This reduces a simple order imbalance. It does not remove JIT compilation, allocator/GC state, cache effects, temperature/frequency changes, serialization carryover or unrelated host work. One declared warmup does not demonstrate steady-state execution. The scope is the observed fixed schedule on this runtime and host, with agents idle as required.

Freeze the scene order, warmup order, repetition numbering, representative full-record selection, per-trial serialization/hash/write order, and release/retention policy. Holding all earlier full records or a warmup object alive changes the heap seen by later trials. Releasing a method result immediately versus retaining its peer until after a pair can also matter. Apply one predetermined policy to both methods and report it. Do not introduce adaptive warmups, forced-GC treatment, retries, pauses or record selection after seeing timings.

Retain warmup timings and identities, all eight measured invocations per scene, and any failed/differing trial. Validate exact call order and lack of overlap. Define ratio direction, preferably `completeNs / hierarchyNs`, so a value above one means the hierarchy was faster. Report all four paired ratios and all individual method durations. For four observations the median is the mean of the two central sorted durations. A ratio of method medians and a median of paired ratios are different statistics and must be labeled separately. Avoid an undeclared pooled speedup whose weighting favors one size or family; no confidence or workload-generalization claim follows from four repetitions.

## Trial digest binding

Freeze the scientific record excluded from timing metadata and a cross-language canonical byte encoding. Object-key order, integer strings, booleans/nulls, array order and UTF-8/newline treatment must be explicit. Full method records differ in assembly/work; compare them against that method's retained record, while comparing the declared common payload across methods.

Compute every trial's digest from its actual newly returned record after the timer stops, before reuse or discard. The saved checker can independently re-encode the retained full record and require every corresponding warmup/measured digest to match. It must also check the exact trial count, ordinal/order, scene/method identity, timing partition and saved work counters. Hash mismatches or unexpected admission/qualification failures must remain visible and close the qualification rather than select a replacement representative.

Digest equality binds a claimed repeated record to the saved mathematical payload. It cannot independently establish that an omitted transient invocation ran, or authenticate elapsed time. Those measurement claims necessarily depend on the reviewed, source-pinned timing harness and its supervision. State that trust boundary rather than calling the digests an independent replay of timing.

## Scene coverage and expected admission

Literal size arithmetic confirms six scenes, 810 drone records and 527,776 logical nonself rotor-source pairs per method. The largest single scene contains 185,760 pairs. With one warmup and four measured calls per method/scene, the kernel performs 60 full method invocations and contains 5,277,760 planned logical pair slots across both methods. The checker should regenerate each fresh scene's full model once, not repeat the oracle for all identical trial digests.

Upright regular lattices give a useful controlled size/density comparison but a narrow workload family. In the sparse family, spacing above the neighbor cutoff is intended to eliminate all nonself support; it is principally an all-zero support-pruning workload. Dense aligned columns exercise coupled loads and a different proof/assembly balance. Exact literal states, prepared dyadics, guards, caps, source ordering and tolerances still require independent validation; this review does not establish actual admission or control witness availability.

All six scenes use regular geometry, the same orientation and a common motor-force pattern. They do not sample irregular/tilted formations, near/cap boundaries, global-proof fallback, invalid inputs or moving trajectories. Two family settings and three sizes are insufficient to infer an asymptotic rate, crossover for general scenes, browser behavior or 5,000-drone performance. Keeping this suite fixed is appropriate; do not enlarge or replace it adaptively after an unexpected refusal or slowdown. The earlier robustness checkpoint remains separate evidence and must not be rerun here.

## Scene-at-a-time independent checking

The inherited checker retains the full `expected` list, loads the entire saved document, deep-copies that whole document for each control, and accumulates reference evidence. Merely changing oracle regeneration to a one-scene loop does not bound the combined heap. The largest active scene can still hold 185,760 pair outcomes plus lookup keys, exact coefficient fractions, geometry, plans, root/load evidence, candidate records and a mutation copy. Persistent helper caches and output serialization buffers also need accounting.

Use a frozen scene lifecycle: obtain one bounded saved paired record; independently regenerate all current raw/prepared operands and every nonself pair; validate both complete and hierarchy plus all their trial digests/timings; select/apply the allocated local controls using the same core; serialize retained evidence; then release the scene target, plans and mutated record before advancing. Keep only bounded global identity/control-witness/summary state. Account for or prospectively clear persistent helper caches in a fixed manner. Do not assume that dropping one variable releases objects still captured by validators, reports or mutation closures.

The final JSON/journal identity check needs a bounded design too: either a reviewed streaming decoder, or an exact incremental byte/digest reconstruction from bounded journal records under a frozen serialization contract. Loading a maximum-size whole document and repeatedly deep-copying it defeats the proposed memory schedule. Likewise, evidence should be streamed or assigned an explicit retained-size bound; the final report must not accumulate the discarded oracle objects indirectly.

Control selection can follow fixed scene order and retain the first independently proved witness for each newly declared control. Run scene-local mutations while that scene's oracle is live. Missing/duplicate/reordered scene and trial-order/digest/timing controls should use the same global sequence/measurement validators as baseline validation. A missing required witness fails the cycle. Do not replace independent pair, membership or signed-load checks with digest/count equality, reduce reference precision, or rerun the previous fixtures/16-control allocation to make memory fit.

## Static resource decision still required

The proposed wall/RSS limits are explicit failure bounds, not proven feasibility. A source-only review can identify allocations and representation limits; it cannot guarantee the runtime or peak Python heap without a conservative argument. No numerical probe is authorized to choose these limits.

There is a concrete mismatch in simply carrying forward the old output caps: two full method records for 810 drones contain 1,620 load records. At the inherited 96 KiB per-load cap, their permitted envelope alone is **159,252,480 bytes (151.875 MiB)**, already above a 128 MiB kernel/journal cap before entries, rows, trees and metadata. This is an upper-envelope mismatch, not a prediction of the actual output size. Derive tighter prospective per-field bounds from the fixed representation, change the retained encoding, or amend the budgets before freeze; retain an explicit oversize failure either way. Checker scheduling does not resolve this output issue.

The final resource argument must include all 60 method calls, warmups, trial digest/serialization work, completed/partial journals, full independent pair/reference work, controls, evidence serialization, raw/public logs, source/runtime rechecks and cleanup. A stage failure remains a retained unqualified outcome. A small Node result may show an observed method-duration difference under this boundary; it cannot establish end-to-end application savings, physical accuracy, coupled trajectory validity, novelty or completion of the original goal.
