A targeted scan of every new text artifact found no private home/temp/attachment path, private-key marker or common credential-token pattern. Source/data category review and visual inspection of all four QA screenshots found research content only. Executable hashes, Node/V8/platform/architecture versions, monotonic durations and sampled resource observations remain as declared reproducibility context. No hardware-general performance claim is made.

The root README navigation is updated to the current summary/viewer and preserves historical entries. It also clarifies the limited experiment process IDs retained in newer records. No frozen scientific source, model, raw result, reference or original supervision artifact was modified during closeout.
