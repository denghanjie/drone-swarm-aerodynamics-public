How to read the boxes

Teal boxes contain sources whose contribution to the selected rotor is proved zero. Amber boxes still require individual checks. Yellow marks the selected rotor. Dense and sparse groups test different amounts of useful screening.

What remains open

These groups contain at most 216 drones. A faster exact calculation here does not establish real-time performance for 5,000 moving drones. Local 3D Boids, coupled flight dynamics, physical validation and a defensible novelty claim remain part of the larger goal.

This is a small static Node benchmark. JIT, garbage collection, cache, thermal and other host activity can affect timings. Independent checks validate the calculations and saved timing arithmetic; they cannot independently remeasure the clocks.
