## Compact contract and claims

The final contract resolves the draft's principal protocol questions: both methods prepare independently from identical raw words, share the prepared scalar/certificate implementation, and must agree exactly on preparation, canonical model outcome/witness, complete positive coefficient sequence, candidate bits and common certificate/load payload. Independent full-model validation remains mandatory. Query/entry indices are original array indices; tree/prepared/solve/load identities and final witness indices are stable IDs. This conversion is now explicit.

Compact scan/leaf complements are zero claims checked by the independent oracle; scalar-call counts or a zeroCount field alone are not evidence. Deterministic disjoint/exhaustive membership, canonical refusal priority and complete enumeration after refusal remain required. Completed false global proof alone selects complete fallback before any tree is built. An enclosure/tree/serialization exception fails the stage explicitly, rather than becoming a model rejection or implicit fallback.

Counters measure selected algorithmic work; they omit neither attempted global proof nor fallback scalar work, but do not purport to measure all arithmetic/build comparisons or total cost. Numerical qualification at maximum64 drones, exact zero coverage and scalar-call reductions cannot establish 5,000-drone speed, browser execution, coupled dynamics, physical accuracy or novelty. The final protocol/model preserve those limitations and distinguish verified refusals from load admissions.

One final checker-review requirement was communicated to the checker author and parent: because agreement is itself checked, a mutation to one method can fail merely through method inequality. Acceptance of each fault probe should demonstrate its intended core/oracle failure family with its proved witness, not only any error/equality mismatch. This is a control-interpretation requirement for final checker review; this reviewer has not executed or fully reviewed the new checker.
