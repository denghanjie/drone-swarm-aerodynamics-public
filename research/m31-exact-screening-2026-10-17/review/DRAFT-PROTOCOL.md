## Common output and comparison contract

1. Bind each method result to the same raw scene identity and its own complete preparation/global validation. Both methods start from raw state; neither obtains another method's prepared state, tree, baseline answer or admission decision for free. Share code, not precomputed comparison results. Reusing a method's own already prepared state on fallback is reasonable and must be declared consistently.
2. Define a checked per-(receiver stable ID, rotor) partition of every nonself source exactly once. Each entry is either an evaluated singleton or a subtree with a sound zero witness. Internal children must partition their canonical membership; duplicate, overlapping, omitted, stale and foreign membership fail. Explicitly remove self from both coverage and subtree counts even when the underlying tree node contains it. Global invalidity retains every preparation result and explicitly accounts for all skipped planned pairs.
3. Exact legacy zero-reason arrays may be omitted, but the replacement must preserve the guarded model's admission/status obligations. A zero subtree is a proof that no member can have a higher-priority rejection or unresolved active coefficient. Retain every evaluated nonzero/refused outcome. Independently reconstruct tree geometry and validate witnesses against original exact prepared operands; a candidate-authored proof-success flag is insufficient.
4. Freeze canonical method comparison fields. For the same scene, baseline and hierarchy must agree on model status/first canonical refusal witness, complete positive stable-ID membership and canonical coefficient intervals. The common positive sequence must follow the same receiver/rotor/input-source order before native proposal or certification. With the same fixed arithmetic recipe, require matching recorded candidate bits and common certificate/load identities, or explicitly preregister which differing but sound certificate fields are allowed and how equivalence is checked. Do not apply numerical tolerances to interaction membership. Permuted-input scenes are separate inputs; same-scene method equality does not presume native bitwise invariance across permutations.
5. Preserve the four exact normalized error categories and signed three-component world loads. Separate independently verified model admission, certificate validity, tolerance success and method agreement. A verified rejection is not a load admission. Finite qualification must not turn unknown/error/partial results into zero coefficients or silently exclude them from denominators.

## Fallback, refusal and work accounting

A failed global bounding-box proof is failure of a sufficient optimization condition, not proof of model invalidity. Give it a separate explicit fallback status/reason. A fallback may still produce an admitted, rejected or unresolved model result after complete scalar evaluation. Likewise distinguish implementation arithmetic/resource failure from a physical/model rejection. Freeze which tree/proof exceptions trigger complete fallback and which close the attempt unqualified; unexpected exceptions cannot be repaired after observing results.

Scene refusal priority and witnesses must be selected in canonical scalar enumeration order after complete coverage, independently of tree visitation order. Node-zero proofs must not hide possible preparation, magnitude, near-field, width, cap or ordering failures. Every actual positive status counts toward the inherited term cap even when its interval lower endpoint is zero. Below-threshold finite scenes do not qualify the 2,000,000-term overflow boundary.

Record at least planned nonself membership, proof-zero membership, evaluated singleton count by status, actual positive terms, proof/traversal/build work, fallback reason, and attempted work before fallback. Explain whether fallback reuses preparation/tree work and avoid both hiding discarded work and double-counting final source coverage. Continue coverage after scalar refusal as proposed. For globally invalid scenes, preparation work and explicit skipped membership remain visible.

The qualification allocation may report exact coverage and operation counts. These are not total computational savings: a proof can cost more than the scalar calls it avoids. A future timing allocation must charge each method from raw input through its complete preparation, build/proof, admission, traversal, pair arithmetic, canonical ordering, solve, certification, output and fallback. Separate any checker/reference or orchestration exclusion identically. Do not credit removal of dense diagnostic tensors already absent from historical timed queries, or extrapolate supervision time to 5,000 drones.
