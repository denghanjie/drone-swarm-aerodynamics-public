How to read this

A box groups source drones. If every source in that box is provably outside a rotor's aerodynamic influence, the method skips those pair calculations. Unproved leaves still get checked one pair at a time. Different receivers and rotors can produce different proofs.

What remains open

These static groups contain at most 64 drones. The full research goal still requires 5,000 drones, local 3D Boids, flight dynamics, mutual aerodynamics, matched total-cost measurements, physical evidence and a defensible novelty claim.

Fewer scalar evaluations do not automatically mean a faster simulation. Exact bounds and tree traversal also cost computation. This checkpoint tests correctness and records selected work counts.
