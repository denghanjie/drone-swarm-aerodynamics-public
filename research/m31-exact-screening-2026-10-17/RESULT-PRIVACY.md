# Result publication privacy review

Results contain only synthetic drone states, numerical coefficients/load evidence, deterministic trees/counters, independent fault probes and bounded process supervision. Public logs contain no private path; both scientific stderr logs are empty. Process IDs, executable hashes and version labels are reproducibility metadata, not credentials. Display screenshots show only the local research page, no browser chrome or unrelated app content.

Recursive pattern scan found no machine-home/temp paths, attachment references, common secret token or private-key markers in this package. Source, reports and screenshots were inspected within their relevant audit scopes. No private raw log, environment dump, chat, pasted attachment, original screenshot or paper asset is added. The result manifest includes exact saved artifacts and inherited source/dependency pins; a separate receipt records exact-commit remote verification after publication.
