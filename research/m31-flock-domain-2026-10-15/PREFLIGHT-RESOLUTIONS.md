# Pre-execution design resolutions

The design memo is advisory; the final PROTOCOL.md and config.json govern this invocation. The final cap is 300 executable seconds, 64 MiB raw evidence (1 MiB reserved for failure), 110,000,000 independent nonself center checks and 864,000,000 combined eligible rotor classifications. No memory acceptance limit is adopted; the supervisor records child maximum RSS as a diagnostic only. The final quaternion norm reporting threshold is 1e-10. All times/ordering/metrics follow the final protocol and code, including case-major order and times 0/20/30/60 s. Advisory fade-region metrics and distinct-pair summaries are not adopted as acceptance measures. The complete indexed classifications preserve their identities for separately labeled later analysis.

The smallest inspected boundary is descriptive in native units and covers only predicates reached by eligible pairs. If no pair is eligible, Infinity serializes as null with a null event. This denotes no inspected boundary, not zero gap or an audit failure. No threshold is relaxed after exposure.

The final static review found no remaining blocker. No new pose decoding, geometry evaluation, field or integration has occurred for this campaign before this freeze. Runtime scientific files will remain unchanged after execution; later analysis and display files are separate artifacts.
