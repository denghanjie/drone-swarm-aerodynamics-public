It verifies all pinned primary case/time/offset identities, input hashes and byte slices; every frozen source hash; result/raw equality; exact raw inventory/bytes; zero saved field/integration counts; and supervised budgets. Expected accounting is 107,972,000 independent nonself center checks, 75,028,480 combined eligible rotor classifications, 40,076,924 raw bytes, and one 12.109875667-second scientific invocation. These expected totals cross-check the saved aggregates; scientific domain outcomes are independently derived rather than hard-coded.

These limits do not reduce byte/count/set coverage, but they must remain visible in publication. A domain pass concerns only the exact all-vehicle primary saved-time snapshots and does not authorize aerodynamic loads, change earlier numerical-refinement failures, establish physical validity, or imply continuous-flight safety.
