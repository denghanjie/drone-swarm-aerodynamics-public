WHAT WE LEARNED

A necessary condition is met.

At 0, 20, 30 and 60 seconds, the retained flock poses stay within the model’s tilt and near-wake exception limits. Two independently written geometry checks agree on every indexed classification. This permits planning a separate force-value comparison on these exact poses.

It does not qualify the unsaved parts of the flight. The earlier individual-path accuracy failures remain unresolved.

THE REMAINING GAP

A skipped interaction is an assumption.

The model intentionally skips some near-field geometry and truncates distant interactions. The gold links reveal the near-field skip policy for the selected receiver. We have not measured the forces lost by doing this.

No physical accuracy, real-time performance, novelty or 5,000-drone aerodynamic flight claim follows from this audit.
