What this can tell us

The radial-flow interpretation assumes rotational symmetry and no mean swirling motion. Actual velocity calibration and the stitching of measurement sections remain unresolved. Even two fields with balanced total flow can disagree on every surface. Cancellation measures here describe sampled weighted errors, not an experimentally proven mechanism.

The samples were already examined and are a retained grid taken every fourth original row and column. The model is unchanged. This is not a new independent experiment or an improvement to the earlier whole-field error score. Physical calibration, safe swarm flight and novelty remain unestablished.
