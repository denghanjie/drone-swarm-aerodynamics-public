The signed-error sum and absolute-error inequalities follow from linearity and the triangle inequality with positive trapezoid weights. They are ordinary mathematical identities, not research novelty. J is a weighted sum of absolute sampled nodal errors; its quadrature need not equal a continuous absolute-error integral across a sign crossing. Neither algebraic identities nor numerical audit agreement establish physical correctness.
