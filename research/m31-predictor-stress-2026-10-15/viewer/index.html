WHAT WE TESTED

Same samples, different predictions.

Hold5ms keeps the latest inflow. Predict5ms uses the latest two actual samples to extend their trend, with nonnegative clipping. Both calculate fresh airflow at exactly the same times. During the first 5 ms, the predictor has only one sample and uses a hold.

The 5 ms predictor met the sampled targets in five of six cases. In the source-pitch case its peak was 2.898 mN, above the 1.324 mN target; a 2.5 ms hold also missed that target. Predicting did not improve the first interval, and it did not beat the faster-refresh hold on every peak.

WHAT THIS CAN ESTABLISH

A model comparison with clear limits.

Two-sample prediction is established prior art. Independent formulas check all vehicle inflows and the receiver’s loads; agreement checks implementation, not aerodynamic truth.

Source wake strength is fixed at nominal hover, even while sources tilt or receive airflow from other sources. These CPU timings include all-vehicle fields and receiver-only loads. Flight dynamics, Boids, GPU work and rendering are absent. The 5,000-drone research goal remains open.
