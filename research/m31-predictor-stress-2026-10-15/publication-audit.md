# Public payload review

The payload contains original analytic fixtures, synthetic model fields/loads, numerical comparisons, technical timings, local independently written code, review/correction history and page-only Three.js screenshots. No private conversation or attachments, home paths, credentials, flight locations, hostnames, third-party raw telemetry, downloaded papers or copyrighted figures are included. Verified literature is cited/paraphrased with source links. Existing vendor licenses are unchanged.

All frozen scientific files and raw records are unchanged. Results include every method/case, the startup target failure and all preflight findings/resolutions. Text scanning is supplemented by provenance review; the manifest identifies all reviewed bytes. Physical-data follow-up is a bibliographic/metadata note only, if included; no raw third-party measurements are republished.
