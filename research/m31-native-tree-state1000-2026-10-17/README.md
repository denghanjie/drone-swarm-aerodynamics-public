## What will be tested

One prospective state, fixed seed 2026101741, 1,000 drones, and three fixed methods: full calculation F; spatial tree without exclusions H0; spatial tree with conservative exclusions H. All three complete scientific outputs must agree. An independent checker then verifies every receiver's omission evidence and all 17,000 primary derivative components—the rates at which the drone states would change. The Boids controller source is unchanged. The checker binds its saved inputs and commands but does not independently reproduce the full neighbor search or controller law. A full high-precision reference at 1,000 drones is also outside this allocation. These limits prevent a native-equivalence pass from being presented as complete physical or numerical validation.
