# Result publication privacy review

New result content consists of synthetic drone state words, fixed numeric IDs/seeds, computed commands/loads, public source identities, runtime version metadata, audit reports and research-only UI screenshots. No lab/participant data, personal attachments, private messages or account credentials are copied into this checkpoint.

The native producer and auditor use declared research schemas. The four complete numerical journals are preserved unchanged; their byte-identical public chunks add no new information. Raw journals were not re-executed or repurposed. Public original-file names and part mappings use repository-relative paths. Runtime executables are hashed, not redistributed.

The parent visually inspected both desktop and mobile screenshots: only the research replay and its explanatory text are present. New text files were scanned for personal absolute paths, private-key headers and common credential patterns; the final precommit scan includes the independent result review and manifest. Binary numerical archives are identified as synthetic by their fixed producer source, the saved-data audit and byte-preservation checks; pattern scanning is not claimed to be a universal secret detector or a decompression scan of every journal string.

Public GitHub identity, localhost port, tool/runtime versions and owned process IDs are provenance, not authentication secrets. The source and archive publication receipts contain public URLs only. No install, paid service, external author contact or submission was performed.
