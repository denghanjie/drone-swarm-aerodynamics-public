# Scientific scope and claims to be tested

Hypothesis: the unchanged conservative-filter/exact-hierarchy load calculation can serve as an independently checked numerical oracle for all six prescribed, nontrivial local3D Boids flight snapshots. This remains untested until the frozen allocation closes.

The snapshot generator has three-axis local separation, alignment and cohesion, shared broad route guidance, collision-avoidance projections, a multirotor controller, six-degree-of-freedom rigid-body integration, quaternion normalization and motor-state lag. These are source-derived implementation facts, not physical validation. The model is airflow-off during generation. Exact aerodynamic predictions are posthoc evaluations and cannot change the saved path. The complete17words per drone retain position, velocity, quaternion, body angular rate and motor thrust state; the aerodynamic law uses geometry and thrust and checks velocity/rate only for finiteness. It has no wake memory or relative-airspeed response.

The exact branch/certificate backend is reused unchanged from the closed filter-cost package. Every pair remains subject to its ordered geometry/domain guards and support cutoffs. Every positive source-to-receiver edge must follow descending center-z and stable-ID rank. Tilt is not categorically forbidden, but it can violate the selected ordering. An ordering witness does not prove a cycle. A general cyclic or alternative-topological-order extension is not part of this experiment. The four normalized1e-8load bounds are numerical engineering tolerances under this model, not validated real-world accuracy.

Observed outputs must be classified separately: generated/not-reached; validated/unchecked/error; admitted/refused/reference-undecidable; certified/tolerance-failed/unresolved. Accepted-frame accuracy must report its denominator separately from the6scheduled-slot admission denominator. Validating a refusal is a useful integrity result and does not mean that the model served that state. Nontriviality failure makes the transfer evidence uninformative, even if every computed load passes.

The checker independently reconstructs preparation, pair semantics, coefficients, selected ordering and load enclosures using its disclosed inherited exact helpers. It validates capture/provenance and saved structure but does not rerun the flight trajectory or establish integration convergence. Scheduled0/5/10s states do not imply admission at intermediate times or Runge–Kutta substages. The51pose frames per complete run are display records, not51certified load queries.

No runtime speedup, real-time browser simulation,5000-drone performance, safety, physical-validity or novelty conclusion is allocated. Closed experiments and failed allocations stay closed. A successful finite informative result would justify further oracle/portability work; a refusal would prioritize its cause before more speed optimization.
