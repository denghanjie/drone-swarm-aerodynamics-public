# Moving-state admission checker — source review note

This is source preparation only. The checker has not been invoked, the copied scientific modules have not been imported, no simulator state has been generated, no control has been evaluated, and no feasibility or numerical result is claimed. Validation performed during preparation is Python AST parsing, JSON parsing, source text inspection, and hashing only.

## Scope and decision

The saved-only checker accepts the complete four-control, two-generation, six-slot ledger. Normal early stop remains a completed generation with explicit missing scheduled slots. All six planned states remain the primary denominator. Kernel programming/error prefixes are preserved by the runner/supervisor but cannot qualify as complete evidence.

`passed` means every reached scheduled method record has independent scientific validation, the four fixed expected status/branch controls succeed, and all twenty fixed targeted corruption checks succeed. A canonical independently verified model rejection can pass integrity. `advancement` additionally requires all six states reached, independently verified and certified under all four unchanged normalized 1e-8 load tolerances, plus every nontriviality clause in both runs. An unknown numerical refusal, candidate/reference status gap, reference-undecidable result, or checker error cannot qualify. Their causes are kept distinct in the report. A missing scheduled state prevents advancement even when the reached records have sound integrity.

Nontriviality is assessed separately for each run: positive edges at two distinct scheduled times; a postinitial prepared horizontal-axis squared norm greater than 1e-6; some postinitial relative displacement change versus drone0 greater than 0.001 m in each coordinate; and a resolved positive-edge set different from that run's initial set. The changed-edge requirement applies to **each run**. Missing or ambiguous witnesses are uninformative and do not authorize replacement states.
