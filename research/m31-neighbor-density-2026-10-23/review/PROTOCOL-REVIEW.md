## Scientific question and fixed interpretation

The proposed calculation is a useful minimal descriptive diagnosis of a known dense-list result. It consumes all 1,920 already selected stage slots in their fixed twelve-window order. It neither chooses fresh examples nor treats repeated stages as independent flights. The six published archive parts preserve the complete selected native records and are a suitable input format once every part and their concatenated original identity have been checked. This avoids treating the much larger replay-call census as independent evidence.

The component cutoff is 4; the unchanged F model's separate Euclidean center-neighbor cutoff is 3. They must remain distinct. On an eligible current component screen, N actual center norm calls is an optimistic lower bound on a list retained by the same conservative component-box rule. Therefore P−N is the maximum current component-rule omission opportunity. A literal zero half-width still has outward ULP padding in the historical implementation and need not attain that optimistic floor. If the floor is the full list on every saved state, changing buffer width alone cannot shorten those lists. This is a statement about that rule and these states, not all conservative neighbor algorithms.

N−G−E counts rejections already performed by the center-distance filter after component screening and center guards. It is not the later rotor-to-wake-axis radial classification. A positive count identifies a possible future proof target, not a demonstrated saving, moving-cache certificate, implementation or cost advantage. Such a future certificate would have to address rounded subtractions, the pinned runtime's norm behavior, all guards, boundary equality and conservative treatment of uncertain cases. A real-arithmetic sphere bound alone does not establish equivalence to the existing binary64 execution.

Rotor support is directed and attitude-dependent. The existing cache retains centers, IDs and boxes without attitude bounds. A current recorded coefficient support set cannot simply be reused as an orientation-independent cache certificate. Symbol 4 is unsupported near-field behavior; symbol 11 includes invalid terms. Their zero returned coefficient does not make them safe omissions. The saved U count is prospectively included and structurally checked, but E−U is expressly not a safe-to-omit count. Admitted and refused strata remain separate, and the one known failed build remains in the census.

The old exact evidence contract protects center-work counts, rotor calls and classification symbols as well as coefficients, loads, status and refusal. A new radial or rotor shortcut may preserve loads while changing that protected evidence. Its comparison contract therefore requires separate prospective review. This checkpoint neither relaxes the old contract nor rewrites historical outcomes.
