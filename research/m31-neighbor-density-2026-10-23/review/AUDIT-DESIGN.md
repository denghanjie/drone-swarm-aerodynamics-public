This checks the full field set, selected-record hash, IDs/positions/time/status/reasons, all twelve counts and twelve symbols, rather than accepting repeated producer digests. Reproduce the exact refusal identities and prospectively fixed branch decision. Compare the entire saved summary serialization byte-for-byte. No significance tests, independent-trial weighting or cost threshold are introduced.

Current component survivors N are an optimistic lower bound on pairs retained by the same conservative component-box rule. At most P−N pairs could be omitted at these saved states; outward rounding can retain more. This is neither a zero-skin implementation nor a speed estimate. A nonzero radialRejected count records exclusions already performed by the model. A radial-support shortcut would need a new bound and explicit review of the full evidence-equivalence contract, including protected center-work and symbol diagnostics. Sparse positive coefficients cannot justify deleting refusal-relevant pairs.

Require the input binding to identify the exact source publication, selected archive and full sorted set of consumed committed input pins; reconcile its read events with the exit receipt.
