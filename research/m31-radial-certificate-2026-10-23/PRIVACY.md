# Publication privacy scope

New material comprises research source, primary-source citations and access outcomes, a public official release checksum list, synthetic saved positions and distance results, audit logs and own evidence-view screenshots. No attachments, private chat records, credentials, personal measurement records or external-user data are introduced. All public paths are repository-relative; installed runtime is recorded by executable identity and version, not private home path.

Screen new source/results/review/receipt text for private paths, credential forms, private-key headers and email addresses; inspect saved desktop/mobile screenshots before result publication. Preserve source-access and transfer failures with sanitized public paths. Publish this checkpoint and additive restart/receipt records only; no alteration of historical sources/results. Pattern checks are limited and are not a universal secret guarantee.
