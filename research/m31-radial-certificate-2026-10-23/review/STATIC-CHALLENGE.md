# Static challenge of moving radius-3 exclusion

Protocol challenger review, 23 October 2026. Source inspection and analytical reasoning only; no numerical exposure, archive decompression, fixture/model execution, reaggregation, benchmark, UI allocation, or old allocation rerun was performed for this review. No historical artifact was edited.

## Recommendation

Continue with a conditional mathematical proof and, if separately authorized and frozen, a finite distance-exclusion diagnostic. Keep the proof assumptions and finite observations separate. The missing executable/path binding is a proof-admission gap for a universal exact-F claim, not evidence of an engine defect and not a fundamental impossibility result.

The companion [conditional lemma](CONDITIONAL-LEMMA.md) derives an absolute error bound for the parent-supplied three-argument max-scaled V8 expression, assuming individually rounded binary64 operations and correctly rounded square root. Its conservative `128*2^-52` bound is analytical. Applying it to the installed `Math.hypot` still requires a provenance and path argument. Successful sampled or even exhaustive finite saved-query checks cannot supply that missing universal implication.

For an engineering diagnostic limited to the already selected saved positions, it is defensible to record every actual center intrinsic result needed for the comparison and check each proposed omission directly. Call this finite qualification of the observed queries. Do not call it qualification of all positions in the temporal boxes or of a production exact native candidate. A failed observation must remain visible; narrowing inputs or moving the margin after exposure requires a new study.

## Exact target: the current F program

The relevant source is `research/m31-native-fresh-transfer-2026-10-17/source/vendor/kernel.cjs`:

- Lines 3–4 already restrict the implementation to a finite pinned-runtime experiment.
- Lines 33–47 compute the current global screen, with finite coordinates, finite nonnegative spans no greater than 256, and component threshold 4.
- Lines 105–110 delegate to unchanged lean behavior when that current screen is ineligible, including original preparation and guards.
- Lines 136–142 calculate the rounded differences, reject when maximum absolute component is at least 4, call the center intrinsic, classify invalid/oversized centers as symbol 11, and only then reject an ordinary center distance at least 3.
- Lines 150–174 contain the rotor paths, including unsupported near-field symbol 4 and invalid symbol 11; lines 205–206 use those symbols for admission refusal.

For each omitted directed pair at each eligible query, the required disjunction is:

1. The current rounded differences trigger F's explicit `maxAbs >= 4` branch; or
2. That actual three-argument intrinsic returns a finite center in `[3,512]`, and F's preceding center guards do not reject.

In case 2, all components are strictly below 4, so the `>257` component guard cannot fire. The finiteness and upper-center guard must nevertheless be justified. Merely proving a real Euclidean norm at least 3 does not establish the actual intrinsic's returned value or its guard behavior. Equality matters: F rejects an actual return of exactly 3.

A radius shortcut must preserve symbols 1 for ordinary center rejection and must never convert symbol-11 refusal into that ordinary zero case. Positive coefficient support is therefore insufficient as an omission criterion. An attitude-independent center cache is legitimate only after its center-branch implication is established; the rotor and admission logic cannot be inferred away from absence of positive coefficients.

## Protected evidence needs a prospective contract

The historical `research/m31-buffered-motion-2026-10-22/config.json`, lines 33–42, excludes timing/build phases, method, the gathered/visited/component-test fields, and specified adapter metadata from semantic comparison. It does not exclude actual center norm work. In particular, `directedCenterTests`, `certifiedSkippedPairs`, `screen.actualCenterNormCalls` and `screen.skippedCenterNormCalls` remain protected. F defines the last two from the corresponding work counters at kernel lines 49–52 and its skipped-pair count at line 201.

A successful radial shortcut changes those work fields. It cannot pass the old exact comparison contract by honestly reporting its actual work. Do not fabricate baseline-equivalent actual norm counts, relabel skipped calls as executed calls, or edit the old contract retroactively.

Any future native candidate needs a new frozen comparison contract that explicitly identifies changed work evidence, preserves scientific outputs and refusal/status evidence, and separately audits actual executed and skipped work. A distance-only diagnostic should report its own actual calls and proposed omissions without implying that it ran a complete native query or saved full-query time.

## Finite diagnostic requirements

A future frozen protocol can responsibly test the proposed geometric certificate on its selected finite sequence if it:

- Pins exact saved coordinate words, IDs, stage selection, traversal order, box policy, fixed margin and cache lifecycle before new numerical exposure.
- Records every actual center return required by the protocol, including guard classification, and verifies every proposed omitted pair is an ordinary F rejection for that current query.
- Keeps the inherited refusal-bearing stage and all selected stages. A center-distance diagnostic alone does not rerun or revalidate the downstream rotor/admission/load chain.
- Compares any previously saved stage totals with the new totals, accurately describing that as aggregate consistency. If original pair classes were not saved, equal totals do not prove old/new pairwise classification identity. Direct pairwise omission checks are against the newly recorded actual calls.
- Uses an independent exact-rational reconstruction of stored-box enclosures and lower-bound arithmetic where feasible. A second language's `hypot` is not an independent oracle for V8's exact returned word.
- Audits complete record/accounting coverage, not just a summary or a subset of passing omissions; preserves failures and the fixed prospective stopping rules.
- Makes no runtime-general exactness, full-native equivalence, performance, flight or physical-validation claim from this limited diagnostic.

The parent-proposed outward squared lower enclosure and upper squared threshold are a sound geometric design under normal binary64 basic arithmetic, as detailed in the conditional lemma. Its concrete implementation still needs static review. The extra margin does not turn the missing engine assumptions into established facts.
