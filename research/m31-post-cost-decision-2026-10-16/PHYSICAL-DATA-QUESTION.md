## Stop boundary and permitted interpretation

The proposed next checkpoint ends with **admitted presentation plus an exact extraction specification**, or a short failure record. It ends before target transcription, receiver evaluation, residuals, fits, or scientific pass/fail claims. If no matched triplet can be identified from this one report view, the present evidence does not support proceeding; record “not recoverable within this access scope,” not “no dataset exists.”

Any later scored comparison needs a separately frozen protocol with `f0 = T0(PWM)`, one unchanged static-thrust comparator, both nonzero conditions, dimensional and baseline-normalized discrepancies, shared-baseline handling, and finite calculation limits. Fixed PWM does not imply fixed RPM, shaft power, electrical power or ideal actuator-disk power. A discrepancy would challenge the **receiver law plus the explicit command-to-static-thrust bridge and transfer assumptions together**; it could not identify a unique failing component.

Incomplete total uncertainty permits only descriptive differences conditional on the source inputs. Calibration accuracy or graphical agreement cannot establish statistical rejection, application adequacy, independent blind validation, or the original vehicle's qualification.
