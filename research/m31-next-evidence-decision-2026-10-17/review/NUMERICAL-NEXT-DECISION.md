# Next numerical decision: prepared operands, with a bounded gate

17 October 2026. Source and saved-record review only; no scientific module was
imported or executed for this decision. The 24-scene panel is exposed regression
evidence, including its two formerly fresh 64-drone states.

**Adopt one isolated prepared-operand extraction as the next numerical
prerequisite.** It removes a concrete redundant operation before considering a
larger exact reference. It is not yet established as the dominant remaining cost,
and it does not qualify 5,000 drones. Keep the now-qualified tree dispatcher and
its exact proof/coverage operations unchanged. Do not bundle new filters, root
algorithms, precision changes, normalizer caches, or a drone-cap increase.

## Concrete implementation seam and proof risks

Place a new helper adapter at the unchanged tree dispatcher's residual
`helper.oracle_case` call. During the scene's existing preparation pass, intercept
the frozen `prepare_one` and `preparation_valid` functions, and register a row only
after validation succeeds. Key it by all 17 canonical raw words and bind the
registry to an immutable snapshot of every constant word and corner order.
Retain the actual prepared binary64 words and exact rational vector operands.
The scalar entry must reject unregistered, mutated, stale, or context-mismatched
operands before executing the suffix.

Extract the original scalar suffix beginning with its self branch immediately
after successful preparation validation. Preserve every expression and branch:
center guard before neighbor zero; rotor/down/radial guards and cutoffs; near and
width refusals; actual `d-h*axis`; projection; fixed192 square-root/fade profile;
K/scaled-K cap rejection versus uncertainty; exact-real receiver normalizer; all
returned intermediate interval components. Keep one outward coefficient
quantization, unchanged root/source routines, and signed load assembly.

This is a trusted scene-internal API, not permission to skip validation on
arbitrary external prepared inputs. Registration must prove correspondence to
the raw row and current constant/corner context. Do not mutate frozen module
globals or trust an externally supplied cache hit. Do not normalize axes, replace
rounded rotor points, share beta/nu eagerly, change refusal priority, or let a
cache return a result from another scene/model. Lazy normalizer reuse is a
separate later decision because moving its evaluation can expose errors on a pair
that should have terminated as zero.

## Cheapest useful next execution, if separately allocated

Use the complete pinned saved full/tree outputs as the baseline. Run only the new
prepared variant once on each of the same 24 inputs; require its entire scientific
wire record to equal the saved tree record, including prepared words, pair order,
branches, coefficients, roots, signed loads, coverage and existing work counters.
This avoids rerunning 79,008 full-oracle calls. It is implementation regression,
not fresh sample confirmation or a new timing campaign. Keep actual reuse
counters separate from inherited logical coverage counters.

Add fixed binding failures for unregistered receiver/source, changed registered
prepared operand, changed constant words, changed rotor-corner order, and closed
context. They must fail before suffix execution with exact preregistered codes;
missing witnesses fail instead of triggering a replacement search. Retain the
qualified six coverage-control results as prior evidence when the dispatcher and
entire wire outputs are unchanged; do not label them newly executed. Rechecking
the existing 14 fixtures and four native/load reports is inexpensive but must be
explicitly included in the new allocation. A direct original/prepared comparison
of the existing complete 322-case pair corpus would strengthen intermediate
interval and prefix-boundary regression if a later contract claims a general
replacement pair API; it is not silently added to this scene-internal allocation.

Prerequisites: reviewed immutable operand binding and literal suffix extraction;
source/runtime/input/saved-result pins; public preregistration and exact committed
byte verification; a separately granted finite wall/output/oracle budget with
time for closure before 03:00 UTC; preserved failed/partial outcomes and no retry
or cap increase. Require exact baseline equality and all adopted controls before
accepting reuse. Instrument preparation/node/suffix/root costs in a future
prospective cost question if deciding the next optimization depends on those
fractions; do not infer them from these aggregate clocks.

After this one extraction gate, reassess using measured remaining work rather
