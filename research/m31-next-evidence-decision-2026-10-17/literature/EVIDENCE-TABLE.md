# Next physical-evidence decision: bounded literature followup

**No new numerical benchmark is admitted by this followup.** The strongest independent *inter-drone load* candidate is Kiran, Ayanian and Breuer's force/moment/PIV experiment. It measures loads directly, so it avoids the firmware-dependent residual-force problem. Its registered numerical dataset is real, but the current repository response prevents inspection of files, conventions and calibration. Retain it as the priority acquisition target, with the specific blockers below.

Five candidate families were considered within 12 charged request dispatches. Three original articles' relevant methods were read in this pass: Kiran and Gielis from versioned HTML, and Bauersfeld from the previously retained, hash-checked PDF. UIUC was checked at its official release page; HVAB remains an inherited documentation lead because its new README request failed TLS. These reading levels are not interchangeable. This is a bounded disposition, not evidence that the entire field lacks usable data.

## Why Kiran is the priority, and why it is not admitted yet

The experimental observable is appropriately independent of a telemetry residual: force/torque transducers measure both vehicles, while PIV measures the wake in a separate experimental series. Its interaction geometry also addresses both aligned and offset configurations. That makes it a stronger direct test of a receiver response than either an isolated-thrust database or a source-only flow fit.

The paper's §II-A describes 100 μm position accuracy. Its PIV plane passes through the front two rotor centers, with a 400 × 300 mm field of view and 1,000 image pairs over about 70 seconds. A body-center radial cut is not automatically the same observable. The reported PIV uncertainty does not supply force or moment accuracy. A catalog sensor name and three repeated trials do not establish its calibration range, calibration matrix, systematic error or cross-talk.

Current access is a concrete blocker: the [DataCite record](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.26300/64d4-wa17) supplies no content URL, file sizes/formats or license details, and the registered Brown page supplies a verification challenge. Empty rights fields do not prove an absence of license; they mean reuse terms have not been established by this record. No alternate endpoint or challenge bypass was attempted.

An eventual admission should first freeze source-file identities and a case-level split, then document geometry, actuation, signs, tare, isolated baselines and calibration. The existing model's rotor dimensions and ideal-power receiver assumption cannot silently stand in for the experiment's voltage/PWM-driven hardware. This review authorizes no fitting, label reconstruction or numerical comparison.

## Search and acquisition limits

The two initial discovery queries failed at the search-service transport and were conservatively charged as two dispatches. Ten subsequent direct HTTPS dispatches used 60-second limits and zero automatic retries. Nine returned bodies; the HVAB request failed TLS. Total successful response bodies: **927,081 bytes**. Private retained bytes, including extracted article text and receipts, are recorded in the private manifest and remain below 8 MiB.

Broad Crossref, Zenodo and DataCite discovery also returned modeling-only, non-rotor, biological-wake and unrelated records. Such hits were not promoted to accepted benchmarks. Repository resource labels alone were not treated as proof of measurements, and no CFD product was substituted for an experimental observable. Only the five candidate families above received disposition; no claim follows about uninspected sources.

Third-party pages, metadata and extracted text remain private. No raw measurement archive, weights or model was downloaded; no third-party source was executed; no forces were reconstructed, fitted or scored; no figures were digitized; and no author, paid service or external collaborator was contacted. Existing packages are unchanged. The result is a source-access and compatibility decision with **no admitted physical-validation score**.
