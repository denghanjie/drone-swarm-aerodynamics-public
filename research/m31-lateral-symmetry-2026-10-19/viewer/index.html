What the pattern means

A pattern shared by both sides could come from several sources, including flow asymmetry, swirl, a misplaced center, background motion or measurement processing. This diagnostic cannot identify the physical cause.

The data were already examined and come from the same experiment. No new fit, center shift, interpolation or airflow calculation is performed. The true airspeed conversion, separate experimental validation and safe flocking remain unresolved.
