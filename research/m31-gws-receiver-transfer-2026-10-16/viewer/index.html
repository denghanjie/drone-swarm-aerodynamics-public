What the comparison establishes

The discrepancy remains in each of the three baseline scenarios. It is not created solely by including nonpositive-thrust records: the separately reported positive-thrust subset also differs.

We preserve every published condition. Five reported coefficients are nonpositive, while the selected equation always predicts positive thrust for this input domain. That is a representation limit of this declared full-run transfer.

What it cannot establish

The experiment used prescribed RPM. The model fixes an ideal actuator-disk power quantity. Those conditions are not equivalent; our bridge between them is a hypothesis.

The comparison cannot identify whether the equation, that bridge, or other unmodeled effects cause the discrepancy. Full uncertainty is unavailable. There is no physical pass/fail threshold, direct 46 mm drone validation, or novelty claim.
