# We need better evidence before changing the model

19 October 2026. This checkpoint checked whether we could find the missing calibration history or a separate experiment suitable for testing the model. **Neither route produced admissible new evidence in this bounded search.** That is an evidence gap, not a new aerodynamic discovery.

Imagine checking a kitchen scale. If you do not know whether the reference weights are in grams or ounces, adjusting the scale until its numbers look right would not prove it works. Our existing measurements have a documented nominal unit convention, but the exact conversion and processing trail remains unverified. We must not guess that trail from the model's preferred answer.

We investigated three additional experiment/data leads:

- **Cene:** the paper is now accessible, but it studies a different, ducted drone. The inspected release is a paper; usable compatible downstream component arrays were not verified.
- **Hackett:** a real velocity dataset, but it measures water stirred by a drone, not the air beneath it.
- **Hundur:** flight logs and computed airflow tables, rather than independently measured wake velocities.

The Brown provenance search also checked manuscript revision information. It found no new exact processing chain; repository-access failures are recorded. This does not establish that the documentation or suitable datasets do not exist elsewhere.

**Nothing was fitted or recalculated.** The previous finding remains: the fixed model predicts smaller radial-transport magnitudes on all twelve checked full-grid radial walls, under the existing reconstruction assumptions. We still cannot identify the physical cause or claim validation, safe flock flight or novelty.

The next useful work is to design a measurement test that distinguishes calibration errors, incorrect sideways-flow prediction and broken symmetry assumptions. The draft requirements specify signed air velocities, known geometry, physical units, multiple planes where possible and uncertainty. No physical experiment, purchase, contact or numerical test is authorized by that draft.
