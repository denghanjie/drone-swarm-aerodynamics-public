## Newly verified water-flow candidate
Hackett et al., Water circulation measurements under a hovering drone, Mendeley Data v1. Metadata describes PIV of water in a flume under an operating drone. It supplies horizontal/vertical water velocities in m/s, coordinates in m, masked values and six named MAT files under blade/body at three nominal airspeed settings. Files are described, not acquired or independently inspected; the rendered landing-page Files section did not expose a filename inventory. Although the measurements are independently acquired and documented, they are water circulation, not airborne wake components. Reject at the observable gate. Do not apply an air-wake model across this air–water interface.
