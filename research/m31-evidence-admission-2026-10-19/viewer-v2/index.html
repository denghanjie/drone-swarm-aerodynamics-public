The next useful step

Specify the measurements that can distinguish a calibration problem from a wrong model: downward and sideways air velocities, known positions, a documented conversion to metres per second, and repeated measurements with uncertainty.

Multiple planes or a volume would also help test whether the flow really has the symmetry we assumed. Until suitable evidence is available, another score on the same data would not establish physical validity.

This checkpoint adds evidence screening, not a new scientific result. No model parameters changed, no new scientific calculations ran, and novelty and safe swarm flight remain unestablished.
