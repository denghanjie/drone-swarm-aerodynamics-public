## Timing, budgets and closure

The primary query boundary includes the owned input copy, complete query/result construction and cache checks/builds/rebuilds. Reset is charged separately into each stream total. Encoding, reference comparison, witness/CAS work and persistence are outside that boundary. comparisonMs is nested within encodePersistMs; the row append is outside that overhead timer. The protocol properly discloses possible allocation/GC influence and avoids an uninstrumented throughput claim.

The audit reproduces each accumulator's exact operation order, including separately accumulated admitted/refused costs. For each measured repetition it sums twelve stream totals before computing C/F. The eight-ratio median averages sorted positions 3 and 4; keep requires median <=.90, at least seven strict wins, measured reuse and at least one measured box-exit rebuild. Cold builds do not satisfy motion coverage. Missing coverage leaves the gate unsatisfied without extra windows. A sound evidence record may validly report failed cost/coverage gates.

The five single allocations retain the stated wall/output ceilings: extraction 1200s/768MiB, replay 1800s/768MiB, audit 1800s/256MiB, finalization 120s/2MiB and UI 150s/16MiB. Runtime identity hashing now occurs inside the supervised process group. Node injection/search variables are stripped; optimized Python is explicitly rejected. Termination begins conservatively before the wall ceiling and late closure fails. Extraction charges underlying gzip writes, including trailer bytes, before writing; extraction/replay reserve closure capacity. Finalizer accounts its <=64KiB viewer pin file inside its 2MiB budget. Finite ceilings are not capacity predictions.

Auditor closure checks require successful internal/external extraction and producer receipts, exact inventories and no failure artifact; all consumed identities are rechecked before success. Finalization requires successful prior closures, passed audit and exact viewer export identity. Browser checks are prospective: 1,920 display buffers/times/statuses, refusal labeling, decisions, controls, responsive screenshots and changed-data refusal. Actual runtime, screenshots and performance remain untested at this review.
