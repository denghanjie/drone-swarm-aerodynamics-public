# Result publication privacy scope

The package contains synthetic drone states, saved model outputs, deterministic storage-only fixtures, hashes, process closure metadata, public references and screenshots of the research page. No user attachments, private conversations, credentials or unrelated application content are included. Binary64 state words and generated seed IDs are computational data, not measured personal locations.

Parent inspected both screenshots and will pattern-scan all new text for personal absolute paths, private-key headers and common credential formats before freezing. Binary archive parts are evidence produced by the frozen synthetic-data program; they are not text-scanned as if their compressed bytes were prose. The audit independently decoded and checked the complete journal structure and raw identities. This is a scoped privacy review, not an exhaustive secret-detection guarantee. Runtime executables are hashed only and not redistributed.
