Why this matters for our research

A downward-only model leaves out the air moving sideways as the jet spreads. This test asks whether a standard physics relation can supply that missing motion without another fitted number.

The candidate assumes steady, symmetric flow with no mean swirl. Only the inner part of the jet is tested, before our artificial outer fade begins. We have not established the true airspeed conversion, complete 3D flow, receiving-drone forces, safe flocking or a novel discovery.

A separate 3D experiment was examined but its available reported measurements were too near the drone for this model. We did not use that mismatch to manufacture a failure or a success.
