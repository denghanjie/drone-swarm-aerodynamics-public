# Literature and source ledger

Primary sources checked 19 October2026. These are documented prior results, not discoveries of this project.

- Kiran, Ayanian & Breuer, *Influence of Static and Dynamic Downwash Interactions on Multi-Quadrotor Systems*, RSS2025, DOI[10.15607/RSS.2025.XXI.102](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss21/p102.pdf), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.09463v1). §II-A and Fig4 identify the front-rotor PIV plane; §III-B defines lateral v along x. Eq6 is not the expression adopted here: retain that distinction rather than silently rewriting their formula. Source mean-field stitching, calibration and limited components remain limitations.
- Their [Brown dataset, DOI10.26300/64d4-wa17](https://repository.library.brown.edu/studio/item/bdr:rnnfmt6u/), CC BY4.0, supplies the already saved original cases. README and plotting script define x,z,u,v and plot without a lateral sign flip. No newly measured experiment is claimed.
- Bauersfeld et al., *Robotics meets Fluid Dynamics: A Characterization of the Induced Airflow below a Quadrotor as a Turbulent Jet*, [arXiv2403.13321v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13321v3), [author publication page](https://lbfd.github.io/publication/2024-robotics-meets-fluid-dynamics/). Prior coefficients and far-field admission beyond2.5 drone diameters; original observable is mean speed magnitude. We test the project's directed axial interpretation, not all claims of this original paper.
- Kiran, Ayanian & Breuer, *The Structure of Merging Turbulent Jets Beneath a Small Quadrotor*, [arXiv2608.30900v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30900v1), preprint. Eq8 axial shape and Eq9 crossstream shape give the same continuity relation independently derived for this checkpoint. This paper uses radial arm length46mm; do not copy its z/l thresholds into the prior Cartesian half-spacing32.5mm. It supplies known theory and experimental precedent, not raw independent validation here. No claim that its raw measurements are the RSS archive.
- Wolf et al., *Volumetric wake investigation of a free-flying quadcopter using Shake-The-Box Lagrangian particle tracking*, Experiments in Fluids65,152(2024), [DOI10.1007/s00348-024-03880-3](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00348-024-03880-3), [DLR publication](https://elib.dlr.de/223206/). Actual3D experiment, documented velocity normalization. Its available reported control-volume fluxes are in the current model's excluded near field; rotor tilt and unequal layout also differ. No new score or digitization performed. FlowFit uses a continuity constraint, so small net mass residual is not an independent check of that imposed law.
- Flem et al., *Experimental Characterization of Propeller-Induced Flow (PIF) below a Multi-Rotor UAV*, Atmosphere15,242(2024), DOI10.3390/atmos15030242; [primary preprint with journal link](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202401.0756). Measured sonic airflow, but coaxial X8, tilted rotors, wall/door influence and no verified raw release for our use. Retain as a candidate with explicit exclusions, not an admitted independent test.

Independent search evidence and access limitations are retained in review/EXTERNAL-DATA-SEARCH.md. Failure to locate a release is not proof that none exists. The original single-PIV SI normalization remains unresolved; the current diagnostic adds no evidence for a factor-two correction.
