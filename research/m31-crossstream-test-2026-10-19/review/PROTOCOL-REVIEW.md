## Mathematical challenge and physical assumptions

The formula is consistent with the existing unfaded axial field under steady incompressible axisymmetry and regularity at the cylindrical axis. With K=2sqrt(2), S=.07668, c=sqrt(2)-1, s=Z+5.817K, b=S*s, Uc=g*10.11K/s, R=sqrt(X^2+1) and xi=R/b, it gives

    V_R = (Uc*S/2)*xi*(1-c*xi^2)/(1+c*xi^2)^2,
    V_X = V_R*X/R.

The source implementation uses exactly this radial expression and signed Cartesian projection. The independent [geometry/continuity note](GEOMETRY-AND-CONTINUITY.md) derives the expression by integrating the axial derivative from the regular axis and also gives a rational alternative for the audit. The K factor enters s and Uc; it does not supply another factor in the final S/2.

The observable projection additionally assumes zero mean azimuthal velocity. Continuity fixes radial flow under axisymmetry, not arbitrary swirl. On the off-axis plane Y=1, swirl would add -V_theta/R to the observed x component. The candidate is therefore a conditional no-mean-swirl completion, not a uniquely reconstructed total measured vector field. Nonaxisymmetry, offsets, reference-speed uncertainty and the prior scalar speed model's directed-axial interpretation remain possible sources of mismatch.

The source-backed +v along +x convention is declared before scoring. The paper associates v with x, the coordinate figure defines +x right and +z down, and the archive plotting workflow does not negate lateral v. This supports the stated orientation without independently recovering unavailable instrument calibration or preprocessing. The original sign must be retained: the radial field has an outward inner region and inward outer region, while V_X is odd in X. X=0 on Y=1 is not the cylindrical axis; its x projection vanishes even when radial velocity does not. A planar divergence check would omit the y derivative and would not test the stated cylindrical identity.

Restricting xi<=2 is scientifically material. The full radial integration interval then stays inside the unfaded field, and the boundary is before the taper changes the profile. The existing axial fade/cutoff is inactive in the saved domain, with the prior near admission retained. This justifies only the local core expression. No continuation through the radial taper, global mass/momentum conservation, Navier–Stokes solution or complete production vector field is established.

The expression is a known continuity relation, consistent with Kiran et al. 2026 Eq. 9 and the explicit derivation. It must not be described as a literal reproduction of the different RSS 2025 Eq. 6. Neither the 2026 fitted spread nor its radial-arm-length height thresholds are imported into the older Cartesian half-spacing coordinates. Novelty is correctly not claimed.
