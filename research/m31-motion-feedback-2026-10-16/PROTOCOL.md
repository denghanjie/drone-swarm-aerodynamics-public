## Equations and integration contract

Reuse the already audited lean coefficient builder and ordered solver unchanged. At each global RK4 stage, normalize all stage quaternions, rebuild geometry/coefficients for the entire simultaneous state, admit the model, solve all four rotor loads and reevaluate residual. Never solve drones independently or use another drone's previous-stage state. Reject a path if the model rejects geometry, positive nominal forces, near field, coefficient cap or descending order, or if log residual>1e-9 or source-total mismatch>2e-9. No zero-load fallback, cutoff change or alternate solver.

Motor states f0 keep their original first-order dynamics. Original nominal wrench remains present. Add only delta f_r = T_effective,r − f0_r: world force R(q)(0,0,sum delta f), body roll/pitch moment (sum y_r delta f, −sum x_r delta f,0). This replaces the nominal axial force/moment algebraically; it does not apply a second full thrust. Nominal yaw drag torque is retained because the aerodynamic closure supplies no shaft/drag torque model. No claim of yaw-physics accuracy follows. No force or torque is added to motor-state derivatives. RK4 stage projection is a new numerical scheme relative to historical F.integrate; both on/off arms use it. We do not assert fourth-order convergence without observation.

The inherited source shape/total-thrust amplitude and ideal-power receiver cubic remain hypothetical. The quasi-steady field has no wake-history transport, source motion advection or explicit own-velocity inflow correction. Four differential rotor loads supply roll/pitch changes under stated assumptions; body aerodynamic drag/side forces, wake moments and dynamic motor torque are omitted. These limits are especially relevant when vehicles begin to move. A converged numerical answer cannot validate them.
