# Result privacy review

New public evidence consists of original synthetic drone states, commands, numeric forces/derivatives, computed diagnostics, code/version hashes and bounded run metadata. No report pages, third-party plots, private user attachments, author contact information, credentials or private paths are included. Supervisor records use generic commands and omit PIDs. CPU/runtime platform data is limited to reproducibility/resource information. Raw numerical files are unchanged. Viewer converts only recorded position/quaternion samples, checks raw hashes and labels physical limits. Saved-only summaries introduce no new simulation. Inspect the narrow privacy/pin scan alongside the final manifest; it does not claim an exhaustive audit of old repository history.
