## Findings

1. **Target choice is explicit.** M-current is a mathematical-model certification track using declared prepared operands as exact real values, with finite binary64 operands interpreted as their exact dyadic values. Supplied constants, exact nominal sums and normalized ratios, and mathematical square roots are distinguished from native evaluation. Certification is conditional on these operands. The raw-state preparation adapter remains a specification gate, and its cost belongs inside every eventual comparator's timing boundary.

2. **The native gap is retained.** The adopted note distinguishes native rounded branches, sums, grouping, `Math.hypot`, bisection and reductions from the exact-real target. It makes no uniform bridge or byte/native-error claim. Finite comparisons cannot close that proof gap. Legacy-native compatibility and physical validity remain separate obligations; M-current is explicitly an intermediate track toward the original goal.

3. **The common interface change is disclosed.** Q-current requires common model identity, qualification status, candidate values and loads, output enclosures, tolerance decisions, and partition/admission/cap evidence. Exact CSR and graph arrays, legacy reason counts, residual maps, ordinary Lmax and checksum outputs are explicitly removed from this common query contract. The native diagnostic contract remains separately identified. F must be requalified for M-current; the old native F result is not automatically a rigorous reference. F, H and B share the same new output/error obligations and complete current-query costs. Historical timings are not a denominator for the new interface.

4. **Admission and resource semantics are adequately separated at design level.** Guard/rejection precedence is expressly preserved for the chosen exact-real model, without asserting native equivalence. Whole-source-cube bounds for both K and K*beta are retained. Failure of a conservative upper bound to prove a cap is unresolved, not proof of a model violation. Similarly, an upper bound on the actual positive-term count can certify safety but cannot establish excess. Refinement/counting or an unresolved outcome is required. Proved domain rejection, unresolved certification and resource failure remain distinct. The removal of the native first-overflow position is an explicit common-interface decision; the separate native track retains its immediate 2,000,001st-positive throw ordering. Refinement, fallback and unsuccessful computational outcomes must be charged and reported.

5. **The timing correction is accurate relative to the reviewed contract issue.** Both notes acknowledge that historical timed L/F already used `fullSymbols=false`, with dense tensors outside measured queries. They do not claim a new speedup merely from removing those tensors. The adopted timing boundary includes current preparation, hierarchy work, admission/count/order evidence, solving, outward arithmetic, refinement/fallback, certificates and consumed load outputs. The summary's historical numerical timing range was not independently revalidated in this narrowly scoped follow-up.

6. **No goal substitution is presented.** Both documents identify the result as design-only, leave tightness and speed unmeasured, and retain the original native, physical, scale/live-use and novelty obligations. The summary describes unchanged replayed flights and no new trajectory/timing result. These are appropriately bounded claims; the replay statements were not independently reverified in this review.

## Remaining gates before execution

The acceptance above relies on the notes' explicit non-executable status. Before numerical evaluation, freeze and independently review the model/constant encodings, raw-state adapter, geometry validity, branch machine and any ordered-solver eligibility restriction. The theorem's allowance for cycles does not itself establish eligibility for an ordered implementation.

A sound executable outward-arithmetic design is still required. Native sqrt/hypot with an assumed one-ulp pad is not established as sound. Exact-dyadic endpoint verification of a proposed square root is only a possible direction until its implementation and bounded unsuccessful outcomes are specified and proved.

Also freeze hierarchy membership and refinement, complete nonoverlapping row partitions, candidate-consistent aggregates, admission and positive-count proof procedures, candidate/reference uncertainty, load propagation, tolerances, controls, held-out inputs, resources, stopping/failure rules, and the common comparator timing/output contract. No performance or native-equivalence clearance follows from the conditional symbolic construction.
