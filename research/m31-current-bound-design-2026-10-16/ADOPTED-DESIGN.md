# Adopted conditional design: current source-group enclosures

2026-10-16. This is a desk result, not an executable specification or experimental result. The initial draft and three independent reviews remain unchanged. This note adopts the conditional construction with the qualifications below. It creates no scientific execution allocation.

## Decision and target

Adopt **M-current** as the explicitly named mathematical-model certification track for subsequent development. This is an intermediate track toward the original research goal. It does not replace the separate legacy-native compatibility, physical validation, live-flight, scale or novelty obligations.

M-current takes declared current prepared centers, rotor points, axes, lever arms and positive nominal rotor forces as exact real inputs. A supplied finite binary64 value denotes its exact dyadic value. Every constant, including D, area, induced speed, kappa and mg, will be an explicitly frozen supplied value interpreted this way; it is not silently recomputed as an ideal expression. Nominal totals are exact sums of the four supplied forces; weights are their exact ratios and therefore sum to one. Beta and nu are the positive mathematical square roots specified in the draft. The complete guarded exact-real geometric formula defines fixed nonnegative coefficients independent of unknown loads. All omitted coefficients remain part of that model.

This choice differs from native JavaScript branch results, rounded nominal sums, grouped arithmetic, Math.hypot returns, finite bisection and reduction order. **There is no proved bridge certifying that native program.** Later discrepancy measurements may describe finite observations; they cannot establish a uniform bridge. No claim of byte equality or a legacy error guarantee follows here. Historical L/F source and results stay immutable.

The eventual end-to-end timing boundary must start at the same raw current state for every comparator. A common preparation adapter must produce and identify the supplied M-current operands, validate their admissibility and charge its complete work. Exact-real certification is conditional on those declared prepared operands, not a proof of the physical attitude or of native preparation accuracy. Concrete constant encodings, adapter, geometry-validity predicates, state-to-model policy and reference uncertainty must be frozen and independently reviewed before any executable qualification. They are not fully specified by this desk note.

## Conditional enclosure chain accepted

For every current node, component boxes enclose its actual centers and axes. Natural interval operations enclose h=d dot a and e=d-h*a. Coordinatewise absolute minima/maxima enclose the norm of e. This remains valid for actual near-unit axes without an orthogonal-projection shortcut. It does not waive geometry admission.

Under the draft's stronger active-domain assumptions and all guards below, nonnegative monotone factor bounds give K_irj <= U_irC for every member. The factors need not attain their extremes together. The resulting upper estimate may be uselessly loose without being false. A positive common lower estimate additionally requires every member to be center-eligible and nonself, PL>0, hU<downCut, qU<radCut, positive denominators and no zero/rejection branch. Otherwise use zero.

For ONE current candidate 0<xhat_ir<=1, define exact yhat_j=sqrt(sum_r w_jr*xhat_jr), and aggregate B_C=sum_j beta_j*yhat_j. Multiplication of each nonnegative member inequality and summation give

    0 <= sum_(j in C) a_irj*yhat_j <= U_irC*B_C/nu_ir.

Every row partitions the entire nonself source universe into disjoint retained singletons, bounded nodes and proved zeros. Self subtraction, if used, must itself be enclosed; rounded aggregate subtraction is insufficient. All rows and aggregates in one certificate use identical current geometry, nominal values and candidate. A changed candidate requires consistent reaggregation.

Enclose each COMPLETE row contraction in [sL,sU], then p_ir=xhat_ir^3+xhat_ir*s_ir in [L_ir,U_ir], with L_ir>0. The inherited corollary uses outward kbar_ir=max(1,U_ir,1/L_ir), Kbar=max_ir kbar_ir, giving

    xhat_ir/(kbar_ir*Kbar) <= xstar_ir <= min(1,xhat_ir*kbar_ir*Kbar).

The independent mathematical reviewer found no algebraic counterexample under these assumptions. The accepted argument is conditional theory; no numeric tightness experiment occurred. One loose row can widen every enclosure through Kbar. Signed rotor-force and moment propagation follows the pinned adopted root-defect corollary, using actual lever arms and rotation column, not assumed exact orthonormality.

## Admission is part of the method

The future M-current state machine preserves the intended geometric thresholds and precedence, evaluated for its own exact-real target. It is not called native branch equivalence. Global input/preparation and positive-nominal validity precede query acceptance. For nonself pairs:

1. Center norm/component guards (512 and 257) precede the neighbor-zero test at center norm >=3.
2. Rotor displacement component guard 258 and absolute down guard 1024 precede down<=0 or down>=downCut zero exits.
3. On the remaining down branch, radial norm guard 2048 precedes near-field rejection. Unsupported means the conjunction 0<h<minDown*D AND r<nearRad; the rest of that slab is zero. Projection zero does not bypass this check.
4. Positive width precedes radial-support cutoff; projection/speed checks remain at their original later location. Retain all finiteness, magnitude and positivity requirements, not just those abbreviated in the active-bound formula.
5. Prove unscaled K<=128 and scaled K*beta<=128 over the WHOLE source cube y in (0,1]. Candidate-only tests are insufficient. U>128 or U*max(beta)>128 means unresolved, not proved violation.
6. Prove descending-z/ascending-ID membership for every possibly positive edge if the ordered solver domain is retained. The inherited contraction theorem itself allows cycles; this restriction comes from the chosen operational domain.
7. Retain the 2,000,000 ACTUAL positive rotor-source term budget. Possible-positive upper counts can certify being below it; an upper count above it cannot prove excess. Refine/count or report unresolved. Count failures separately from mathematical rejection.

Four outcomes must remain distinguishable: proved admissible, proved domain rejection, unresolved certificate, and resource failure. An uncertain branch is never an admitted zero. Hierarchy refinement or exact singleton fallback must be charged; overflow, underflow and nonpositive computed denominator/lower defect bounds need explicit unsuccessful outcomes. No tolerance-based clipping repairs a missing proof.

For the proposed new load interface, exact legacy reason-count arrays and first-overflow location are not required outputs. Its resource/status rule must be specified for ALL methods. Legacy diagnostics and immediate 2,000,001st-positive throw ordering remain unchanged on the separate native track. This is an explicit interface change, not preservation of native failure order.

## Common prospective query Q-current

The adopted design direction is a common **certified-load** interface. It must return model/input identity; qualification status; candidate rotor ratios and physical-unit loads; enclosing rotor, collective/world-force and moment bounds; a decision against predeclared tolerances; and sufficient partition/admission/cap provenance for independent checking. No successful result may hide omitted positive contributions or discard a failed case from the denominator.

Exact CSR coefficients, graph arrays, exact per-class counts, ordinaryLmax, legacy full residualMap and first/last-K checksum are NOT required Q-current outputs. Full inspection can remain a separate qualification interface. All competing Q-current methods have these same obligations. If future users require those old outputs, that is a different comparison and their cost must be paid.

Compare (F) a requalified F-derived exact screening method, (H) an exact hierarchy resolving all required terms except proved zeros, and (B) bounded omission, under the same target and output/error contract. The existing native F is not automatically a rigorous M-current reference. H separates ordinary spatial-enumeration gains from gains due to bounded omission. No free baseline solve, prevalidated admission oracle, stale tree or cached full answer may prepare B's candidate.

Complete cost includes current preparation, build/refit, guards, ordering/count proofs, candidate construction/solve, candidate-dependent aggregates, outward arithmetic, refinement, fallback, complete row certificate, load propagation and output consumption. Independent saved audit and rendering may be separately accounted, consistently across methods. Historical Chrome medians are context, not new-interface denominators.

**Correction retained:** timed historical L/F queries already used fullSymbols=false. Dense classification tensors were outside those query calls. Removing them cannot explain a prospective speedup.

## Remaining gates

No sound executable outward arithmetic is adopted yet. Native sqrt/hypot with an assumed one-ulp pad is not a proof. A possible development direction is to use a native proposal and verify enclosing square-root endpoints by exact dyadic comparisons, with a bounded unresolved fallback; this is only an idea. Basic interval arithmetic and exceptional ranges also need justified inclusion.

Before numerical evaluation: freeze the exact model/adapter/constants and branch machine; prove the arithmetic primitives; specify a deterministic hierarchy, complete membership and refinement; specify candidate construction and reference uncertainty; predeclare tolerances, representative controls, held-out inputs, resource/stopping budgets and failure accounting; freeze/push/independently verify source. No existing allocation is reopened.

Prior art: controlled hierarchical kernel sums (Gray–Moore 2003) and tree/traversal/pruning separation (Curtin et al. 2013) are established. Rump 2010 was verified bibliographically and by introductory extract only; no specific arithmetic theorem was read. The independent access ledger records exact limits. The composition may be worth testing, but no novelty or publication-readiness claim is supported.
