A substantive gate inconsistency in the original design was identified and resolved in scope by the coordinator. Frozen C exposes its full decision mask, selected-positive source/bound list, accumulated B, candidate/zero/retained/fallback counts, and actual work counts. It does not expose retained-positive bound values or fallback source IDs. Therefore direct runtime equality of every source bound and every fallback identity cannot be claimed from these records. Re-evaluating the source-bound function to manufacture evidence would add forbidden uncharged work and weaken the intended control.

The accepted narrower invariance gate is exact equality of C's mask with Z's shadow mask, selected source/bound lists, accumulated B, candidate counts, zero identities, C-retained shadow counts and fallback counts, plus a static proof by source comparison that the relevant branches remain identical. The independent oracle audits all shared zero identities and all C-selected terms in its existing single direct pass. The fallback-identity and unselected-positive-bound gap must remain explicit. This is a satisfactory resolution of the documentary overclaim if the final driver and final report use the narrower statement consistently.
