What this comparison can tell us

The change includes all later consequences of coupling: altered motion can change neighbors, steering and controller commands. It does not isolate a direct force-only effect. The two time steps are checks on the same starting configuration, not independent trials.

If a saved run stops, its pane becomes empty after that point. We do not extend the path or keep a stopped drone hovering. Differences beyond that time cannot be treated as a matched flight comparison. Neither arm is proof of real-flight safety, physical aerodynamic accuracy or a new research contribution.
