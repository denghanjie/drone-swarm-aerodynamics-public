What we can learn

One multiplier can change how strong the predicted airflow is, but cannot change its spatial pattern. Checking it farther away helps us see whether that simple adjustment is enough to describe these measurements.

The multiplier is an empirical fit. It does not establish the true physical airspeed conversion. A better match here would still leave3D airflow, receiving-drone lift, flocking and safety unvalidated.
