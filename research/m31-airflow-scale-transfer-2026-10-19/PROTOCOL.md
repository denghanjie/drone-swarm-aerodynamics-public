## Rationale and scope
An unknown constant conversion between reference airspeeds could multiply every value by one factor. A coefficient fitted from observations cannot identify that conversion physically: it may absorb motor operation, model amplitude or other errors. Therefore call it an empirical gain, never a calibrated Ui/UH ratio. Multiplying the entire observed field by a positive constant multiplies the fitted gain and residuals by that constant; the corrected residual norm divided by the observed norm remains unchanged. This invariant diagnostic can assess transfer without assuming an absolute SI calibration. Spatially varying calibration, coordinate/shape mismatch and velocity-observable differences remain unresolved.

The prior formulas and3,240saved mean cells were exposed before this design. This is a prospective within-dataset diagnostic on known evidence, not blind, independent, statistical or physical validation. No raw PIV decoding, new cells, interpolation, length-unit changes, wake re-evaluation, force law,3D extension or swarm flight. Published RSS planar modelB was fitted to this experimental study already; its additional gain is not a fair independent challenger. Results cannot claim novelty or reject either source paper.
