# Publication review
New package uses only already-public derived laboratory records and their exact public provenance receipts. No original user attachments, personal messages, private flight/location data, credentials or private acquisition paths are included. Public script expressions and source hashes are disclosed; original scripts are not executed or redistributed here. Author names are public research attribution. A text scan and final result-log review are required before publication. Any source normalization discrepancy is described with its limits, without attributing an unproven error to an author.
