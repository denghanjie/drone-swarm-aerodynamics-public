# Next milestone: one diagnostic at the existing reference ceiling

17 October 2026. Source and saved-record review only. No scientific module was
imported or run; no network, new extraction, fitting, native call or browser call
was used. This memo proposes a later protocol; it is neither implementation nor
an execution allocation. Earlier packages remain frozen.

**Choose one prepared-tree capacity diagnostic on the existing dense and sparse
N=180 states, with one qualified N=64 control.** Use standard profiling and saved
exact endpoints. Do not make another optimization first. This is the smallest
available test that both reaches the permitted reference ceiling and materially
increases the positive interaction/root workload. It can reject the current
implementation as a practical route before more engineering is spent on it.

The decision question is: *Can the unchanged prepared exact-reference path
reproduce the already qualified complete root and signed-load enclosures at its
current ceiling, within a small fixed reservation, and what work remains?*
It is deliberately narrower than whether exact N=5,000 accuracy is affordable.
A good result at 180 is necessary evidence for considering a larger contract,
not sufficient evidence for approving that contract.

## Why this milestone changes the decision

The prepared adapter passed 24 exact saved-tree comparisons in one 3.747833208 s
qualification observation: 447 scene preparations, zero pair preparations,
26,570 suffix calls, and two operand lookups per suffix. Its largest state was
64. The qualification proves the finite reuse regression and binding controls;
it does not isolate preparation cost or establish a speedup. Subtracting its
clock from an earlier differently ordered run would invent a performance result.

The 5,000-drone Chrome timing question already has substantial finite evidence:
both large states passed in three processes, with F/L paired medians
0.7411–0.8048 and F marginal medians about 366–396 ms. The adverse 1,000 state
failed the usefulness rule. That evidence uses an independent floating
comparison, not the current outward rational four-category 1e-8 criterion. The
old solutions also lack delivered collective/world-force/world-moment arrays.
Neither fact should be hidden by another favorable timing campaign.

| Alternative | What a successful next run would establish | Why defer it now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile only the qualified 24-scene corpus | Cost attribution on exposed states of at most 64 drones | It leaves the available reference ceiling untested and invites another local cache change. One small control is enough for the new profiler boundary. |
| Native large-output bridge | Exact transfer of the shared old Chrome projection plus added load/association consistency and inclusive wrapper cost | Valuable and already concretely proposed, but it still leaves `largeExact1e8Qualification: not-established`. The core is byte-identical and the large timing win is already known. Choose this instead only if actual large delivered-word operation becomes the immediate product question. |
| **Existing-cap reference diagnostic** | Current prepared-tree output agreement and measured capacity on two N=180 workloads with contrasting positive work | Directly tests the presently unmeasured obstacle to strict accuracy evidence. No cap increase, new scene search, or full oracle is required. |

This choice is not a claim that 180 is representative of 5,000. The dense180
baseline has 3,056 positive rotor terms versus 228 in the fresh-a64 control,
whereas sparse180 has none. It exposes a substantial positive/root workload and
a zero-heavy counterpart. Both 180 scenes are axis-aligned regular grids; they
do not represent tilted, guarded-fallback or adversarial large states. The
limitation is preferable to pretending that another small replay answers scale.

## Minimal instrumentation and prospective bounds

Prefer the standard library `cProfile` around the **one actual prepared scene
call**; retain its complete function table (`ncalls`, internal time, cumulative
time, file/line/name) and readable top tables sorted separately by internal and
cumulative time. Do not edit the scalar arithmetic, tree proofs or root routines
to add bespoke timing branches. Relevant existing function names expose
preparation/validation and operand binding, tree construction and `zero_rule`,
`_oracle_suffix`, Fraction operations, `reference_roots`, `bisect_cubic`,
`source_box` and `load_boxes`. A profiler entry absent because a branch was never
used is informative; do not force it to execute.

Record non-overlapping outer wall stages for raw loading/pin verification,
profiled scene computation, wire construction/serialization, saved comparison,
and writing/hashing; include total elapsed and all retained bytes. Cumulative
profile times overlap and must never be summed. Profiled clocks include profiler
overhead, so they cannot be compared with the earlier unprofiled 3.7478 s as a
speed estimate or used as production latency. If a phase cannot be isolated
without changing science, label it combined rather than manufacture attribution.

Retain existing work and reuse counts, non-point versus point root counts from
the saved output, positive term count, compact terminal count and JSON bytes.
Record `resource.getrusage` peak RSS with platform units and scope: process
lifetime, including loaded baselines/profiler data. A shared process peak cannot
be attributed to the current scene by subtraction. This is diagnostic RSS,
not an OS-enforced memory limit or a measured live-graph size.

Proposed later reservation: three first-use calls, zero warmups/repetitions,
at most 50,000 attempted suffix entries in total; 60 seconds per scene and
180 seconds aggregate science time; 60 seconds for final saved checking/closure;
240 seconds combined. Allow at most 64 MiB per scientific file and 192 MiB total
new output, including profiles and partial/anomaly evidence. The 50,000 suffix
limit is a finite guard above the fixed expected45,455, not permission to accept
a changed count. Sampled/reported RSS above 1 GiB is a stop condition at the next
poll, not a promise that memory can never exceed it. If the runner cannot enforce
a claimed wall/output guard, the contract is not ready. All numbers are proposed
administrative limits, not extrapolated runtime predictions.

Freeze source, runtime, inputs, old result/checker pins, projection and guards
before any run. A later allocation must leave full closure time inside its
authorized window. No timeout retry, larger reservation, state replacement,
fallback to approximate endpoints, profiler-off rescue, cap increase or partial
success claim is allowed within this milestone. Save the first failure and any
completed evidence. A failed dense case does not license replacing it with the
sparse case. No native/browser/full oracle or new scene generation belongs here.

## Falsifiable outcome and stopping decision

| Outcome | Required disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Any pin/schema/word/endpoint/coverage/count mismatch | Fail correctness/contract readiness. Diagnose the bounded disagreement from retained evidence; do not discuss a speed benefit or run larger states. |
| Correct completed outputs but a wall/output/RSS guard fails, or a record is partial | Report bounded capacity failure. Stop reference escalation and the automatic optimization chain. Any different architecture or narrower claim requires a new explicit decision. |
| All three exact gates and administrative bounds pass | Report **finite prepared-reference capacity at N≤180**, with attributed work and resource observations. This permits consideration of a separately reviewed scale contract, not a larger run. No cache/filter implementation follows automatically. |

Even a pass does not identify a universally dominant cost from three workloads.
If profiling points to binding, suffix, proof, roots or serialization, report that
observation without converting it into an automatic next optimization. A future
larger-scale proposal must explain how complete omission proofs, positive work,
root work, output storage and worst-case fallback will be bounded on its actual
states. At 5,000 the logical rotor/source workload is 99,980,000 and there are
20,000 roots; a simple N/180 scaling of this diagnostic is invalid. Native
positive counts (96,244/95,931 in the Chrome cases) also do not bound exact-tree
residual work. The present strict reference cap remains 180 after every outcome.

## Physical accuracy is a separate unresolved question

Exact arithmetic establishes fidelity to the stated model, not fidelity to
aerodynamics. The bounded physical review found no presently admitted calibrated
interaction benchmark; Kiran's direct load experiment remains the strongest
conditional quantitative target, with access/conventions/calibration unresolved.
That is a limitation of the inspected evidence, not proof that usable data do not
exist or that all useful physical conclusions require downloadable raw files.

Retained published measured results can support a narrowly matched qualitative
challenge. A useful candidate is: **does the current ideal-power receiver law's
directional thrust response under downwash agree with the reported response at
the same aligned/offset geometry and actuation convention?** The retained Kiran
methods already establish tethered4 V/hoverPWM rather than fixedRPM; Gielis uses
powered-hover tare and measures incremental aggregate loads. Those differences
must be resolved in the question before a force-sign disagreement is attributed
to the model. Published trends/figures can falsify a matched sign or qualitative
assumption without a calibrated score; they cannot silently become per-case
labels, signed PIV force pairs or a quantitative uncertainty bound.

No new qualitative result is claimed here, and no figure extraction or scoring
was performed. A retained-source compatibility note could later advance the
physics without firmware reconstruction. It must not be represented as physical
validation supplied by this numerical capacity milestone. If the project's next
decision becomes physical adequacy rather than strict computational evidence,
that question deserves priority over a further numerical optimization.
