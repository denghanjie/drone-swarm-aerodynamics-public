# Minimal prospective normalizer-reuse experiment

Source-only review, 2026-10-16. No module was imported, numerical expression evaluated, trajectory generated, control executed or benchmark launched. The old allocations remain closed. This design supports a separately reviewed successor; it is not execution authorization or a finding of speed benefit.

## Decision and narrow estimand

Use two arms of the complete `filtered` method: A is the unchanged uncached implementation; B differs only by lazy within-invocation reuse of successful source beta and receiver/rotor nu intervals. Compare A and B on identical raw binary64 state words. This is the smallest ablation of the combined change. It cannot assign separate timing benefits to beta and nu, or establish that either was a dominant cost.

The draft `createQuery` factory and two method call-site substitutions are consistent with that narrow candidate, conditional on the proof obligations below. Leave adapter preparation, center/rotor geometry, filter, tree, pair order, coefficient arithmetic, load proposal, solver, certificate and tolerances unchanged. In particular, do not extend this experiment into reusing terms in `loads.cjs`.

## Fixed fresh workload and denominator

Proposed successor schedule: seeds `26171011` and `26171012`, 64 drones each, 10 s, dt 0.0025 s, wide factory, Boids, grid search, wall-priority filter, ideal observations and airflow off. Capture all 17 raw state components at 0, 5 and 10 s; optionally retain the unchanged 0.2 s pose recording for provenance. Before freeze, verify that the two proposed seeds were not used in a prior scientific allocation. If they were, choose replacements before any numerical work and record the reason. After freeze there is no reseeding or state substitution.

Generate each flight exactly once; both arms consume the same copied immutable capture, input digest, stable IDs and row order. Keep six scheduled states in the denominator even when a flight stops or an arm fails. No attitude flattening, force replacement, geometry tuning or aerodynamic feedback. Reuse the prior nontriviality requirements: positive edges at two times per run, postinitial horizontal-axis squared norm above 1e-6, each relative coordinate changing by more than 0.001 m versus drone 0, and a changed resolved positive-edge set. Missing witnesses make the fixed workload uninformative; they do not authorize replacement.

Call each arm once on each of the four already fixed small controls (positive, distant-zero, raw-force refusal and ordering refusal). For each reached scheduled state, run exactly four paired repetitions: eight complete-method calls per state, 48 scheduled calls and at most 56 primary complete-method calls including the eight fixed-control calls. Within each state use AB, BA, AB, BA; reverse that sequence to BA, AB, BA, AB at run-a t5 and run-b t0/t10. Each state therefore has two AB and two BA pairs. Do not choose order from observed cost. No warmups, pilots, extra repetitions or adaptive stopping for a favorable result. The four implementation controls are descriptive fixtures, not extra moving-state samples.

## Source proof obligations

| Obligation | Required argument before freeze |
| --- | --- |
| Dependencies and key | Beta is exactly `sqrt(toInterval(sum(source.forces)/mg))`, keyed by the invocation-owned source exact object. Nu is exactly `sqrt(toInterval(receiver.forces[r]/((2*rho)*A)))`, keyed by the invocation-owned receiver exact object and integer rotor r. No ID-only, receiver-only, source/receiver-swapped or cross-rotor key. Constants are the same pinned objects throughout the call. |
| Lifetime | A fresh closure is created after `onPhase('spatial')` within every method invocation. Neither map nor closure escapes in the returned record, lives at module scope or crosses calls. Object identities are newly constructed from that invocation's admitted raw words. |
| Laziness and priority | Geometry and K remain first; beta remains before nu; both remain before scaled-K/cap and nu-normalizer checks. Do not eagerly prepare beta/nu, move the cap check ahead of them, or infer that a later-refused pair need not perform its earlier arithmetic. |
| Successful values only | Store only after the original expression returns successfully. A successful frozen interval can be reused even if a subsequent cap/normalizer check refuses that pair. An interval with nu.l equal to zero is still a successful computation; the original later normalizer refusal must remain. |
| Exceptions | `I.Unqualified` must retain its original code and unresolved handling; other errors must propagate. Failures are not cached, replaced by zero, converted to misses with a fallback value, or broadened into an unresolved catch. A successful beta may remain cached after nu fails. |
| Immutability and non-interference | R/I operations read their operands; I intervals are frozen. Exact forces and constants never change during assembly. The synchronous runner's callbacks cannot mutate input or dependencies, and inputs are copied/frozen before a call. No runtime preload or monkeypatch can alter helpers. |
| Domain | This proof covers the closed admitted method path. The exported `createQuery` is not safe for callers who mutate prepared objects while retaining its closure; do not claim such an API contract. |

The unchanged method still evaluates every logical nonself rotor/source pair through its complete partition. For successful computations the candidate has at most N distinct beta entries and 4N nu entries per invocation. This is a bound on successful computation reuse, not a bound on all work when failures repeat, an observed count or a measured bottleneck.

## Exact equivalence and independent evidence

Save both full canonical method records. Require byte-identical scientific records after only a fixed, explicit envelope exclusion for arm/source identity, timings and process metadata. The method record itself should need no projection: `name`, preparation, all hierarchy/scan terminals, filter-zero lists, legacy work counters, statuses, refusal witnesses, candidate binary64 words, all rational/interval endpoints, load boxes, errors and certificate fields must match exactly. Do not weaken this to overlap, matching final forces or 1e-8 closeness. Preserve differing records and the first canonical field difference on failure.

Independently validate each arm's raw preparation, complete pair partition and semantics, zero claims, positive coefficients, graph/order/refusal witnesses and every certificate. Equality alone permits a common-mode error. Retain the four separate normalized bounds—rotor, collective, world force and world moment—at the unchanged 1e-8 thresholds. A known model refusal can have valid record integrity; it still fails all-six certification. Reference-undecidable, checker error, missing state and model refusal remain distinct.

Retain the qualified 21 targeted corruption recipes, including the corrected semantic false-zero recipe and its separate counter-interception regression, against each arm's saved evidence: 42 bounded saved-record checks. Independent pristine copies, exact fixed witnesses and registered failure families are mandatory. The old 20-control contract is historical, not the successor's control list. No generic digest failure may substitute for a registered semantic rejection.

## Minimal cache-specific controls

Use a separately frozen small semantic harness and one same-worker method sequence. These run only in the future successor's allocated kernel and cannot be invoked now. Their literal inputs, complete call order, expected fields/failure families and branch witnesses must be public before freeze. A control that never reaches its intended cache access fails; no generated-state witness search is allowed.

| Control | Required observable distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Source key | In one closure query the same receiver/rotor with two distinct source objects whose force sums differ, then the first again. Compare the complete pair result including beta and coefficient with uncached calls. Equal-thrust sources are insufficient to expose a bad key. |
| Receiver and rotor key | Use distinct receivers with unequal rotor forces, and two rotors of one receiver with unequal forces; return to the earlier receiver/rotor. Compare nu and every returned interval. This must detect receiver/source confusion and omission of the rotor index. |
| State lifetime | In one persistent process call complete methods A-state, B-state, A-state, separately for each arm (six extra complete-method calls). Reuse stable IDs, change forces and reorder rows in B. Compare each call with its uncached counterpart and its own raw-input digest; the second A must match the first. This is needed because isolated timing workers would conceal an accidentally persistent cache. |
| Early exits | Use fixed zero/refusal paths with normalizer accesses arranged to raise a sentinel if reached. They must exit at the original earlier branch without evaluating beta/nu. Also preserve global raw-force refusal before cache creation. |
| Qualified exception and retry | Force an `I.Unqualified` at beta and at nu separately, twice at the same key; require the same unresolved code on both attempts and no failure cache. Include successful beta followed by failing nu, and successful calls after failures. |
| Unexpected exception | A fixture-only accessor/dependency sentinel raises an ordinary Error at beta or nu. Both arms must propagate the same error class and message on each attempt; do not compare source-line-dependent stack text. Such probes are synthetic harness controls, not admitted flight states. |
| Arithmetic priority | Freeze witnesses showing K failure before normalizer access, beta failure before nu, and beta/nu evaluation before later cap/normalizer refusal. Include a successfully computed nu interval with zero lower endpoint if representable by the fixed fixture. Missing a necessary witness blocks freeze, rather than silently dropping the case. |

Budget the direct semantic harness at exactly 32 prescribed query attempts per arm (64 total), with the final ordered roster accounting for every attempt before freeze; no reserve may be adaptively spent. Limit the lifetime sequence to the six calls above, making the total complete-method cap 62. For success-only/retry assertions, fixture-owned access counters may observe their own getter calls; do not instrument or monkeypatch timed scientific helpers. All test-created prepared objects and exception probes are explicitly labeled synthetic.

Add six independently targeted saved-transcript corruption checks: wrong source-beta association, wrong receiver-nu association, wrong rotor-nu association, wrong invocation/state binding, swallowed qualified exception, and swallowed unexpected exception. Register exact families and keep all unrelated fields/counters consistent so the intended validator is reached. Combined with the inherited checks this is at most 48 saved-evidence mutations. The semantic fixture executions exercise the implementation; transcript corruptions separately exercise the checker. Neither substitutes for the other.

## Complete cost and garbage collection

For the primary moving-state comparison, use a fresh, otherwise identical Node child for each arm/state, one complete method invocation per child, and wait for its confirmed exit before starting the paired child. Both children read the identical canonical input file. Keep all validation/reviewer work out of this measurement window. Use a fixed runtime/executable/environment, no warm cache carried across invocations, and no forced GC or differing runtime flags.

Primary cost is monotonic wall time from child spawn through exit, plus group CPU and peak RSS for the whole child lifetime. It includes process/module startup, raw ingestion, admission, exact preparation, tree/filter work, closure/WeakMap creation, lookups, successful misses, unchanged assembly/solver/certificate, output serialization and flush/close, all GC that occurs, and process teardown. Record supervisor-observed bytes and exit status. Startup and output costs are charged equally, not subtracted by an estimated baseline. Generation and independent-checker costs are separately reported for the complete experiment.

Also record method entry-to-return and phase times as secondary descriptive values. These contain cache allocation but can miss deferred GC, so they cannot alone support an end-to-end benefit claim. Fresh-worker exit releases all remaining cache/process memory; this gives a complete isolated-worker cost model, not a persistent-process GC benchmark. If the intended claim is persistent browser/server reuse, a new prospectively bounded repeated-call/cleanup protocol is required. Do not silently use this experiment to make that claim.

Report all 24 paired wall/CPU/RSS observations, each paired B/A wall ratio, each state's ratio of four summed B times to four summed A times, and the overall ratio of summed complete wall times when all 24 pairs complete. Require all repeated scientific records for a state to be identical within and across arms. Never discard a slow, refused or failed arm, pool only favorable frames, subtract an empty call, or stop when a ratio looks favorable. The four fixed repetitions characterize order/noise on these states; they do not create 24 independent flight samples. Avoid confidence intervals treating repeated frames as independent random samples, general stable-speedup claims and beta-versus-nu attribution. A performance regression is publishable evidence and does not invalidate semantic equivalence.

## Bounded successor and decision gates

Proposed hard caps: one control/generation/paired-query kernel, 600 s wall, guardian 610 s; one independent saved-evidence checker, 600 s, guardian 610 s. Each process group is limited to 1 GiB sampled RSS. Each timed arm also has a 60 s subdeadline within the shared kernel budget. At most two generation calls, 62 complete-method calls, 64 direct semantic attempts and 48 saved-transcript mutations; no retry. Scientific output 256 MiB, including at most 192 MiB kernel and 64 MiB checker; checker report 2 MiB, each scientific record 32 MiB, each generation record 8 MiB, metadata/logs 16 MiB and combined 272 MiB. These are fail-closed limits, not a claim of fit. Missing output or a breached cap remains in the result; no increase or second attempt after observing it.
