What the comparison means

F — direct scan: checks each pair using a cheap screen before doing detailed airflow work.
H — tree shortcut: organizes drones into groups and skips distant groups.
HC — tree plus records: also creates detailed records of the groups it skips.
H0 — tree without skipping: pays for the grouping machinery while visiting every group.

Why it lost: both methods still performed 599,336 detailed rotor checks. The tree skipped cheap preliminary checks but added the work of finding and sorting candidate drones. Detailed records added only about 6% to the paired query cost.

This test measures one saved state in one browser process. Agreement with the earlier calculation does not prove that the airflow model matches real drones. No sustained flight, 5,000-drone result, physical validation or new scientific discovery is claimed.
