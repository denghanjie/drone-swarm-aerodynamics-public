Reported elapsed times, RSS samples, maximum sample gaps, process groups, output/journal sizes and hashes match. The prose correctly treats timing as supervision evidence, without comparative speed or throughput claims.

**Minor display issue:** `group-scene.js` initializes `fraction=1` when no certificate load exists, then assigns its full-thrust color to a non-rejected preparation. The unconditional note says color represents predicted thrust reduction. For refused/unresolved scenes this supplies a color with no corresponding saved load estimate, although the table correctly says no qualified load certificate. Use a neutral unavailable color when `!load`, preserve red for preparation rejection, and make the legend conditional. This does not alter any scientific result or numerical claim.
