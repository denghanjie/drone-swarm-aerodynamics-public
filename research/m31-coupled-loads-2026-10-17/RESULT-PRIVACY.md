# Result privacy review

Public results contain synthetic scene inputs, prepared geometry, numerical intervals/rationals, source/runtime hashes, sampled process supervision, independent controls, research reviews and screenshots of the local research viewer. They contain no user chats, attachment contents, credentials or external paper assets. Raw process logs remain privately retained; published logs redact machine/runtime path prefixes. Both stages emitted short structured output and empty stderr.

Before committing, inspect every new text path for personal filesystem paths and credential markers, inspect screenshots visually, and verify exact result/source pins. Public checkpoint publication was authorized by the user. The final publication receipt separately binds the committed manifest and independently downloaded exact-commit public manifest.
