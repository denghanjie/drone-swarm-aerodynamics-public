# Complete groups under M-current

This package extends the published pair checkpoint to complete small groups. It freezes the same current-state adapter, constants, guards, coefficient law and exact-dyadic interpretation of prepared operands. The four copied candidate modules are unchanged. It neither reconstructs ideal rotations nor claims equivalence to legacy native arithmetic.

Each source amplitude is y_i=sqrt(sum_r w_ir*x_ir), where w_ir=f_ir/sum_r f_ir is exact and positive. For every admitted receiver rotor, x_ir solves x_ir^3+x_ir*sum_j a_irj*y_j=1. All nonself pairs are enumerated; no approximation to membership is allocated. Pair support/admission decisions precede solving and are preserved even when a different pair causes refusal. Cap guards hold over the full 0<y<=1 source cube under the inherited nonnegative coefficient model.

The operational subdomain requires each positive edge source to precede its receiver in descending prepared center z, then ascending stable ID. This allows an independent topological reference. Cyclic interactions are outside this implementation allocation although the inherited contraction reasoning is more general.

## Conditional certificate

Use the adopted arbitrary-root defect corollary in ../m31-root-defect-planning-2026-10-16/ADOPTED-DERIVATION.md. The native candidate is only a proposal. Enclose exact candidate-derived source amplitudes and each complete defect p=xhat^3+xhat*sum_j a*yhat. For [L,U] enclosing p with L>0, choose kbar>=max(1,U,1/L) and Kbar=max kbar. Directed arithmetic returns xhat/(kbar*Kbar)<=xstar<=min(1,xhat*kbar*Kbar). The fixed96 lattice encloses real values; it is not a replacement model for the coefficient law. Independent fixed192 reference coefficients enclose that same law.

## Supplied-geometry load map

Let b_i be the prepared axis and ell_ir the separately supplied prepared offset. Rotor thrust is t_ir=f_ir*x_ir, collective T_i=sum_r t_ir, world force F_i=b_i*T_i, and world moment M_i=sum_r (ell_ir cross b_i)*t_ir. These are exact real linear operations on the prepared dyadic operands. All three world coordinates are retained; no exactly-unit attitude or zero world-z moment is assumed. This is a signed linear extension of the reviewed body-load bound, not a new physical yaw model. The lever is not inferred by subtracting rounded world points.

The certificate encloses the full signed map of its returned root box and the independent reference. Exact rational candidate loads are stored alongside it. Normalized error bounds cover the entire returned box relative to those candidate loads. Rotor normalization is mg/4; collective/world force mg; world moment mg*R. World errors use three-coordinate Euclidean norms. The tolerance is exactly1/100000000 in all four categories.

## Limits

The nineteen static scenes (maximum16 drones) are a bridge toward the original5,000-drone Boids/dynamics/mutual-aerodynamics comparison, not its completion.
