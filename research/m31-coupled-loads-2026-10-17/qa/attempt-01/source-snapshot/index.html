What this checkpoint asks

Checking one interaction is not enough. Every source must be included, and a change in one drone's load must feed into the others. The independent checker solves the same declared model in a different way and tests whether the returned bounds contain it.

What remains open

The full goal still includes 5,000 drones, local 3D Boids, flight dynamics, mutual aerodynamics, total computation cost and evidence of physical accuracy. These small static groups do not establish those claims or a novel result.
