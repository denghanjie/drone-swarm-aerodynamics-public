# Why this bounded computational question remains

This checkpoint asks whether storing conservative source-ID lists across a short moving-input stream reduces complete current load-query cost relative to frozen F, while preserving exact scientific outputs. The adopted workload and counts are solely those in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md): eight prescribed N=1,000 snapshots, one warmup pair, eight measured pairs, 144 stream queries and 24 control queries, at most 168 native calls. There is no N=64 replay bridge in this exploratory screen. An earlier desk proposal for a longer stream was not adopted.

The assessment below read saved narrative, protocol, implementation and restart records; it did not rerun their scientific work. Some historical reads were partial, so “not found” means absent from the inspected records, not proven absent from every repository file. The separate [literature check](LITERATURE.md) establishes familiar antecedents and is not an exhaustive novelty search.

## Retain the current F baseline

The [18 October cost decision](../m31-cost-decision-2026-10-18/SUMMARY.md) compared F with the unchanged H center-tree method on one saved N=1,000 state. F's median was 36.85 ms and H's 85.15 ms. Median paired H/F was 2.282222, with 0/24 strict wins. All 116 complete scientific semantic outputs agreed exactly. The decision was to retain F and stop this unchanged H implementation for that workload.

H reduced candidate visits from 999,000 to 627,164, but both arms retained the same 487,242 center-distance tests, 599,336 pairCalls and 9,012 positive rotor terms. Build/admission medians were 27.10 ms for F and 75.90 ms for H. One diagnostic H gathering measurement was 49.70 ms and combined traversal, collection and sorting; it cannot identify sorting alone as the cause. Logging was also not the principal explanation. See the saved [protocol](../m31-cost-decision-2026-10-18/PROTOCOL.md), [claims](../m31-cost-decision-2026-10-18/CLAIMS.md) and [restart](../m31-cost-decision-2026-10-18/RESTART-RESULT.md).

The baseline entry point remains `research/m31-native-fresh-transfer-2026-10-17/source/native-load.cjs`, called as `query(input, 'F', guard)`, with `source/vendor/kernel.cjs` and its retained dependency closure. The [tree implementation record](../m31-native-tree-design-2026-10-17/IMPLEMENTATION.md) documents the enumeration seam, canonical source ordering, F's current whole-scene eligibility rule and fallback behavior. These locations were identified from saved documentation; the current checkpoint's source review and manifest must verify the actual bytes.

## What “reuse” previously meant

The [saved reuse inventory](../m31-reuse-literature-2026-10-16/HISTORY-INVENTORY.md), [clarification](../m31-reuse-literature-2026-10-16/HISTORY-CLARIFICATION.md) and [parent decision](../m31-reuse-literature-2026-10-16/PARENT-DECISION.md) distinguish several earlier mechanisms:

- DOT's exact-efficiency `reuse` arm refreshed typed geometry and scratch within each query. It did not retain a conservative neighbor list across changing query inputs.
- Earlier fixed-hover cadence reduction, held inflow and predictor experiments reused field values. Their restricted motion/strength settings do not certify the present current-state calculation.
- Joint solved-root hold failed all 24 approximation/event cells; the finest sampled position difference was 36.861 mm against a 1 mm screen. The later [root-secant result](../m31-root-secant-2026-10-16/POSTRUN-RESTART.md) failed all three normalized screens and produced four roots above 1.
- A [root-defect analytical decision](../m31-root-defect-planning-2026-10-16/PARENT-DECISION.md) was conditional and did not remove the need to construct current coefficients.

The present candidate reuses IDs alone. Current geometry, attitudes, nominal forces, coefficients, admission, roots, residuals and loads must still be evaluated. The eight-stage stream deliberately changes positions and nominal forces. This is a distinct computational question from holding or predicting any solved quantity, with zero permitted semantic difference from F.

## Spatial construction and sorting work already attempted

The [existing-work audit](../m31-zero-screen-2026-10-16/EXISTING-WORK-AUDIT.md), [builder design](../m31-post-cost-decision-2026-10-16/BUILDER-DESIGN.md) and [neighbor plan](../m31-coupled-cost-planning-2026-10-16/EXACT-NEIGHBOR-PLAN.md) describe fresh spatial construction and sorted candidate gathering. Cold grids are therefore established project work. A bounded-way merge of already sorted cell buckets appeared as a possible later alternative in the inspected narrative; an executed incremental stable-bin experiment was not found. This checkpoint does not add stable bins.

The [typed-assembly result](../m31-typed-assembly-2026-10-16/POSTRUN-RESTART.md) already tested stable typed rows/CSR and removal of dependency Set/sort. It failed its T/L <= 0.90 gate at N=5,000, with 3/6 wins and ratios near 1. The present candidate must not be described as the first attempt to remove sorting, nor as another chunk-size, inlining or allocation variant of that failed comparison. It instead amortizes source-list construction across changing inputs and stores rows in canonical order.

Related [browser-screen results](../m31-browser-screen-2026-10-16/POSTRUN-RESTART.md) and [current-construction planning](../m31-current-construction-planning-2026-10-16/POSTRUN-RESTART.md) already tested exact screening and normalizer reuse. Normalizer reuse preserved outputs but passed none of six speed gates. The [filter-cost result](../m31-filter-cost-2026-10-17/RESULT-DECISION.md) reduced exact scalar work in a different reference pipeline; it is not a speed estimate for current native F.

## Approximate positive omission is also closed here

The [oracle-omission milestone](../m31-oracle-omission-renewal-2026-10-17/MILESTONE-SUMMARY.md) found less than 10% optimistic savings for the fixed tau/16 positive-omission rule in all six states. The [direct-load diagnostic](../m31-direct-load-diagnostic-2026-10-17/MILESTONE-SUMMARY.md) found startup rotor errors about 6.17% and 6.59% when deleting the weakest tenth. This checkpoint does not revive either omission rule or discard a nonzero contribution for an approximate speed gain.

## Scope of the new screen

The exact N=1,000 saved input derives from the [single-state native-tree checkpoint](../m31-native-tree-state1000-2026-10-17/RESTART-RESULT.md), whose completed computation was one projected state and one derivative, without integration. The new eight snapshots are synthetic perturbations of its saved words, not measured flight or an evolved trajectory. The earlier [32-stage coupled qualification](../m31-coupled-stage-qualification-2026-10-17/RESTART.md) remains historical context and is not being replayed under this allocation.

Temporal conservative list caching was not found as an executed comparison in the inspected histories. This justifies one bounded engineering screen, not a new-method claim. Its possible benefit is avoided repeated enumeration/list construction; detailed retained interaction work remains. Cold builds, rebuilds, containment checks, storage and row consumption must be counted. Failure to achieve the protocol's exactness and cost gates closes this fixed implementation/workload screen without tuning after results. All prior sources, results and expired allocations remain unchanged.
