Can we reuse the shortlist?

Each drone keeps a list of drones that might affect its airflow. When the group moves a little, we try to keep that list. Every airflow calculation is still done again using the new positions and motor forces.

Checking saved evidence…

These are eight prescribed test states, not a flight simulation. The dots mark 1,000 drone centers. Changing the state shows the exact saved test positions. This test measures computer cost and numerical agreement; it cannot establish real-world airflow accuracy or safe flight.
