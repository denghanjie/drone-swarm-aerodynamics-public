## Scope of the final conclusion

The benchmark can finish with retained navigation failures and explicitly failed numerical criteria. It cannot finish by treating missing trials as passes, error-normalized airflow as corrected evidence, replay frame rate as simulation speed, or unvalidated loads as accurate physics. A completed benchmark goal would still leave empirical validation, general safe navigation and a defensible scientific novelty claim open in the wider research programme.
