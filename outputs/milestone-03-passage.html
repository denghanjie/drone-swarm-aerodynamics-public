Assumptions, reproducibility and numerical checks

These are deterministic CPU calculations displayed through Three.js/WebGL. Each vehicle retains the earlier 6-DOF mechanics, four motor thrust states, gravity, attitude control and motor limits. A local velocity rule aims toward five contracting lanes, repels nearby neighbors and slows behind traffic. It is neither an optimized planner nor a guarantee of collision avoidance.

The experiment stops at the first 15 cm center-to-center contact envelope or vehicle model limit. No impact dynamics are modeled. The requested separation is 20 cm. Unique valid crossings use a body-clearance margin at the gate; crossings outside it are recorded as failures. The planned horizon is 60 s, including stopped runs in the denominator. Exact crossing times use interpolation, and pair separation uses swept line segments between integration steps.

Random seeds vary initial lateral and altitude positions by up to 5 mm. There is no wind, sensing error, communication delay or aerodynamic interaction. Numerical refinement covers the three displayed examples, not every trajectory in the parameter study. Physical accuracy and real-flight safety remain unvalidated.
