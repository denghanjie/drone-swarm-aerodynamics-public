What this experiment tests

Do alignment and cohesion help a three-dimensional group navigate, beyond separation and shared map guidance alone? Inspect the approach, obstacle encounter and final outcome. Counts stop increasing when a contact threshold is reached; a stopped run is a failure, not successful passage.

The evaluation and numerical checks are preserved in the milestone summary.

Scope and evidence

Replay seed501. Eighteen matched cases: three scenarios × two controllers × three fresh seeds. Same physics and initial states. The sphere has radius1 m; both controllers receive an explicit preference to pass above it. This route preference is programmed, not emergent planning. The aperture wall is infinite and zero-thickness in the contact calculation; the displayed wall is a finite visual patch.

A sphere of radius7.5 cm around each drone defines contact. Swept segment checks detect obstacle contact between time steps. The run stops on contact; impact mechanics are not modeled. Three.js renders recorded states. CPU steering; airflow and sensor errors are off. This milestone does not establish physical safety or novelty.
