What this shows

In the two-drone view, slide the lower drone sideways. Its predicted inflow drops as it leaves the upper drone’s wake. The lift estimate assumes constant ideal induced power; a real motor and controller may respond differently.

In the flock view, each drone still follows local separation, alignment and cohesion rules plus common route guidance. Changing the airflow model changes the simulated flight. The four recordings use the same 50-drone starting flock and factory.
