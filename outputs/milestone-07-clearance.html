What these tests mean

120 runs: two controllers × three widths × two error profiles × ten new seeds (101–110). Each uses 50 drones, 0.6 m initial spacing, a 0.5 m/s requested speed cap and 60 seconds. The candidate was frozen before testing these seeds. The replays show seed 101. Frames and all safety measurements use true states; the scheduler alone receives the imperfect observations.

Extra clearance is a bundled intervention: gate center span width−0.35 m; minimum nominal lane spacing 0.42 m; cautious staging; position gain 0.8; release after the previous group is beyond x=0.65 m. This study does not isolate each change’s individual contribution or prove optimality. Navigation bias is fixed within ±2 or ±5 cm per axis, with 0.10 or 0.25 s delay. Low-level stabilization and initial map remain exact; airflow is off.

The gate remains virtual, no wall impacts are resolved. A sub-15 cm separation stops the run, and sub-20 cm is a spacing breach. No mathematical or real-flight safety guarantee is claimed.
