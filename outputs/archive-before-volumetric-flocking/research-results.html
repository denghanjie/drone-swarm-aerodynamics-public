Study design and limitations

45 matched cases: three controllers × three widths × five holdout seeds (201–205), 50 drones each, 60 seconds, ideal observations, 0.01 s physics steps. Replays show seed 201. Parameters were inspected in seed-1 pilots, then frozen. All failures are retained.

Neighbors are within 1.25 m; CPU discovery scans all pairs. The purple drone is an example, not a leader. Links show its current neighbors. The gate is a virtual measurement plane: no wall impact response. Runs stop below the assumed 15 cm contact distance; distances below 20 cm are spacing breaches. No physical safety guarantee is established.
