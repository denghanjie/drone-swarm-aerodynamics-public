What these tests mean

150 cases: two schedulers × three widths × five observation profiles × five seeds. Initial spacing is 0.6 m; requested speed is 0.5 m/s; the horizon is 60 seconds. Each pair uses identical initial conditions and errors. The seed-1 replays display true positions, not the scheduler’s estimates.

Bias means a fixed offset per drone, independently sampled in each horizontal axis within ±2 cm or ±5 cm. Delay applies to navigation state only, sampled every 10 ms. Until sufficient history exists, the initial state is held. The scheduler knows the initial layout exactly. Low-level stabilization still receives perfect current state; this is not a test of delayed motor commands or end-to-end communications. Values are illustrative, not calibrated sensor specifications.

Ground-truth states drive passage, separation and stop measurements. A 15 cm center separation triggers a contact-envelope stop; 20 cm is the desired separation. Invalid crossings are retained. The gate is a virtual measurement plane without wall collisions. Airflow is off. Navigation is near one altitude with full 3D rigid-body motion; Three.js renders CPU-calculated records.
